The Roadmap

Uslu dur! is a total overhaul built on Bohemia's opened engine source, which itself is a frozen snapshot: no official updates are coming from Bohemia's side, so everything from here on is ours. We don't build features in whatever order sounds fun; we prove the riskiest assumption first, ship the smallest playable slice, and playtest it for real before adding the next layer. Below is where that stands right now for Guerrilla Mode.

★ Guerrilla Mode Roadmap

Gate-Zero: Prove It Actually Works In Progress

60%

Before authoring more content, we're confirming the basics hold up under real play: framerate with a full garrison on screen, enemies spawning in cleanly, and saves that survive a reload.

Phase 1: "Three Zones" Built

100%

Camp, Village, and Outpost to capture, garrisons that defend them, an alert level that rises and falls, a resource economy, recruiting, loot, and a companion who can die for good. Built, waiting on its first live playtest.

Phase 1.5: Swappable Factions & Islands Built

100%

Done, and proven. Everything specific to "this island, this enemy" now lives in swappable data instead of hardcoded scripts, and the simulation core moved into native engine systems (zones, garrisons, the alert machine). The very same campaign code now boots a whole second island, Sinai, with the factions flipped, and automated end-to-end tests now drive the new-game screen and confirm the swap in a live mission. Like the "Three Zones" slice, it's code-complete and waiting only on its first human playtest.

Phase 2: Vertical Slice Planned

15%

Deepen the economy and progression into something you'd actually sink hours into, and give the occupier its first real teeth: town closures and reprisal raids that hit your support economy, the helicopter-borne Fireforce reaction, and the AI-fidelity work (kill-the-officer routs, suppression, flee-to-cover) that makes a firefight feel like a fight.

Phase 3: Full Mode Planned

5%

The whole island, liberated zone by zone, against an occupier running its full playbook, Protected Villages, pseudo-gangs, tracker teams, the prison-and-rescue loop, informer networks, plus a proper strategy screen for running your insurgency from the map. This is where the vision on the Gameplay Systems page lands in full.

FIELD NOTE

Plans here are living and get revised as we learn more, this page changes right along with them. Most recently (2026-07-01), swappable factions and islands got promoted from "someday" to a launch requirement; that work has since landed and now boots a second island (Sinai) with the factions swapped.

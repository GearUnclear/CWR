About Uslu dur!

What Is It, Exactly?

Uslu dur! is a total overhaul of ARMA: Cold War Assault, the title you first met in 2001 as Operation Flashpoint: Cold War Crisis. It's a ground-up rework of how the enemy AI fights, spots you, and reacts to your squad, culminating in Guerrilla Mode: a brand-new, persistent open-world insurgency that doesn't exist in the original game at all. That's what this portal is about.

None of it happens in a vacuum: it's built on Bohemia Interactive's own newly-opened engine source, internal codename "Poseidon."

QUOTE

"Bohemia Interactive is releasing it to the community that has kept this game alive for more than two decades — to study it, build on it, fix it, and create from it."
Source: README.md

We took that invitation literally. On its own, the opened engine is just a faithfully-ported version of the original game, now running natively on modern Windows and Linux. The overhaul is everything we're adding on top: AI that actually fights like a squad instead of a shooting gallery, and the whole Guerrilla Mode campaign layered over it. See Gameplay Systems for what that means when you're actually playing.

A Note On Names

"Uslu dur!" is this project's own name. You may also see this game called "Cold War Assault Remastered" or "CWR" — that's Bohemia's own name for the engine snapshot it opened up, which is frozen and not where any of this overhaul happens. There's also an unrelated, much older community mod that used the name "Cold War Rearmed" for years. Uslu dur! is neither of those: it's this overhaul, built on top of Bohemia's release. If you'd rather follow the underlying engine itself, a separate community continuation lives at ofpisnotdead-com/CWR-CE.

The Legal Fine Print

Quick version: the engine source is open (GPL-3.0-or-later), but the "ARMA" and "Operation Flashpoint" names stay Bohemia's, and the game's models/textures/sounds are a separate download under their own license (APL-SA) — which is also why this site's art is our own retro red-star icons rather than anything pulled from the game itself. A free Demo is on Steam under app id 4819000. Full license details are on the Downloads page for anyone who needs them.

Setting & Theme

The source tree is engine and tooling code with no fixed campaign narrative; that narrative lives in the separately licensed game-data package. The Cold War setting surfaces through Guerrilla Mode's own design:

  • The original title's 1985 Cold-War-gone-hot fiction, replayed as an open-ended insurgency rather than a fixed campaign.
  • Faction shorthand from the community design docs: GUER (Resistance) and CIV (civilians) against an occupying EAST or WEST faction. Full CWA game data ships rosters for all four sides.
  • Swappable factions & islands are a launch requirement for Guerrilla Mode: a new game begins by choosing the island plus the civilian, resistance, and occupying factions. Full CWA data covers all four canonical islands: Everon, Malden, Kolgujev, and Nogova. This is an active, living design (see Gameplay Systems).

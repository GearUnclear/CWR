★ Build an Insurgency From Nothing

Start as one Resistance fighter with a rifle and a grudge. Ambush patrols, strip their dead, recruit the locals, and grow a hideout in the hills into an army. Territory pays for training, gear and reinforcements, so every zone you flip funds the next fight. Your named companions gain rank and skill, and stay dead if you spend them badly.

Vanish in Plain Sight

Sling the rifle across your back and you're a farmer. Drop it in the grass and you're nobody at all. Steal their jeep and drive it straight past the sentry. Every enemy squad reads you separately, and only the ones who saw you shoot remember your face.

Pick Your Island. Pick Your War.

Every campaign opens with a choice: the island, the occupier, the resistance. All four classic islands and every faction roster are in play, so you can fight NATO on Nogova, dig the USSR out of Everon, or set up matchups the original never shipped. Rosters, vehicles, disguises and loot all follow the factions you pick.

The Occupier Answers Back In development

Checkpoints choke the roads. Curfew empties the streets after dark. Blow an ambush and the reaction force comes in by helicopter while the town that fed you pays the price. Win loudly and the island tightens around your victory; lie low and the occupation spreads anyway. There is no quiet corner to grind in.

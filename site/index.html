Welcome, Soldier

DECLASSIFIED   FILE: UD-001 / EYES ONLY

Uslu dur! is a total game overhaul of ARMA: Cold War Assault, originally released in 2001 as Operation Flashpoint: Cold War Crisis: a ground-up rework of how the AI fights, perceives, and is commanded, spanning twelve mod-plans deep, culminating in Guerrilla Mode, a brand-new persistent open-world insurgency that doesn't exist in the original game at all.

It's built on top of Bohemia Interactive's own newly-opened Poseidon engine source, handed to the community "to study it, build on it, fix it, and create from it." This is the official portal and living field manual for the overhaul itself, the source of truth for the gameplay systems, the design loop that drives them, and Guerrilla Mode. See the footer, soldier, for the full Bohemia Interactive disclaimer.

★ Featured Operation: GUERRILLA MODE

You are one Resistance (GUER) fighter who liberates an island zone-by-zone and holds ground against an escalating occupier. A persistent single-player insurgency built with SQF mission scripting and C++ engine work side by side: this overhaul reaches directly into the engine source as well as the mission layer, wherever the design calls for it. A new campaign starts by picking the island, civilian faction, resistance faction, and occupying faction, backed by the full four-island, full-roster CWA data. Phase-1 "Three Zones", covering Camp / Village / Outpost, capture triggers, garrison spawning, an alert state machine, a resource economy, recruiting, loot & gear unlocks, a named companion, and save/load, is Code-Complete, pending its first live playtest.

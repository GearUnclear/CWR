Welcome, Soldier

DECLASSIFIED   FILE: UD-001 / EYES ONLY

Uslu dur! is a total overhaul of ARMA: Cold War Assault, the 2001 classic you know as Operation Flashpoint: Cold War Crisis. Same islands, same guns, same Cold War powder keg, but the soldiers fighting you now flank, suppress, panic and call in help like they mean it, and there's a brand-new campaign built for exactly one kind of player: the one who wants to build an insurgency from nothing and take an island back, zone by zone. That's Guerrilla Mode, and it doesn't exist in the original game at all.

Grab the free Steam demo and see for yourself. This portal is where we track what's shipping, what the AI does differently now, and how Guerrilla Mode plays. See the footer for the standard "not affiliated with Bohemia Interactive" disclaimer.

★ Featured Operation: GUERRILLA MODE

You're one Resistance fighter with nothing but a rifle and a grudge. Ambush patrols, loot their gear, recruit locals, and grow a cell into an army that liberates the island zone-by-zone, while the occupier escalates right back at you. Get spotted and it isn't instant death: the alert level climbs in stages, so a blown ambush is a problem you can still run from and recover, not a mission-ender. Every new campaign starts the same way: pick your island, pick your civilian, resistance and occupying factions, and go. The full four-island CWA roster is in play. The first playable slice, "Three Zones" (camp, village, outpost), is built, already boots on two different islands with the factions swapped, and is about to get its first real playtest.

The Undercover Set

One fighter, one rifle, one Soviet sentry, and the only thing changing between frames is what the sentry can see. The full sequence, with the rules spelled out, lives in the Undercover dossier.

Face-on, rifle slung: a farmer. His torso hides the AK. The garrison watches him walk past.
He turns around: a target. Same man, same rifle, one rotation. Walking away from a checkpoint is the dangerous direction.
The rifle rides inside. A civilian car is anonymous. Soldiers who would spot the AK on his back wave it through with the gun on the seat.
Checkpoints are where stolen trucks die. An army jeep reads as friendly at range. At 14 metres the sentry looks at the driver.
LIVE CAPTURETwenty metres is the line. At conversational range nothing stays hidden. Note the sentry, left: already prone, already aiming.

guerrilla: Traffic deletes seated men by moving them out of the hull first (World::_outVehicles leak)

::DeleteVehicle only flags a man sitting in a vehicle; he stays in
World::_outVehicles, which never purges deleted entries, so every crewman,
rider and seated corpse the ambient Traffic service deleted lived on as an
invisible object and was written into saves (found by the phase 5 column
work in CounterAttack, 37e24ba). Traffic now uses the same leave-then-
delete idiom through four file-static helpers (SeatHull finds the hull of
a man with or without a brain; LeaveSeat calls GetOutDriver / Gunner /
Commander / Cargo at the hull, no sound; DeleteTrafficPerson; and
DeleteTrafficHull, which empties every seat first: dead men, brainless
men and traffic-group members are moved out and deleted, any other living
man only steps out) on every seated-deletion path: DeleteCrew, the
SpawnEntry teardowns, SeatOrDelete, the quiet far despawn, CleanupReleased
(bodies may sit in another row of the convoy) and CleanupFleeing. Despawn
timing and gating are unchanged. One behaviour change: an escort torn down
at spawn for lack of a driver now deletes its seated gunner and riflemen
instead of ejecting them as a stray group.

before live 9 of 9 and dead 3 of 3 outlived their hulls, after 0 and 0.

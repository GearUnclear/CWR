Landmass (RoadRoute): Malden's Southeast Compound stands on its own
island (about 800 m of deep sea to Le Port; the earlier join leg crossed
the sea). RoadRoute::Landmass labels the 50 m geography cells joined over
land (a road over water is a bridge); the join lands only on the source's
landmass; gmLandmass and gmGeoGrid. A ground force never comes from, and
a Traffic vehicle never drives to, a base on another landmass (PickSource,
Resource with an INFO line, BuildSources, the wave fallback, Traffic
PickDest); such a base is an air-only source (PackageSource::airOnly) and
the birth chain re-plans as an insertion at once when no other base shares
the target's landmass. The offscreen fixture's SEBase moves to the Le Port
quay for that reason. The W and S Compound gates are closed wire gates.

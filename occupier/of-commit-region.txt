guerrilla: the playRegion default bounds base seeding only; fixtures and reference slices pin seedBases off

The default playRegion (the authored zones' bounding box padded 5 km,
used when the template authors none) was also bounding the Auto city
seed and the START TOWN list, which cut Sinai's K from 26 to 13 and the
Lebanon80 names fixture from 14 zones to 12. Now an AUTHORED region
bounds the base seed, the city seed and the START TOWN list; with none,
the default box bounds the base seed only (ZoneTuning::PlayRegion::
ContainsTown) and towns seed over the whole map as before 3946b5a. The
base-detection INFO line says which applies. The whole-map playRegion
that 3946b5a had put into the unit fixtures is reverted.

Every fixture mission and reference slice that authors no AIRFIELD or
SEAPORT pins seedBases = 0 (Market, Qrf, Undercover, UdIsland, the Demo
fixtures, factionpack, interaction, names, native, offscreen): they author
every zone they want, and the default-on seeding had materialised an
Abel garrison in guerrilla_offscreen_idle and added two zones to
guerrilla_names_classify. Shipped templates (Eden, Sinai, Demo) keep
seeding. Docs: ISLAND-PACKS.md 10, OCCUPIER-FORCES.md 16 / 17,
OFFSCREEN-CONTRACT.md 5.

Sinai after this: poolStart 132 (R 86, K 23, G 92), 7 seeded bases; K is
23 rather than 26 because three labels now name airfields (Egyptian
Airbase, Aqaba Internation Airport, Egyptian Fire Base) and no longer
count as towns, the designed effect. Eden 74 (R 38, K 18), Abel 60,
Lebanon80 24 unchanged.

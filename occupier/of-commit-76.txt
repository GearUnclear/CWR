guerrilla: auto-detect AIRFIELD and SEAPORT zones per island and seed them at load (#76 slice; seedBases, playRegion)

BaseDetect (pure core): airfields from the world's ILS table (ilsPosition /
ilsDirection / ilsTaxiIn / ilsTaxiOff and any SecondaryAirports; rejected
at the map origin, off the map or with a wet runway; centred on the apron;
an inherited ILS is never seeded) and from hangar / shelter / control-tower
clusters (300 m, at least 3 objects, 2 of them hangars; strips within
1500 m fold into one airfield, which keeps Sinai's Egyptian Airbase one
zone); seaports from pier / quay / crane / dock clusters (150 m, score
1.5 with one weight-1 structure, water within 30 m, the centre walked
inland to dry ground; "jerabina" the rowan tree excluded); outposts from
military clusters (120 m, at least 4) reported offline only. Authored
zones win (covered within max(300 m, footprint) of an authored military
zone or 150 m of any zone). Names: an airfield adopts an airfield-like
Names label in reach (the city seed then drops that label, so no Egyptian
Airbase CITY), else "<World> Airfield" / "<nearest settlement> Harbour"
where a settlement is a dry label with 3 objects within 300 m, within
4 km, numbered when duplicate.

Runtime (ZoneRegistry): seedBases (absent = 1 when the template authors
no AIRFIELD or SEAPORT) seeds the detected bases after LoadZones and
before the city seed, occupier-owned, seeded, garrison 12 / 8, default
income, rebuilt from config on load with dynamic state matched by name;
playRegion[] (absent = the authored zones' bounding box padded 5 km)
bounds the base seed, the city seed and CollectTownNames. gmZone slot 10
is the seeded flag; gmSeededBases and gmDetectedBases (status, coveredBy,
evidence). The roster R, the wave hub, the air origin and the sources see
seeded bases by type and owner. Lebanon80 authors seedBases = 0 with its
reason (no airfield or seaport in the theatre by design; Ramat David is
7 km south, the Tyre quay wall is not a harbour).

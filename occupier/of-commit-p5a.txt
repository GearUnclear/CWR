guerrilla: occupier forces phase 5, the package catalogue side (OCCUPIER-FORCES.md 6.5 armour and columns)

ForcePackages offers every ground package (DefaultPackages = foot, truck,
armed car, APC, tank; Phase3Packages stays for tests), with crew counts
(driver, gunner, commander as the hull has them), a seat cap of
min(12, transportSoldier) marking a hull that cannot hold the minimum as
seatsShort (quoted, never chosen), the town-edge role (armed hulls
support, trucks hold back), the foot downgrade when the source has no
stored spare (MustDowngrade / Downgrade, refund = the hull price), and
columns: a carrier (truck or APC) plus at most one escort (armed car, APC
or tank), priced as both hulls and both crews, lethality = carried
infantry x 1.25 + the escort bonus, pace = the slower hull. The choice is
unchanged over the enlarged set; both 6.5 worked examples still pick the
plain truck. Orchestrator decision: armour escorts a carrier rather than
going alone, since 3.2 prices a column by the slowest hull and the 6.5
table gives the tank a bonus with no seats.

The director still offers Phase3Packages until the CounterAttack side
(escort hull per record, support at the edge, commander seat) lands, so
live behaviour is unchanged by this commit.

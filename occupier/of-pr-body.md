## What the occupier does now (key on, the default from phase 7)

- **One pool pays for everything.** Garrison regrowth, refills of retaken towns and every operation draw on a theatre pool in soldier-equivalents (poolStart = max(24, R + 2K), about 60 on Malden). It refills slowly (full in about 4 h) and jumps when a reinforcement wave lands. Bands STRONG / STRETCHED / THIN / SPENT with hysteresis change what the occupier can afford and how it fights (garrison tiers, traffic, undercover detection and the QRF read the occupier war level).
- **A director that knows only what its men saw.** Dormant until the second distinct town falls, then awake with a small command budget. Candidates come from sightings, alarms, heat and flips, never the player's true position; it buys the cheapest package that is lethal enough (the fastest within 1.5 x when urgent): foot, truck, a column of a truck or APC with one armoured escort, a helicopter insertion, a jet or attack-helicopter strike. When strong it also goes for the player's bases and quiet towns; with a deliberate force, his HQ.
- **Forces are born out of sight and move at real speed.** At the base or farther back, never within 250 m of the target; a watched route re-sources to the next base back, then re-plans as an air insertion, then cancels unseen. Trucks brake and dismount at the town edge, escorts support, trucks hold back. Helicopters land troops 300 to 500 m out, in plain view if they must. Nothing pops in beside the player (pending deposits, the 3 x 3 hidden-release grid).
- **Any enemy standing in an empty liberated town or base retakes it** through the mirrored capture meter; a passing column drains too. Only a deliberate assault takes the HQ, through a meter at the HQ itself; the HQ is then destroyed with its cache and garage and the player sets up a new one anywhere.
- **Three waves** at control 0.75, 0.50 and the final stand, striking the densest cluster, able to retake bases. The win latches only when every enemy military zone is held at once.
- **Air strikes on towns** cut both sides' standing there by 40 (10 within 2 km), panic the civilians and halve the town's income for 20 minutes.
- **Alarm answers** replace the scripted QRF: a RED zone is answered natively by the director's package of choice. Old saves keep the scripted QRF and heat cooling (`legacySave`).
- **Lose state.** No HQ, no base and no town, after the campaign once had something: the campaign ends with a lose debriefing (EMLoser); saves stay available.

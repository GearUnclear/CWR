Players starting near a town report that it feels barely defended: enemies are rarely encountered, and the soldiers that do appear are difficult to find in places that make sense for controlling a town. The current code explains both symptoms: CITY zones have no native standing garrison, arbitrary start-town selection does not guarantee nearby military content, and existing garrisons are random groups around a marker rather than a security layout.

This issue proposes a focused first slice of #35: persistent town security and purposeful local placement, with diagnostics that distinguish missing content from actual spawn failures. #71 owns the broader island-scale/off-screen simulation contract.

**Research basis:** read-only source audit on 2026-10-11 at [`d4e3a5e`](https://github.com/GearUnclear/CWR/commit/d4e3a5ec310affd076c205ec3077ccb2d0b981dd), plus the player's report. No new gameplay run, live census of the player's save, or performance measurement was performed. Findings below describe the audited implementation; proposed counts and behavior are design recommendations.

**Target:** full official CWA Remaster and its `remaster.cpp` contract. Older Classic/Demo language in existing tests and issues is historical; do not add Classic compatibility shims or new Classic-only test lanes. See [current platform guidance](https://github.com/GearUnclear/CWR/blob/d4e3a5ec310affd076c205ec3077ccb2d0b981dd/CLAUDE.md#target-platform-the-remaster-classic-compatibility-is-being-dropped).

## Reproduction and diagnostic baseline

On full Remaster with the shared core and templates installed:

1. Start Malden from the authored camp; approach its Outpost. This is the existing nearby military-spawn control case.
2. Start new campaigns in two other selectable towns well away from that Outpost. Walk the main street and both road approaches, allowing the approximately five-second cache pass to run. Record whether any persistent occupier security exists; distinguish shakedown actors, traffic crews and unrelated assailants.
3. Capture the relevant zone's type/owner/reserve, `gmGarrisonSpawned`, `gmGarrisonLive`, `gmGarrisonGroups`, group/unit positions and distance from the zone center; use existing `gmTrafficDiag` for route availability/failure evidence ([commands](https://github.com/GearUnclear/CWR/blob/d4e3a5ec310affd076c205ec3077ccb2d0b981dd/arma_CWR/engine/Poseidon/Game/Guerrilla/TrafficCommands.cpp#L77-L108)).
4. Repeat on Everon and a supported LoBo island/faction combination. Evaluate occupation coverage against the island's intended playable region, not every map label.

Expected current result from source: city garrison queries remain empty because CITY is excluded; remote military reserves only materialize inside the [default 800 m gate](https://github.com/GearUnclear/CWR/blob/d4e3a5ec310affd076c205ec3077ccb2d0b981dd/arma_CWR/engine/Poseidon/Game/Guerrilla/GarrisonCache.cpp#L229-L249). Record actual results rather than treating this source prediction as a completed playtest.

## Proposed first slice

### 1. Explicit, persistent town security

Define occupier security strength and roles separately from a city's civilian support/administrative owner, so a NEUTRAL civic record does not accidentally mean zero occupation presence. Specify initial security, casualties, caching, removal/withdrawal on liberation, and any explicit peaceful exemptions. Use the selected occupier's resolved faction descriptors; counts and composition must be configurable. Initial tuning candidates: 4-6 soldiers for a small village, 8-12 for a larger town, subject to playtest and frame budget.

Do not simply set every city to OCCUPIER and reuse the military capture path. Cities already have [support-based liberation and occupation-presence checks](https://github.com/GearUnclear/CWR/blob/d4e3a5ec310affd076c205ec3077ccb2d0b981dd/arma_CWR/engine/Poseidon/Game/Guerrilla/ZoneRegistry.cpp#L2108-L2139). Feed town security into those checks deliberately; units posted just outside the current 150 m presence circle must not become invisible to the control model, nor may a stale reserve permanently prevent a cleared town from progressing.

### 2. Defenders in meaningful places

Allocate one real roster across 1-2 main road approaches/checkpoints, a short street patrol, and a reserve/command position. Derive the settlement footprint from available roads and buildings instead of assuming the map label is the best defensive point. Validate dry, navigable, unblocked positions and patrol connectivity; use building interior/overwatch positions only when the model exposes usable positions. Provide a logged outdoor fallback for maps without suitable buildings.

Assign each group its own home anchor or route. Keep fixed guards at their posts during ordinary suspicion; send the patrol/reserve to investigate. Normal combat AI must remain able to react, rather than an unconditional stationing loop repeatedly overriding it. Ensure start-town selection retains the HQ/garage safety envelope and a reachable nearby encounter; do not place enemies at the player's spawn or compensate by teleporting troops into view.

Reuse the existing road queries, faction resolution, AI orders and native persistence where suitable. Whether to extend GarrisonCache with explicit roles or introduce a sibling service is an implementation decision, not settled by this audit. Simple stock barriers/sandbags can make entrance posts readable, using verified full-Remaster assets.

### 3. Spawn accounting and lifecycle

Expose intended strength, attempted/created/alive bodies, cached survivors, assigned anchors and reasons for missing coverage: policy exemption, distance cache, no usable anchor, class failure or group-budget exhaustion. Reuse `gmTrafficDiag` for traffic rather than rebuilding it. Verify that partial creation does not consume missing bodies silently: [current reserve accounting](https://github.com/GearUnclear/CWR/blob/d4e3a5ec310affd076c205ec3077ccb2d0b981dd/arma_CWR/engine/Poseidon/Game/Guerrilla/GarrisonCache.cpp#L402-L437) subtracts requested group sizes before deciding whether any bodies materialized, so a partially successful attempt needs explicit coverage.

Persist casualties, chosen anchors/routes and group references without duplicating soldiers or refilling the roster on every cache transition. Define migration for saves predating town security, including how it avoids repopulating already-liberated towns. Keep live-unit/group budgets bounded; measure the added cost before raising island-wide density.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fresh campaign in each tested occupied town has a documented security budget and visible, reachable occupation presence along sensible streets/approaches. Explicit peaceful exemptions are identifiable in diagnostics.
- [ ] Two non-default START TOWN selections far from authored military posts are covered; headquarters safety and a navigable player exit are preserved.
- [ ] Guards occupy distinct validated anchors; a patrol completes its connected street circuit over multiple cycles. Soldiers are not stuck inside non-enterable buildings, submerged, or scattered in arbitrary fields.
- [ ] YELLOW suspicion sends designated responders while fixed posts remain covered; combat, disengagement and return-home behavior are exercised over several alert cycles.
- [ ] Diagnostics reconcile intended/created/alive/reserve counts and report partial class/placement/group-budget failures. Zero security by policy is distinguishable from a failed spawn.
- [ ] Leaving/re-entering the cache area and cross-process save/reload preserve casualties, roles and anchors, with no duplicate defenders or automatic healing. A pre-feature save follows the documented migration policy.
- [ ] Town support, intimidation, readiness and liberation remain coherent with the new security presence, including guards on outer approaches and the transition after defenders are removed.
- [ ] Full Remaster coverage includes Malden, Everon and a supported LoBo island/faction combination; tests exercise descriptor/side swaps and a town without usable building interiors. Existing military garrison behavior remains covered.
- [ ] Record live AI/group counts and frame-time impact, and complete a human walk-through of entrances, main street, alert response and liberation. Close on measured placement/play behavior, not just a spawn-count assertion.

Implemented behavior
- Cache exclusive bone membership for fire-geometry components when each ManType loads.
- Use the model's actual rifle/launcher bones, not fixed component numbers. Mixed or unweighted
  components retain their previous behavior.
- Classify only components actually intersected by a ray. Ordinary physical geometry is unchanged.
- Check current weapon visibility and inventory before accepting a weapon component.
- Weapon hits use an ordinary world impact with dust and a solid/armor hit sound. Flesh keeps its
  existing blood effect and flesh-hit sound. The ordinary effect stays at the contact point.
- Skip both direct damage and the stock bullet's small indirectHit contribution to its carrier
  for weapon-only non-explosive hits. Explosive direct and splash damage remain active.
- Transfer weaponHit in ExplosionDammageEffects messages. Older message formats default to false.

Performance study
Baseline = clean aa52258 production code plus the same read-only measurement helpers.
Patched = final production fix. Both local builds use the same compiler/configuration/dependencies.
Workload = real stock SoldierEB objects with AK74 and RPGLauncher; rays through animated fire
geometry component centers. Both variants returned matching intersection counts.
Counts = 1, 32, 128, 512 soldiers. Seven samples each; 200,000 rays/sample; 20,000 warmup rays.
Alternated baseline/patched ordering. 11.2 million measured ray tests total.
The timer covers Object::Intersect, including animation, convex clipping and classification.
Scene creation, rendering, AI, projectile simulation, effects, and network transport are excluded.

No collision-throughput regression was observed at these scales. The small improvement is not
evidence of a general FPS gain: code layout, compiler and host noise can affect this measurement.
Raw samples: performance-results.json. Reproduction driver: benchmark.py, using harness.py.
Bone classification is cached per model type; each intersected component adds a lookup and weapon
hits additionally check the small inventory list. There is no per-frame scan of all model vertices.

Further performance coverage, before making a general performance claim:
- Matched frame-time runs with increasing visible AI counts, graphics settings, and incoming shots.
- Miss-heavy rays and model mixtures, including addon models, in addition to the hit-heavy stock test.
- Sustained automatic fire to measure particle/effect load and allocations.
- Two updated multiplayer clients to verify observer visuals, sounds, and impact-message bandwidth.
- Multiple hardware profiles and repeated runs. Finite samples cannot prove behavior at every scale.

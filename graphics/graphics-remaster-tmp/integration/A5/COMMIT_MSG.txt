graphics: temporal anti-aliasing steadies foliage and cleans cheap soft effects

What the player sees: alpha-tested leaves and wire stop crawling when the
camera moves, and sub-pixel wire stays whole: the M113, Jeep and Zelda
antennas, tent ropes and power lines that 4x MSAA alone draws as dashes
are continuous lines. In a yaw pan over the forest edge the crowns change
22.6% less from frame to frame. The ambient occlusion and sun shaft sample
patterns turn every frame and the history averages them. Colour and
brightness do not change (mean colour within 0.3 levels and the 5th and
95th brightness percentiles within 0.4 on the forest and lowsun_fp views,
within 1 level at town_night, whose fire differs shot to shot); the frame
is 6.7% less sharp on the
forest hero view by the mean neighbour difference, sharper on lowsun_fp
(+2.4%) and on Sinai (+1.0%), and 9.5 to 10.7% less sharp during a pan.
A Jeep crossing at 25 m leaves no trail at 0.12 or 0.5 m per step; fire
and smoke keep their flicker and brightness.

How it works: Graphics/Remaster/TemporalAA (pure) holds the math. The
world is drawn shifted by a Halton(2, 3) sub-pixel offset over 16 frames:
ApplyTaaJitter fixes the frame's offset and camera at its first world pass
(after the frame gates) and hands the offset to the three remastered
vertex shaders through vpScale.zw, guarded so a zero offset leaves every
position exactly as computed; UploadVSScreenConstants keeps it for the
world's own screen-space draws (smoke, rain), and EndWorldScene and the
next InitDraw take it off, so the HUD, dialogs, portraits, 3D previews and
the shadow-map depth pass are never jittered (TaaPassJitterNdc is the one
rule). A -0.5 texture LOD bias on the material samplers rides with it. Two
post stages land: Temporal (psPostTaa) rebuilds the frame at the pixel
centre (Gaussian exp(-4 d^2) over the 3x3), reprojects from the nearest
depth of the 3x3 with the camera motion (rows built in double precision,
BuildTaaReprojection), reads the half-float history with a 9-tap
Catmull-Rom, drops it where its stored view depth misses the reprojected
one by 3% + 0.15 m or it falls outside the 3D viewport, clips it into this
frame's YCoCg box and blends at alpha 0.1 with luma weights; Sharpen
(psPostTaaSharpen) writes the result back into every sample of the frame
target with a bounded unsharp mask (no halos) and a +-1 LSB dither. When
they run the composite draws into the TAA input instead of the frame
target. The particle mask: Engine::BeginTranslucentObjects, called once by
Scene::DrawObjectsAndShadowsPass2 between the opaque world and the
back-to-front translucent objects, copies the frame target; where smoke,
fire, muzzle flashes, glass, the cockpit pass, rain or flares changed a
pixel by more than 2 levels the resolve takes up to 0.8 of this frame
(all of it above 24 levels) without luma weights. History is dropped by
any frame without a resolve (the chain runs on the engine frame counter),
a camera cut (over 50 m or 60 degrees in a frame) and a new target size.
The post GL helpers moved to EngineGL33_PostPass.hpp for the stage files.

The switch and its gates: taa (A1 registry, now landed, GPU section
"taa"), with the sub-knobs taaSharpen 0.5, taaMipBias -0.5 and taaAlpha
0.1 under it. The gate runs it on a drawn world with the post effects on,
not in a vehicle interior, under optics or night vision; the stages also
skip a frame that turned out to clear its depth or draw an overlay.
taa=0 draws the frame of before: no jitter, no LOD bias, the same post
plan and the same AO and shaft patterns.

Kept, rewritten and dropped from the prototype (temporal track): kept the
Halton jitter in vpScale.zw, the resolve (3x3 reconstruction, nearest
depth, double-precision camera reprojection, Catmull-Rom history, depth
rejection, YCoCg clip, alpha 0.1, motion-raised alpha, luma weights), the
bounded sharpen 0.5 and the mip bias -0.5. Rewritten: the stages as
PostTemporal and PostSharpen in EngineGL33_PostTemporal.cpp; the jitter in
the remastered vertex shaders only, guarded; the view depth in the history
alpha instead of two R32F targets; the reconstruction kernel exp(-4 d^2)
instead of exp(-2.29 d^2) (a sweep: 15.5% against 5.0% less sharp on the
hero view, wires still continuous); the per-frame AO and shaft pattern
instead of the stochastic 4-tap AO without blur; the particle mask, which
the plan listed as phase 2. Dropped: taaStochAlpha, taaGamma, taaJitter,
taaDepthReject, the stochastic AO knobs (the stochastic PCSS is B1's), the
TAA debug view. Not built: the Taa LitConstants slot and the raw shadow
sampler on unit 6 (no A5 shader reads them; B1 lands both), per-object
motion vectors and a resolve before the tone curve (the remastered shaders
tonemap per fragment and the frame target is RGBA8 display space).

Measured cost (RTX 4060 Ti, 1920x1080, render scale 1.0, 4x MSAA, one
game, 3 interleaved rounds, not a quiet-machine run): the taa section
0.16 to 0.22 ms and sharpen 0.05 to 0.10 ms; the GPU frame median rises by
0.71 to 0.81 ms (forest_edge_golden 5.55 to 6.30, lowsun_fp 5.39 to 6.20,
town_night 6.21 to 6.92), most of it the texture LOD bias in the world
pass: with taaMipBias=0 the rise is 0.08 to 0.34 ms and the hero view 11.2%
less sharp. The opaque copy is within the round-to-round noise.

Known risks: during camera motion the frame is about 10% less sharp than
without TAA (the look check's limit; taaSharpen 0.6 to 0.75 would buy it
back, 0.75 gave +1.6% on the still hero view); the LOD bias costs 0.4 to
0.7 ms of world time on these views (more than the 0.3 ms the plan
estimated for TAA); moving soldiers and vehicles have camera motion only
(no trail seen at 0.5 m per step, but rotors, first-person weapon sway and
fast aircraft are untested); a still camera shows a frame-to-frame jitter
residual of 0.0026 luma (0.0007 without TAA, under one level); glass edges
and everything drawn after the opaque copy take most of their own frame,
so they keep less anti-aliasing; vehicle interiors get no TAA and the
first frame into an interior or optics goes out jittered and unresolved;
no RTX 3060 was measured; the motion clips step the camera once per two
rendered frames.

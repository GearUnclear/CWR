graphics: fires, flares and shell bursts light the men and cast shadows

What the player sees: at night a camp fire, a burning wreck, a shell
burst, a flare, a muzzle flash, a tracer and a missile motor light the
ground and the men around them as real light sources, warm and bright
near the source and falling off with distance, and the four strongest
throw long radial shadows from the men and vehicles. A shell burst
whitens the frame for as long as Classic's does: at eye height the first
flash frame reads 57.1 against Classic's 55.6 and the frame 0.13 s later
57.0 against 58.0; from 5 m up both read brighter than Classic (114.4
and 112.1 against 70.4 and 75.7). The flash is white-hot and cools to a
fire's warmth. Smoke and dust round a burst glow from inside, and the air
round a flash takes a faint glow. A burning wreck's black smoke stays
dark against the night sky, with a small warm halo over the embers. By
day the same lights keep a fifth of their strength (bursts) or 12%
(fires), so a noon shell still marks the crater; the shadows run only at
night. A paused game holds every light where it was, and under time
acceleration bursts still end.

How it works: Graphics/Rendering/Lighting/CombatLights (pure) keeps the
combat lights of the world in one set, ticked by the simulation step in
World::Simulate (a paused frame does not age them; one tick ages them by
at most kCombatMaxTickStep, 0.25 s, so setAccTime 4 at a low frame rate
still ends a burst and the set does not grow). Bursts (muzzle, cannon,
explosion) are spawned and forgotten; continuous lights (wreck embers,
camp fires, tracers, flares, missile motors) are owned by their object
through a CombatLightHandle. The sources are the existing gameplay hooks:
WeaponLightSource and WeaponFireSource (the firing vehicles and soldiers
pass the round's hit, which sets the muzzle strength), SmokeSourceVehicle
(the fuel burst and the wreck's embers), Explosion (a burst sized by the
explosion), ShotBullet (tracers), Missile (the motor while it thrusts),
IlluminatingShell (flares) and Fireplace. Each kind has its envelope
(EvaluateCombatLight: a 30 ms muzzle; an explosion whose white-hot flash
holds 0.15 s, then falls in 50 ms while a flicker fades in and the colour
cools to the fire's; a fire that breathes on two slow sines and smooth
noise) and a ball falloff E = I * window / (1 + d^2 / r0^2) that ends at
the light's range (at most 120 m, a flare 400 m). The fire's colour is
(1.0, 0.70, 0.45); burst colours stay at or under its red over blue.

The lights enter A6's tiled list as up to 12 HDR entries at the head of
the frame's list (MergeCombatFrameLights, packed as kind 2 with the
window range), and the forward shader shades them in linear light
outside the classic clamp (combatLights), with a flash highlight on
glossy materials but not on terrain. The classic budget, B3's physical
lamps and low beams, and B7's lamp streaks skip those head entries; the
streaks give a combat light its own model at its own linear light. While
combat light is on, the classic light of the same source lights nothing
(UpdateLightTiles and Object::DrawDecal skip the Combat and Fire kinds);
it stays for its glow sprite and for the switch off. The list keeps every
fire, flare and missile motor first and cuts the weakest bursts and
tracers (KeepCombatLights). Smoke and dust particles take the combat
lights in DrawDecal next to B3's physical night light; a fire's light
reaches a particle of its own column through the smoke below it
(CombatSmokeTransmittance), so a wreck's black column glows at its base
only. The shadows: up to four lights are picked for the four channels of
a half-resolution RGBA8 mask on unit 14, with hysteresis (a light keeps
its channel unless another is 1.5 times stronger for 0.3 s). At the start
of each world pass EngineGL33_PostCombat traces from each mask texel
toward each shadowed light through the previous frame's half-res linear
depth (24 steps over at most 25 m), then blurs it with a depth-aware
7-tap filter. The forward shader maps its fragment into last frame's
screen through three rows in the FrameLights block and takes one
bilinear tap for all four lights. The flash exposure: explosions and
cannon at the camera open the exposure in proportion to their irradiance
there, at night only, at most 4x, held with a 0.12 s decay. The composite
adds the in-scatter of up to 8 lights. Debug view 11 ("Combat shadow
mask", after B6's two) shows the mask.

The switch and its gates: combatLights (Combat light, Night and combat)
and combatShadows (Combat light shadows), both landed (26/26 switches
landed), with the sub-knobs combatFlash, combatFire, combatSmoke and
combatScatter (0 to 3, default 1). combatLights is on with the light list
and a combat light whose reach comes within 60 m of the camera; without
the light list it is off and the classic lights light the frame as
before. combatShadows also needs a night above 0.3, the post effects (the
mask reads the post pass's linear depth, which LinearDepth then runs for)
and the world in view. The simulation does not depend on either switch,
so off and on shoot the same paused frame. GPU sections: "combatmask"
for the mask pass, the shading in "world". No LitConstants slot (72
stays padding, Count 79).

Kept, rewritten and dropped from the research prototype: kept the
envelopes' shapes, the ball falloff and its window, the day scaling, the
gameplay hooks, the analytic in-scatter, smoke lit from inside, the
glossy flash and the night-eye lift. Rewritten: the lights are A6's
Combat and Fire kinds at the head of the tiled list instead of an HDR
flag through the 8 per-draw slots; the per-fragment trace through last
frame's depth became the half-res mask pass with hysteresis and blur;
strengths come from the round's hit and the explosion size, not a table;
the explosion's flash holds 0.15 s; the fire, fireball, muzzle and cannon
colours are less saturated; a fire lights its own smoke only as far as
the smoke lets the light through, and its lit air is a small halo.
Dropped: LitConstants slots 59 to 64 (the rows ride in the FrameLights
block), unit 6 (the mask is on unit 14), the 2x2 ordered jitter, the
combatWreck knob and the prototype's light flags in Lights.hpp.

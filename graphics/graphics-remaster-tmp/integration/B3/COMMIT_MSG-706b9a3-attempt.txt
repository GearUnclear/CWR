graphics: low-beam headlights, moon shadows and stars at night

What the player sees: a moonless country road is dark, under a field of
stars, and the Jeep's low beams lay their own light on it: the hot spot
20 to 30 m ahead is the brightest part of the road (218 levels against
128 at the bumper and 143 at 40 m; before, 113 at the hot spot and 142
at the bumper, a flat disc), the verges take the wide spill and the
light stops at a top edge. The far field away from any lamp goes from
20 to 14 levels. Under a moon the moon casts shadows: a parked Jeep, the
bushes and the town's trees lay moon shadows on the road and the lawn.
Street lamps fall off from the bulb instead of lighting everything
within 50 m at full strength and reaching 500 m: the town square at 22:00
loses its flat khaki wash (lawn saturation 0.11 to 0.03) and its mean
brightness halves (98 to 56 levels), with lamp-lit facades and crowns
near the lamps (the birch around a lamp 61 levels, not black), and the
camp fire's smoke is fire-lit instead of white. Stars are small soft
splats with a faint colour each (mean peak over the sky 33 to 76
levels). By day, in Classic and under night vision nothing changes.

How it works:
- Moon key (moonShadows): ComputeKeyLight (LightRig) chooses what the
  shader's sun slots carry. Under the moon key it moves a share of F9's
  moon (kMoonFull 0.02, its chroma and sky fill unchanged) out of the
  shadowless litMoon term into litSun/litSunDir, so the shadow map, the
  terrain horizon (the key's azimuth and elevation), the cloud field and
  the glossy highlight follow the moon. MoonKeyShare (NightLights) is 0
  unless the gate is on (sun 3 deg under, moon above 3 deg, phase above
  0.25, no night vision) and fades in over 3 deg above the sun and moon
  edges and 0.1 of phase, so the shadows fade in. The scene renders the
  shadow pass at night when Engine::RemasterMoonKey() says so (the
  frame's gate) and RenderShadowMapDepthPass takes the moon's direction.
  Under the moon key the sun shadow constants select B1's stock 12-tap
  filter, not the soft one (a fiftieth of the noon sun shows no
  penumbra); the leaf lift stays.
- Physical lamps and low beams (nightLights), a model of the tiled light
  list (the gate needs lightTiles). With LitConstants slot 71 (Night: x
  on, y lamp intensity per start metre, z low-beam peak) on, street lamps
  and headlights leave the classic model and its budget of 7 (which now
  ranks the classic kinds among themselves) for physicalLight: intensity
  over d^2 + 0.25 (lamps 1.9 * start * nightLamp, headlights 800 *
  nightSpot * the low-beam pattern), the configured colour taken to
  linear, no warming; the low beam has a hot band 1.7 deg under its axis,
  a top edge closing from 0.3 to 1.4 deg above it, a +-14 deg core and a
  +-34 deg spill, a faint floor to 34 deg under, nothing behind the lamp;
  it is aimed from the vehicle's body (the lamp's heading, the body's
  pitch along it, 1% down; LowBeamAim in UpdateLightTiles), not from the
  model's beam axis. A physical reach (PhysicalReach: where the strongest
  irradiance falls to 0.002, at most the classic 10 start distances; 218
  m for a 50 m-start lamp) ends each light in a smooth window, and the
  tiles bin physical lights by it. Rough diffuse (Oren-Nayar, sigma 0.5),
  leaves wrapped with the crown normal and the forced diffuse as for the
  sun, a lamp's classic ambient capped, a gleam on glossy materials, the
  sum linear outside the classic 1.0 clamp, times the draw's night
  factor. A near fade from 1.0 to 0.25 m off the bulb and the back plane
  keep a lamp off its own housing and vehicle (a draw in an instanced run
  has no single object to exclude by id). F9's night eye is unchanged.
- Smoke and dust (Object::DrawDecal) take the same lamps and headlights
  through Engine::ParticleNightLight and FrameLights::ParticleLight,
  summed in linear light; the classic loop skips those two kinds then.
- Stars: DrawPoints draws each star as a 5-vertex fan (MakeStarSplat:
  centre 1.8 times the star's alpha, 0 at a 1.6 px square's corners, a
  cool-to-warm tint per star at unit luminance) while the stars gate is
  on, the 2 px bilinear dot otherwise.
- New Graphics/Remaster/NightLights.{hpp,cpp} (the moon key share, the
  Night slot, the star splat); the physical model, the aim, the reach and
  ParticleLight are in Graphics/Rendering/Lighting/FrameLights. The
  planned padding 68-76 split around slot 71 (litPlanned 68-70,
  litPlannedLate 72-76).

The switches and their gates: nightLights, moonShadows and stars
(night track, Night and combat), landed (12/26). nightLights and stars
run with the night effect above 0.01, nightLights also needs the light
list switch; moonShadows runs under the moon key (above), and its gate
also opens the sun shadow gates. Sub-knobs nightLamp 1.0 and nightSpot
1.0 under nightLights. GPU sections: world (nightLights, stars), shadow
(moonShadows). Off is the look before this step.

Known risks: towns at night are about half as bright as before (the
lever is nightLamp); moonless country away from the beam is very dark
(the open night-exposure question); the closest verge in a headlight is
yellow-beige (hue 41 deg, saturation 0.33, value 196: the grass
texture's own colour at its noon saturation; the moonless road's verges
are neutral, saturation 0.05); F9's night eye greys medium-lit lamp
light, so pools read grey-warm, not amber; the beam is the configured
rosy white (0.9, 0.8, 0.8); hill roads were checked by unit test only,
not in game; physical lights skip the classic budget, so a tile with
many lamps evaluates all of them; lamp light still passes through walls;
moon glitter on the sea (the water sparkle reads litSun) is untested;
the first frame of a moon key may sample the previous map with the
moon's direction (the key fades in from 0 there). NightLights.{hpp,cpp}
and test_night_lights.cpp are new files to add with the commit.

graphics: rain wets cloth and stone, fills puddles and streaks lamp light

What the player sees: rain now wets the world, not only the ground. Cloth,
tents, plaster, brick and wood darken as they soak; paint, glass, metal and
asphalt take a thin water film. In the sun the film shows a soft warm sheen
toward the sun (even at a low sun it does not clip to white, and the gravel
shows through it); at night it throws every street lamp, fire and headlight
across the wet ground as a long warm streak toward the eye. Puddles stand in
level hollows as dark water with rain rings and keep mirroring the houses
(B6's reflections). After the rain the world stays wet and dries over
minutes, faster in sun and wind, and the puddles linger longest. Dry weather
looks exactly as before.

How it works:
- Graphics/Remaster/Wetness.{hpp,cpp} (pure): the world's surface wetness and
  puddle level, stepped with the weather (Weather::MoveClouds). Rain wets with a
  90 s time constant at full rain, puddles fill over 600 s; surfaces dry over
  1200 s divided by a drying factor of sun height, clear sky and wind, puddles
  over 3600 s. A SetRain change made at once (a mission's or a capture's
  `0 setRain x`) snaps the state to what that rain settles at, on the first
  step that moves the clock (a change made while paused waits for its rain).
- Graphics/Remaster/SurfaceMaterials.{hpp,cpp} (pure): 14 surface classes from
  a table of texture-name rules per object kind (the stock Czech and English
  tokens of the research prototype) that mods extend or override with
  CfgSurfaceMaterials (surface, any[], none[], kinds[]); each kind's default
  class; the porosity table the shader mirrors. The object kind rides on the
  draw descriptor (render::DrawObjectKind, set by Shape::Draw from the
  object's simulation and class, cached on its EntityType); each texture
  classifies itself once per kind (TextureGL33::SurfaceClassFor).
- LitConstants: MatClass (58) per draw, the class and kind, uploaded only
  when they change while the wet gate is on, plus the frame's streak and
  reflection flags; Weather (73): wetness, rain, puddle level, time. Both zero
  while the gate is off.
- Shaders: a new prelude piece, Puddles (wetPuddle), is pasted by the
  reflections' trace too, so the forward pass's puddles and the trace's
  mirrors are the same puddles; it adds the height map's hollows to B6's noise
  and reads the height map with smoothed interpolation, so no puddle edge
  follows the terrain's triangles. wetSurface in the surface piece darkens by
  porosity (the land tells grass from bare ground by its albedo; matte paint
  and rubber darken a little too, so wet paint reads deeper, not greyer), lays
  the film (sun glint, sky reflection on objects at half weight and capped,
  lamp streaks over A6's tiled lights with B3's physical lamps), run-down
  streaks on walls and hulls near the eye, rain rings on puddles near the eye.
  With the wet weather on, B6's trace leaves the ground's darkening to the
  forward pass and takes its puddles from wetPuddle.
- The sun glint is the film's lobe and the puddle's, blended by the water's
  share as the lamp streaks are, under its own peak cap (kWetSunCap 3; the
  lamps keep 80). A low sun's lobe on a 0.3 rough film peaks at well over a
  hundred times the sun, so under the lamps' cap it clipped a white column
  from the horizon to the eye. Bare ground breaks the film: its glint is 0.5
  rough on half the film's share. The pixel's footprint widens the lobe's
  alpha by 0.25 per metre, so far wet ground draws no white dashes.

Switches and gates: wetWeather (sub-knobs wetness, -1 = the weather, 0..1
forces it, and puddles, 1) and lampStreaks. Gates as A1 built them: wetness
above 0.02 (the larger of surfaces and puddles), the streaks also a lamp lit
at night. With wetWeather off the reflections' ground follows the rain as
before. triFeatureState reports wet=.

From the prototype (research track materials, override_v9): kept the
porosity response, the water film, run-down streaks, sheltered ground drier,
puddles in hollows with rain rings, the v9 split of a dry base and a water
film lobe for the light reflections. Rewritten: classes from a rule table
with an object-kind field on the descriptor (no global), terrain per pixel
from its albedo, wetness as world state, puddle mirrors through B6's pass,
streaks over A6's tiles, one lobe per roughness blended at puddle edges, the
sun glint under its own cap, rougher on bare ground and widened by the pixel
footprint. Dropped: vbfix (F8), the dry satin rim, the albedo bump, dry glass
reflection, the research knobs and logs.

Cost (4060 Ti, 1080p, scale 1.0, 4x MSAA, other lanes' games running; one
reading per run): GPU frame minimum on minus off +0.57 ms at the wet night
town, the wet street and after the rain, +0.14 ms in rain on the fields, none
at dry noon (gated off). After the glint rework, one more reading: +1.30 ms at
the wet night town and +0.51 ms after the rain. The spread between runs is of
that size. Not measured on a quiet GPU or a 3060.

Tests: test_wet_weather.cpp (15 cases: the integrator wets, dries faster in
sun and wind, puddles lag, snaps, the knobs; the classes on the research log's
stock names, kind defaults, a mod's rules, object kinds, the shader's porosity
table; the descriptor's kind outside its equality; the switches, sub-knobs and
gates; the shaders' and backend's off contract and the shared puddles; the
sun glint's own cap far below the lamps', its rougher, smaller lobe on bare
ground and the footprint term), the LitConstants layout and piece table
cases, the landed-switch cases. Six mutations turn their cases red (the
sixth puts the sun back under the lamps' cap of 80). Gate tags 642/642; the
full suite has only the 6 known localisation failures; smoke_abel 1/1
(KHR_debug 131154, 131169, 131218 only).

In game: tmp/integration/B7/ (lane captures b7lane: the wet night town hero,
the low sun after the rain, the wet street by day, rain on the fields, dry
noon; off against the base build within its own repeat noise). The low sun
and the night town were shot again after the glint rework (b7glint). In the
low sun's ground band (the lower 45 % of the frame), 1.39 % of pixels are
over 200 levels and 0.15 % over 240. Before the rework the figures were
12.0 % and 9.0 %; with the switch off they are 0.02 % and 0 %. The glare
column's core reads 143 levels, and the gravel's texture there measures 3.7
(5.4 off, 0.9 when it clipped). B6's mirror of the Jeep stays in the
puddle. The night town's lamp streaks and puddles are as before (0.08 % of
its ground over 240 levels).

Known risks: a puddle in the low sun's path still glows warm and bright (up
to 234 levels on its sun side), though it keeps its mirror; by day in rain
cloth and walls darken only a few levels; terrain classes come from the
albedo, so a grey lawn under warm lamps may still pool water in the trace
while the forward pass keeps it dry (a reflection without the puddle's
darkening); dry glass has no sky reflection, which departs from the plan's
Keep list (it needs a dry-weather switch, a follow-up); the trace's road
test on land reads the class the terrain draw left set (harmless while no
island texture code matches a road token); no persistence of the wetness
across a save; motion not checked (rain rings animate, streaks follow the
camera); cost unmeasured on a quiet GPU.

graphics: low-sun vehicles keep Classic's evening hue and readable shaded sides

What the player sees: at a low sun the side of a vehicle turned away from
it is no longer a dark grey shape. Its paint, stowage and markings read at
about 0.6 of the sunlit side (M113 and Jeep 0.61 / 0.62 at a 12.7 degree
sun, 0.57 / 0.61 at 7.5, 0.55 / 0.61 at 4.8; the Sinai Zelda and HMMWV
0.60 / 0.63 at 12.2 and 0.56 / 0.59 at 7.6), where it was 0.41 to 0.49
(Classic 0.33 to 0.53). The sunlit side of a hull takes the original's
evening colour from 25 degrees of sun down, as Classic's own sunset ramp
does: warmer than before in every measured box and at least as warm as
Classic except two Jeep values, slightly less warm than Classic on one
ratio each (lowsun_box r:b 1.26 against 1.28, dawn_box5 g:r 0.59 against
0.58; the other ratio warmer). Golden-hour tree crowns lose their pale
grey-green: the big broadleaf at forest_edge_golden comes down to
Classic's brightness and reads greener, with the same light-to-dark
spread inside the crown. The ground, its cast shadows and the noon frame
keep their light.

How it works:
- lowSunRig (Graphics/Remaster/LightRig, ComputeLightRig): the sun takes a
  further LowSunHueShare of Classic's sunset hue over EveningSunChroma (0.4
  from 25 degrees down, 0.3 more below 9). The sun shafts take the rig's
  sun colour (the post pass's shaft tint), so the low-sun hue tints them
  too. Vertical faces get sideways fill below 40 degrees (LowSunFill: full
  from 8 degrees down to the horizon, faded out under it): the sky part of
  the anti-sun horizon times 1 + 2.6 fill, the sunward one 1 + 0.5 fill,
  the ground bounce 1 + 1.3 fill. The zenith term, the exposure meter and
  the sun's strength are untouched, so up-facing ground and its shadows
  keep their light; from 40 degrees up, and at night, the rig is unchanged
  to the bit. New: a limit on the fill for walls, the rule of the twilight
  cap but at every sun height the fill runs (-2 to 40 degrees), not only
  in twilight: the fill lifts a vertical face at most to the visible sky
  and never below its value without the fill (under a dim dusk sky the
  prototype lit a wall facing away from a 1 to 3 degree sun at 1.4 to 2.1
  times the visible sky; under a clear sky the limit does not bind).
- crownCanopy (shadeRemastered, the research's crown V18 as plain code): the
  sun reaching a leaf crosses the crown in front of it (crownSunPath, the
  crown as a sphere, optical depth 1 per radius, a quarter always passes),
  the core sees 0.45 of the sky instead of 0.6, the leaf albedo deepens its
  saturation at equal luminance (a power of 1.3, renormalised), and below
  35 degrees of sun, fully below 12, the crown takes 0.65 of the sun and
  0.75 of the sky. Crowns only: grass (terrain draws) keeps its look.
- The canopy flag rides in litHorizonSun.w (slot 23 had a free w): no
  LitConstants slot lands, the layout is unchanged.
- Debug view 7 shows each lit surface's albedo (display space; the lit
  shaders see it as 4 through ShaderDebugView). With post=0 a shot of it
  divides a lit shot into the light on every surface: the research's
  calibration view, kept as the plan allowed (5 is the linear depth, 6
  A6's lights per tile; the lane, on 807ce4b, had it at 6).

Switches and gates: lowSunRig and crownCanopy have landed (12/26 on the
merged tree), both gated "always" (the rig is constants once a frame; the
canopy is a few terms on foliage, and both fade themselves by sun
height). The rig reads the switch (it runs before the gates, which take
its cloud cover); the canopy flag comes from its gate. GPU section: world
for both. Off (both 0) is the rig and the foliage of 807ce4b.

1.01 sentinel became lowSunRig and the g_variant selector plain code under

Added: the limit on the fill, grass left out of the canopy. Dropped: the

composite (F5), all landed as fixes; the debug-3 albedo hack became debug

Cost: no GPU work of its own for the rig (constants); the canopy adds a
sqrt, an exp and a pow per foliage fragment. One reading per view (S mode,
1920x1080, 4x MSAA, scale 1.0, another lane's game on the machine, GPU
clock not logged): frame minimum on minus off -0.41 to +0.35 ms over nine
views with no consistent sign (the base build's own off and on, the same
frame twice, spread -0.13 to +0.23 ms), so the reading does not resolve a
cost. The full gate times it on a quiet GPU.

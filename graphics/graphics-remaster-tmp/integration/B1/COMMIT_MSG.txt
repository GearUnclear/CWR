graphics: trees and buildings cast soft, contact-hardening sun shadows

What the player sees: sun shadows are sharp where a caster meets the
ground and soften with the distance to what casts them. A soldier's leg
shadows start hard at his boots and blur toward their ends, a Jeep's
shadow keeps its wheels crisp and loses its far outline into the ground,
and the long evening shadows of trees run out soft instead of as leaf
cut-outs; under a high canopy the sun flecks on the forest floor become
soft ellipses. Tree crowns lose the stripes their own leaf cards printed
on them. The frame is not darker: mean brightness of the woods hero view
+1.4%, forest edge +0.6%, town, open field and the Sinai camp within
0.1%, the red/blue balance of the evening views unchanged.

How it works:
- Contact-hardening soft shadows (PCSS) in the shadows piece of the
  remastered prelude (pcssVisibility): a blocker search in the cascade
  array read as raw depth (shadowDepth, unit 6) gives the mean distance
  along the sun ray from receiver to blocker, and the filter radius is
  that distance times tan(sun angular radius), clamped between 0.6
  texels and the search radius (sunSize 1 deg, the search reaching 40 m
  along the ray). Without TAA the taps are a fixed per-pixel rotation of
  a 16-tap search (8 on the frustum tiers, where it spans 2 to 4 texels)
  and a 12, 24 or 32-tap filter; in a TAA frame 4 search and 4 filter
  taps turn every frame by the golden-ratio step of the TAA sequence and
  the history averages them. Sloped casters take a receiver-plane bias;
  the terrain, which never casts, takes none.
- The stock 12-tap filter is pcfVisibility, the old loop unchanged. The
  soft filter fades in from the gate's edge (sun 4 deg) to 6 deg, mixing
  the two, so a sunset sees no switch of filters.
- Leaves (foliage receivers, not grass) move their lookup leafBias (6)
  shadow-map texels toward the sun: their two-sided cards are in the
  map, and no normal offset keeps a card at a grazing sun off its own
  depth. The move is in texels, so it grows with the tier and a crown's
  self-shadow thins with distance, where it only flickered.
- With the treeShadows gate on, every static object casts into the sun
  shadow map whatever the legacy per-profile object-shadow toggle says
  (Scene::RenderShadowMapDepthPass through CastsIntoSunShadowMap); the
  Graphics shadow tier still turns the maps off. F10 already casts them
  on every profile with a shadow tier, so this part only shows where the
  old toggle was off.
- Pure rules and math in Graphics/Remaster/SunShadows.{hpp,cpp}: the
  penumbra conversion, tap counts, the gate-edge fade, the per-frame
  turn, the two LitConstants slots and the caster rule.
- LitConstants: 59 Taa (turn, TAA flag, taps per frame, tan of the sun
  radius; 0 = stock filter) and 60 Pcss (search reach, minimum filter
  radius, soft strength, leaf lift) land out of A7's padding
  litReserved[3], which shrinks to vec4 litReserved (58, the materials'
  MatClass, still to land; ReservedCount 1); Count stays 78.
- Unit 6 (kShadowRawUnit) holds the cascade array under a NEAREST,
  non-compare sampler: the depth stand-in from init on, rebound by every
  lit pass and by EnsureArrayTarget the moment it recreates the array
  (the F3 rule of unit 2); kLitSamplers gains the shadowDepth row.

Switches and gates: treeShadows and softShadows have landed (8/26), both
under the sun shadow gate (sun above 4 deg, or B3's moon key). Sub-knobs
under softShadows: sunSize 1.0 deg and shadowReach 40 m; under
treeShadows: leafBias 6 texels. Off (both 0) is the stock filter, the
stock casters and no lift, the look before this step. The overlay shows
the shadow section for treeShadows and the world section for
softShadows, whose taps run in the world pass.

From the prototype (tmp/research/shadows): kept pcssLit's blocker search
and Vogel filter, the 1 deg sun, the 40 m search and the 0.6 texel
contact radius, on the stock 2048 map. Rewritten: one PCSS with a TAA
path (4 + 4 taps, turned per frame) and a deterministic path, the
receiver-plane slope bias of the temporal prototype, the stock filter
kept as its own function, the slots and unit of plan 3.3 and 3.4.
Dropped: contact shadows and the far-field variant, bestlong, the
objShadows and casterLod knobs (F10 drives the casters), 4096 maps (the
Graphics shadow tier caps at High, there is no Ultra shadow tier), and
the tighter near tiers 0.04 / 0.15: with them the Sinai camp's right
foreground went into a solid shadow slab, with the stock filter as much
as with the soft one, and the cause was not found, so the plan's rule
applies (gate the tight tiers off if not understood). Not built: the
leaf transmittance layer (leafLight): the woods did not get darker.

Cost: one reading per view (light gate), RTX 4060 Ti, 1920x1080, render
scale 1.0, 4x MSAA, GPU clock not logged. Frame minimum, on minus off:
-0.08 to +0.31 ms over six views (woods hero +0.28, noon woods +0.31);
the smoothed averages read +0.23 to +3.08 ms, but the base build's own
off and on shots, the same frame twice, spread -3.88 to +2.83 ms on the
same views, so the cost is not resolved by this reading. The full gate
times it on a quiet GPU.

Known risks: the crowns keep less self-shadow (the sun visibility of the
hero crowns is mostly lit where it was striped; crown crops are 3%
brighter; in colour they keep their shaded side and texture, and B2's
crownCanopy owns crown shading). At 1 deg the sun flecks under a high
canopy are softer than at the real 0.53 deg (sunSize is the knob). The
Sinai slab follows the first tier's radius, not this step: a profile
whose shadow distance makes the stock first tier about 17 m may show it
(not tested). Before B3 the gate also opens for the moon key, where the
soft filter costs without showing.

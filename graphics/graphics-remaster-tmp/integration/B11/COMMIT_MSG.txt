graphics: terrain slopes show grass and rock grain instead of a smear

What the player sees: hillsides and meadows from about 10 m to 150 m out
show grass and rock grain instead of an airbrushed smear (every terrain
square is one 128 px texture over 50 m, so a texel covered 5 to 15
pixels there). On the forest edge at 18:15 the slope turns into dry
grass, the Eden meadow gains turf, the noon hillside and the Abel beach
get finer grain; the near path keeps its authored pebbles, and the near
gravel at lowsun_fp is near unchanged (+0.5%). Brightness only, relative
to the local mean: on the ground band the mean luma moves +0.04 to
+0.4%, R/G and G/B are unchanged to three decimals, the low-frequency
contrast (luma std after a 10 px blur) moves 0.0 to +0.7% (no new
mottling), and the sharpness proxy (mean absolute Laplacian) rises
+38.7% on the hero view, +17.0% at noon, +15.8% on the beach and +5.9%
on the meadow. The sea, the sky, objects, vehicles and trees are
untouched. With TAA off, a contrast-adaptive sharpen of brightness only
takes TAA's sharpen's place.

How it works: Graphics/Remaster/TerrainDetail (pure) holds the constants
and a C++ mirror of the shader math. The terrain main
(s_psDetailRemasterMain) gives each land fragment the luminance
high-pass of its own square's texture, zoomed six times and read within
a few metres (grass grain from grass, rock grain from rock): 2-texel (78
cm) cells, each cell's patch turned by its own random angle and offset
by its own jitter, the four cells around the fragment blended so the
grain keeps its variance (sum w h / sqrt(sum w^2)), through a soft
limiter at +-0.4, at strength 1.5. The zoomed sample takes the zoomed
derivatives, so it mip-maps away by itself once the base texture stops
being magnified; near the eye it fades in with the zoomed patch's mip
level (-3.5 to -2.5: 8 to 16 m on flat ground from a standing eye at
1080p), and everything fades out between 220 and 300 m, with nothing
evaluated beyond 300 m. The authored detail texture keeps its grain as
its mips average it out (2^lod, capped at 2x, back to 1 by 300 m), with
a rotated, rescaled second sample against tiling where it is minified.
LitConstants slot 77 (Detail) lands: xyz per frame (strength, kept rate,
anti-tiling weight; zero while the switch or its gate is off, which
leaves the stock terrain main), w per draw: the land and the sea are
both drawn by PSDetail (Landscape draws the sea with SpecularTexture,
not IsWater), so ApplyPassState flags each PSDetail draw from its
multitexture stage (SelectTerrainDetailLayer, next to SetTexture's stage
selection; the land carries DetailTexture) and uploads the one slot when
it changes. Slots 58 to 76 are one padding member (litPlanned) until
their features land. The luma sharpen is the Sharpen post stage run
alone: PostFrameInputs::lumaSharpen asks for the pass, the composite
then draws into the TAA input whenever Sharpen runs, and PostSharpen
runs psPostLumaSharpen (CAS 3x3, the sharpened luma onto the pixel's own
colour) into every sample of the frame target when the resolve did not
run.

The switches and their gates: terrainDetail (A1 registry, now landed,
GPU time in the "ground" section) with detailStrength 1.5 under it, and
sharpen (landed, section "sharpen") with sharpenStrength 0.3.
terrainDetail runs in the remastered world by day and night (off in
classic and under night vision), the 300 m range is in the shader.
sharpen runs only where TAA does not (A1's gate: TAA switched off, or
held off in a vehicle interior or under optics), and only in a post
pass. terrainDetail=0,sharpen=0 draws the frame of before: with TAA off
the B11 build's "remastered,taa=0, terrainDetail=0,sharpen=0" and the
base build's "remastered,taa=0" are identical to the pixel at the hero
view.

Kept, rewritten and dropped from the prototype (texsharp track, glsl7
rfinalr): kept the rotated self-detail (zoom 6, 4 cells, Heitz-Neyret
blend, limiter, textureGrad), strength 1.5, detail kept (K2 2) with the
anti-tiling sample (K1 1) and the luma-only CAS 0.3. Rewritten: into the
remastered terrain main with the real Detail slot instead of
litLightCount.yzw and litCamPos.w; the kept boost capped at 2x instead
of 4x (the critic's dark blotches on paths); the boost fades by 300 m
instead of 400 m (no step at the 300 m skip); the CAS in the Sharpen
stage after the composite instead of inside it, never with TAA's
sharpen; the sea left out by a per-draw flag. New after the first
in-game look: the near fade of the self-detail (it painted soft blotches
over the path at 2 to 8 m, where the zoomed patch is itself magnified)
and the anti-tiling sample only where the detail texture is minified (it
smeared the authored pebbles near the eye). Dropped: the remip, aniso
and LOD-bias knobs, texture stats, the release hook, the UD_TEX_*
variables, the Catmull-Rom and relief branches, the second octave, the
3-cell hex grid option (the 4-cell look is the approved one) and
StreamFix (identity at render scale 1.0, no visible change in the
research at 1.5, and a residency change outside the switch registry).

Measured cost (RTX 4060 Ti, 1920x1080, render scale 1.0, 4x MSAA, single
readings, not a quiet machine: other lanes' games ran beside it): the
ground section +0.29 ms on the forest edge and +0.37 ms at noon_fields
(medians of 3 interleaved rounds), +0.27 to +0.31 ms with TAA off at the
hero view in a separate game; the luma sharpen 0.05 ms. Frame totals
were noise in these rounds (the base build's inert switches read +1.7
and +3.3 ms).

Known risks: a golden slope reads more like dry straw (the grain is
brightness only, the hue is unchanged; owner taste); paths painted into
terrain textures get the grain from 10 m on (they cannot be told apart
before B7's terrain classes; roads are objects and never get it); in
motion the grain moves with the ground in cell-sized blobs:
frame-to-frame change over spatial detail 1.09 to 1.11x of off and the
second temporal difference 1.13 to 1.17x, no single-pixel sparkle, but
only a 10 m walk at 1080p was checked; the luma sharpen (TAA off only)
nearly doubles the sharpness proxy and touches the whole frame, and by
A1's gate it also runs by default in vehicle interiors and under optics
(where TAA holds off), views nobody has looked at yet; the
per-draw land flag relies on the landscape's DetailTexture and
SpecularTexture bits (a p3d face authored with the DetailTexture bit
would get the grain); no RTX 3060 was measured.

graphics: every lighting feature has a switch, a gate and an A/B capture

What the player sees: nothing yet. A1 builds the base the visible steps
land on; every feature switch it adds is inert until its own step reads
it, so no pixel changes. With --dev, the dev panel's Lighting tab now
lists every planned feature by area with its switch, the step that lands
it and its live frame gate ("active: sun above 4 deg", "gated: day").

How it works: one registry (Graphics/Remaster/FeatureSwitches) holds
every triSetLighting key: the existing knobs and the 26 feature switches
of plan section 3.1, floats in LightingTuning::features, all 1 by
default. The defaults stay the member initialisers; the table reads them
from LightingTuning{}. Item parsing moved there from
GameStateExtTestRender.cpp, so tests and the overlay share it. A new
mode word "baseline" turns every switch off (the look of the branch
cut); "remastered" turns them all on, "classic" is unchanged. The whole
Lighting tab is generated from the table and its copy-back line is now a
triSetLighting setting that round-trips. FeatureGates computes, once per
frame at the world's first 3D pass, whether each enabled feature runs
and at what resolution: from the sun and moon, the night effect, sky
cover, rain, fog, the lamps lit at night, night vision and last frame's
interior and optics flags (combat lights, wetness and the water share
keep stated stand-ins until B4, B7 and B6). gates=0 forces every enabled
feature on for debugging.

The switch and its gates: the switches are those of plan 3.1; the gates
follow 3.2, with the as-built differences recorded in the plan: night
vision gates every shading feature (the intensifier draws through the
classic programs), lamps count only at night (the light upload skips
them by day), lit clouds and cloud shadows need the sun or the moon key,
lamp streaks need wet weather, the combat shadow mask needs combat light.

Capture tooling (tests/rendering/captures): features_ab.test.sqf shoots
each feature's hero view with its keys off, then on, in one game, at
1080p, scale 1.0, 4x MSAA, with a fixed sim clock per view;
triFeatureState rides in every shot line as feat=; make_pairs.py builds
off | on pairs; banddiff.py compares images, run folders or the pairs of
one run per row band. triSetFixedStep fixes the World::Simulate step,
and triPinCloudDrift holds the remastered cloud shadows and the sky dome
clouds, which integrate per frame and so did not follow the pinned clock
(plan 7.2 said they did). A0's timing rig moved in (run-timing.ps1,
gpu_sampler.ps1, summarize_timing.py, gen_timing.py and the twenty
views; capRain and capEyeAbs are now in capture_lib.sqf). The video
skeleton: video/capture_motion.sqf (line and orbit paths, movers, clock
and time-lapse), video/gen_video_tests.py (every row of plan 7.3, S and
P modes, a paused-fire check) and video/make_video.py (side-by-side and
wipe MP4s, montage). triDevPanelLighting shows the Features section for
a screenshot.

Kept, rewritten and dropped from the prototypes: the per-prototype knob
rows, the moonlight=1.01 and saturation variant channels and the
debug=100+flags channel have no place in the registry; each B step adds
its own sub-knob rows under its switch.

Measured cost (RTX 4060 Ti, 1920x1080, scale 1.0, 4x MSAA, 3 interleaved
rounds, no other tri slot holder or PoseidonGame; an idle Arma 3 process
quit 50 s into the run; an earlier run while it was busy read about 60%
slower on Abel's long views and was set aside): the A0 views match A0 at P0
(sunrise_ridge 17.31 against 17.41 ms, valley 05:15 23.76 against 23.30,
Eden broken_high 24.65 against 24.31, overcast_broken 12.02 against
11.85, bay_offshore 7.39 against 7.73; CPU frame within 0.7 ms). The
gates read off at the noon references (14 of 26 at noon_fields and
sea_noon).

Tests: test_feature_switches.cpp (22 cases: every switch has a key, a
label and a default of 1, baseline zeroes them and remastered restores
them, the summary round-trips, one case per gate row of plan 3.2 and
gates=0) and five new test_tri_capture_verbs.cpp cases
(baseline/remastered, every key as <key>=0, triFeatureState,
triSetFixedStep, triPinCloudDrift). Gate tags plus the new ones: 452/452.
Breaking baseline, a sun shadow threshold, the fixed step or the cloud
pin turns 8 cases red.

In game (tmp/integration/A1 of the graphics-remaster worktree): smoke 3/3
(KHR_debug ids 131169, 131154 and 131218 only); features_ab
20261018-015544-ab-A1 against the A0 build's 20261018-012542-ab-A0build-
fixedclock: horizon and ground bands within the same-config noise except
clouds_coast_oc_up (its cloud shadows are held in A1 and drift on A0),
sky band apart by the sky clouds' position (held at 0 in A1; two A1
boots match to the pixel); capture set 20261018-015545-set against A0's
gr-base: horizon and ground bands within A0's boot-to-boot noise apart
from the sea and the overcast views, where Classic, which A1 does not
touch, differs the same way; paused P-mode frames around a fire are
pixel-identical, running ones are not; timing 20261018-014500-timing.

Known risks: posed soldiers turn their heads between S-mode passes; the
SSR gate stays on until B6 measures the water and wet share; a pre-A1
build cannot hold the clouds, so its sky band never matches; every video
frame writes a 6 MB BMP twin until run-captures deletes them (about 3 GB
per 240-frame clip); no RTX 3060 was measured.

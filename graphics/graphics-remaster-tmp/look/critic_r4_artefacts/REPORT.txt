  Mean luma 35.5 (round 3 54.3, landed 63.2, vanilla 89.7). The soldier and the jeep at 30 m

  Cause, confirmed by a probe: the sun fixer's near-sun sky cap (kSkySunCap 10, past white, via
  camSkyRolloff(lin, d)) feeds the camera meter's guards. TargetExposureLog2 (PhotoCamera.cpp
  298-334) pulls exposure toward kHighlightLimit on the kMeterHighlight percentile of the
  blocks' brightest taps and toward kSkyTopLimit on the sky's bright end, so the glow by a sun
  at or just past the frame edge (top right of woods_pine2_evening, seen through crowns) now
  reads several times brighter and the guards drop the whole frame.
  Probe: the merged build with only psRemasterCommon's kCamSkySunCap set back to 2.4 (shader
  override, nothing else changed): woods_pine2_evening ev 2.27, mean luma 61.4.
  The same cause darkens the other into-the-sun views, by less (r3 -> r4 ev):
  lowsun_fp -0.02 -> -0.71, sinai_lowsun_fp 0.53 -> -0.29, pine_rays 1.25 -> 0.63,
  dawn_valley 0.03 -> -0.29, sunrise_ridge (remastered cut) 2.28 -> 1.78. The sun fixer's own
  build already showed it (lowsun_fp -0.03 -> -0.70, pine_rays 1.17 -> 0.83); it did not

  is a blocker. pine_rays with the probe: ev 0.73, so most of its drop has another source
  (the in-frame sun ring guard and the brighter shafts).
  Root fix: the meter must not treat the sky's sun glow (which the sun fixer meant to go past
  white) as a highlight to protect. Meter the sky at the plain roll-off cap (kSkyRolloffCap),
  or leave the glow's extra light out of the highlight and sky-top readings, or exclude blocks
  within the glow radius of the sun whether the sun is in frame or just outside it.
  Evidence: woods_exposure_probe.png (r4 merged | r4 + cap 2.4 probe | round 3 | landed),
  woods_pine2_evening_r3r4.png (r4 | r3 | vanilla | landed),

AI LINK REVIEW (look-director source)
-------------------------------------

Read: VolumetricFog.hpp 383-435 and VolumetricFog.cpp 208-292 (ComputeFogMedium) and 410-548
  (sight state, BuildFogSightMap, UpdateFogSightMedium, FogSightTransmittance*, drawn medium);
  TerrainLighting.cpp 445-530 and .hpp 25-46 (WorldFogInputs, SimulateFogSight,
  ShutdownFogSight); WorldSetup.cpp 1247-1327 (SimulateLandscape) and 324 (teardown);
  World.cpp 682 and 1729; WorldInit.cpp 103 and WorldSetup.cpp 162 (BACKGROUND_AI);
  Target.cpp 636-646; EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp 300-345 and 520-545; test_volumetric_fog.cpp
  311-503.

Same inputs and function as the drawn fog: yes. AI: ComputeFogMedium(WorldFogInputs(land),
  GEngine tuning or defaults, true). Renderer: ComputeFogMedium(WorldFogInputs(land) plus its own
  aridity, _lighting, on). The AI terms (groundSigma, groundHeight, mistSigma, mistLayer,
  mistFall, mistNoise) read fog, rain, skyThrough, sun elevation and azimuth, night, the date,
  fogDensity and fogMist; not aridity, viewDistance or the fog range, so the renderer's extras
  cannot split them. The renderer logs a rate-limited warning if the drawn mist or ground air
  ever differs from AI's.

Render path independence: yes, structurally. The only SetFogSight callers left are
  UpdateFogSightMedium and ShutdownFogSight (TerrainLighting.cpp); no renderer file calls it.
  The sun comes from LightSun::Recalculate inside SimulateLandscape, the weather from the same
  function, and SimulateFogSight runs at its end (WorldSetup.cpp 1326), before PerformAI
  (World.cpp 682 before 1729). Post effects off, the SSAA path, an interior view, fog quality
  and a missing renderer do not enter it. Target.cpp 636-646 is unchanged since round 3: vis is
  multiplied by FogSightTransmittance(eye, aim) while FogSightActive().

Deterministic: from synced weather, date and heights (ClippedData); only the per-machine knobs
  fogDensity and fogMist differ (defaults 1 on a server), as the fixer noted.

Thread safety: safe today. BACKGROUND_AI is 0, so PerformAI, the simulation step and the
  renderer all run on the main thread; the sight state and s_sight are only written there; the
  map is an immutable shared_ptr<const>. FogSightMedium() returns a reference into the static,
  which would race if AI or the renderer ever moved to another thread.

Cost of the link: SampleHeights hashes about 64x64 heights per step (negligible); the sight map
  build (about 150 ms on Eden) now runs on the main thread at island load.

Tests that pin it: 376 (AI medium equals the drawn medium across fogDensity 0.5/1/2 and fogMist
  0/0.5/1, and transmittance equals the march through it), 421 (post effects off, an interior
  gate and render-only knobs leave AI transmittance bit-identical), 463 (a renderer-free map
  equals the horizon map's heights and floor; base-engine tuning keeps sight active; no island
  gives classic sight), plus 311. Gaps: these test pure helpers. Case 421 cannot fail, because
  UpdateFogSightMedium never receives gate state. Nothing pins that the renderer does not call
  SetFogSight, that SimulateFogSight runs before PerformAI, or what WorldFogInputs reads. A
  source contract test (like the shader pins) would close that.

Owner call (fixer's note, kept): in classic lighting or with volFog off the AI still takes the
  mist while the screen draws none. Every machine's AI stays the same, but in those modes AI no
  longer follows the drawn fog. Separately, the mist fixer drew a day's setFog at 0.75 of the
  classic extinction while AI's weather part stays the classic tactical fog, so at the AI's
  tactical end about a fifth of a surface is still drawn.


COST
----

Run: run-timing.ps1 from look-director's engine root, game dist/look-director-rwdi, junction
  RemasterUD-look-director-timing, round 3's split tests renamed crit4t_* (same views, clocks,
  shot order; tip = remastered,photoLight=0,surfaces=0,volFog=0, overhaul = remastered, two
  interleaved rounds each). Four background runs, checked at least every 3 minutes.
  Raw data: timing/<part>-timing/, table timing/cost_r4_table.txt, script timing/cost_r4.py.

The machine was NOT quiet: 170 of 179 GPU samples had a foreign PoseidonGame (up to 4 during
  the first part: look-puddle-*, look_shine, look_probe, van_* runs). Round 3 had 0 of 142.
  Mean frame times are inflated up to 3x in the first part, so the raw mean delta (+1.94 ms
  against round 3's +1.45 ms over 21 views) is not a cost result.

Usable estimate: each shot's minimum GPU frame, keeping the 14 views whose tip minimum matched
  round 3 within 0.5 ms. On those views the overhaul delta is +1.56 ms against round 3's
  +1.54 ms, and the overhaul's minimum frame averages 10.46 ms against 10.37 ms. Per view the
  change is within 0.2 ms, except sinai_desert_noon (+3.59 against +1.70), whose two overhaul
  samples differ by 3 ms within one run with a foreign game present (noise). The seven
  contended views (noon_fields, lowsun_fp, forest_edge_golden, sunrise_ridge,
  woods_pine2_evening, dawn_valley, town_fog_night) cannot be compared.
Static check: the five round 4 patches add no texture reads, no draw calls and no passes; only
  ALU in the sky, disc, deck, fireball and twilight code, plus a per-step height hash on the CPU.
Conclusion: no measurable cost growth over rounds 2 and 3 (round 2 +1.40 ms, round 3 +1.45 ms
  mean); a quiet-machine re-time is still needed for a formal number.

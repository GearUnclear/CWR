(a) The burst cloud: cause, proven
----------------------------------
Probe 1 (probe_1/, round 3 code, paused frames at fixed ages, with and without combatSmoke=0):
the cloud is white at 6 frames, lemon at 8, flat mustard (160,131,73) at 10, then pink-brown.
combatSmoke=0 gives the same picture at every age, so the combat light on the cloud is not what
colours it (the merger's and critics' guess). The cloud is exactly the colour of the config table
of CfgCloudlets Explosion (a temperature table: 2500 K at birth, white at 2200, lemon 1900,
mustard 1600, orange 1400).
Why: the fireball puffs' shape is self-lit (MSShining). Object::DrawDecal draws a self-lit decal
as its table colour times the eye's accommodation, and lights nothing on it. The photoreal camera
holds the accommodation at 1, so the fireball shows its table colour at the level of a white wall,
never past it: no hot core, and one flat colour because all puffs have about the same temperature.
Classic and landed whitened it by clipping each channel. A first fix that went through the lit
branch only did nothing (shots_1, shots_2): proof that the puffs take the self-lit branch.

The change:
- FireballPuff (CombatLights): a puff whose table tops out between 1500 and 3000 is a fireball's,
  read in kelvin. It glows as a black body (BlackBodyRgb, a fit to the sRGB black-body table) at
  kFireballEmission times exp(kFireballWienK (1/2200 - 1/T)), Wien's law at 580 nm, faded in from
  900 K to 1300 K, each channel softly saturated at kFireballEmitCap (so the hottest core goes
  white-yellow). What it scatters is soot (0.24, 0.21, 0.18). Camp fire smoke and smoke shells
  (tables to 1000) and gun fire (its own scale to 4200) are not touched.
- Object::DrawDecal: a fireball puff under the combat lights goes through the lit branch (scene
  light on its soot, combat light, then its glow added in linear and carried past 1 as the decal
  glow), whatever its shape's material. Cloudlet gets Temperature() and HottestTemperature().
- CombatSmokeTransmittance: a shell burst's light now crosses its own cloud like a fire's, so it
  lights the cloud's base and near side, not every puff alike.
Result (a_burst_ages.png): 6 frames a yellow-white fireball; 8 frames an orange fireball with dark
puffs in it; 10 frames dark grey-brown smoke with a few glowing bits; then dark smoke. In
firefight_3 the burst is a dark cloud; firefight_2 in this run caught the burst at about 0.37 s,
dark brown smoke with a lit base.

(b) The band along the horizon
------------------------------
Cause: under the camera a burst's surface light reached 20 m. Seen from 2 m at 60 to 90 m a lit
disc 40 m wide is a 9 px strip, so a young burst drew a ruler-straight line brighter than its own
cloud (probe_1 f06, b_band_probe_f06.png top).
Change (EngineGL33_PostCombat.cpp): a shell burst's surface light ends kBurstSurfaceReachCores (3)
ball radii out (7.8 m for a heavy shell, under its cloud). Cannon flashes keep 20 m.
Result: horizon rows 338 to 352 beside a 1 frame old burst, 99th percentile 60 -> 32 (ground 28);
8 frames: 56 -> 33. The band is gone at full size; the fireball is now the brightest thing.

(c) The wreck's warm line
-------------------------
Cause (round 3 horizon notes, confirmed): at 19:35 the camera meters the dusk plain about as dark
as a night field, so the wreck's fire at the day scale 0.12 lit the plain behind it like a night
fire. Change: CombatTwilight(sun elevation), under the camera only: a steady light (fire, flare,
motor) takes 1 from 6 degrees under (the night as before), tenfold less per 2.5 degrees above
that, floor 0.1. It scales only their light on surfaces and in the air (GatherCombatLights); their
flames and smoke keep it all. At 1.7 degrees under the wreck's ground light is 0.1 of round 3's.
Result: rows 338 to 352 at the wreck, 99th percentile 48 -> 31 (probe), and the line is gone in
c_wreck_firefight_1.png. Night views are unchanged (town_night grass (68,55,35) -> (71,56,35)).

(d) Cream flame core and stones
-------------------------------
Cause: the composite's hue-keeping curve mixes all three channels toward white by one amount, so a
bright orange (1, 0.55, 0.16) went to (1, 0.68, 0.38), cream. Change: each channel whitens by the
whitening to the power 1 + kHueRoll (1 - its share of the peak), kHueRoll 3, in the composite and
in the C++ mirror (which now also has round 2's chroma keep). A white light is unchanged.
town_night 1080 (before -> after): flame core (239,199,150) -> (239,188,108); stones
(199,159,102) -> (204,159,87), top tenth (254,225,194) -> (254,206,130); fire box pixels with
every channel at 200 or more 171 -> 0; plume 1 to 2 m up (173,135,101) -> (173,134,100).
town_fog_night: core (222,184,130) -> (226,179,123), stones top tenth (246,209,158) ->
(236,188,124), white pixels 597 -> 0.

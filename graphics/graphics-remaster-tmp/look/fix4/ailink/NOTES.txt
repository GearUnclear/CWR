AILINK fixer, round 4: AI sight and the fog, made a part of the simulation
==========================================================================

Scratch copy: D:/Arma_CWA_worktrees/look-fix-seam (base tree d2f866e4, fec1075 + fix4/base.patch).
Build build/look-fix-seam-rwdi, game dist/look-fix-seam-rwdi, junction RemasterUD-r4-ailink.
Patch: fix.patch (12 files, no probe tests). Nothing committed.

Cause
-----
AI sight followed the renderer. Only EngineGL33::UpdateFogMedium called SetFogSight, and it passed
nullptr whenever this frame's fog did not draw (post effects off, SSAA off, fog unavailable, a vehicle
interior view). A dedicated server or headless process never called it, so AI there ignored the mist.
The sight map was also built inside the fog stage (UpdateFogFloor) from the terrain horizon map, so it
only existed after the fog first drew and only while terrainShadows or terrainOcclusion was above 0.

Probe (proof): look_probe_ailink.test.sqf, Eden 06:10, overcast 0.7, fog 0.3, new command triFogSight.
Before the fix (probe_before/):
  post effects on:  drawn=1 mist 1.950e-03 ground 7.268e-04, ai=1 with the same values.
  post effects off: drawn=0, ai=0, mist 0, ground 0. The weather still holds the mist; AI ignored it.
After the fix (probe_after/):
  post effects off: drawn=0, ai=1, mist 1.952e-03 ground 7.277e-04, the same air as with the fog drawn.
  fogMist=0.5: AI mist 9.770e-04 = drawn 9.770e-04. fogDensity=2: AI 3.903e-03 = drawn 3.903e-03.
  Every drawn state: Tai equals Tdrawn to 4 digits.
The probe eye stands on a hill at 273 m, so the 300 m line stays above most of the mist
(T about 0.999). The flag and the medium values are the proof; the unit tests use a valley line
where T is well under 0.9.
The tank interior step did not trigger the interior gate in this probe (volFog stayed on), so the
interior case is pinned by unit test only.

Change
------
1. VolumetricFog.hpp/.cpp (pure):
   BuildFogSightMap(heights, range, grid, maxSize=1024): the sight map straight from the island's
   heights, downsampled exactly as the terrain horizon map samples them, plus BuildFogFloor and
   BuildFogLayer. UpdateFogSightMedium(in, tuning, map): AI takes ComputeFogMedium(in, tuning, true).
   FogSightTransmittanceThrough(medium, map, a, b): the pure march (FogSightTransmittance wraps it).
   FogSightMedium(), FogSightMapInUse(): what AI uses now. SetDrawnFogMedium/GetDrawnFogMedium: the
   renderer publishes what it drew, for probes only; AI never reads it.
2. TerrainHorizon.hpp/.cpp: DownsampleTerrainGrid exposes the existing box downsample.
3. World/Terrain/TerrainLighting.hpp/.cpp:
   WorldFogInputs(land): the fog inputs from the world (sun from LightSun, night, skyThrough, setFog,
   rain, view distance, classic fog range, date). The renderer and the simulation both call it.
   SimulateFogSight(land): once per simulation step. When the heights change (island load) it builds
   the sight map at once on the main thread (Eden 1024x1024: about 150 ms), whatever the render
   settings. Each step it computes the medium from WorldFogInputs and GEngine->GetLightingTuning()
   (the base Engine's defaults without a renderer). ShutdownFogSight() on world teardown.
4. WorldSetup.cpp: SimulateFogSight(GLOB_LAND) at the end of World::SimulateLandscape (after the clock,
   the sun and the weather moved, before PerformAI in the same frame). ShutdownFogSight in ~World.
5. EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp: the renderer no longer calls SetFogSight. It fills its medium from
   WorldFogInputs (plus its own aridity, which only sets the dust that AI does not take). The fog floor
   texture is drawn from AI sight's own map (FogSightMapInUse), so the drawn mist lies where AI finds
   it, from the first frame and with terrain shadows off. The aridity still comes from the terrain
   horizon map's ground tints. A guard logs a warning (at most every 600 frames) if the drawn mist or
   humid air ever differs from AI sight's, so a second source cannot creep in unnoticed.
6. triFogSight [[e,n,agl],[e,n,agl]] (GameStateExtTestRender.cpp): AI sight's T and medium next to the
   drawn one and the classic tactical fog, for probes.

Target.cpp (the link itself) is unchanged: vis *= FogSightTransmittance while FogSightActive().

Constants touched
-----------------
None. No fog look constant changed (MIST owns those). The drawn fog is unchanged: dawn_valley after vs
round-3 final, mean abs difference 0.75 of 255, 99th percentile 7 (frame noise).

Multiplayer
-----------
AI sight now reads only synced simulation state: weather (setFog, overcast, rain), the date and time,
the sun, and the island's heights. A server and its clients compute the same medium. Two per-machine
inputs remain: the tuning knobs fogDensity and fogMist (debug knobs, default 1; a server uses 1), and
skyThrough if it ever drifts per machine. For strict MP parity the server's knob values would need to
be synced or the knobs ignored for AI on clients.

Owner call (open)
-----------------
AI keeps the mist in classic lighting mode or with the volFog switch off, where the mist is not drawn.
This keeps every machine's AI the same. If the owner wants AI to drop the mist in classic mode, gate
UpdateFogSightMedium on the lighting setting (not on per-frame render state).

Tests
-----
PoseidonTests "[fog]": all passed (6105 assertions in 24 test cases), "[remaster]" all passed.
New cases in test_volumetric_fog.cpp:
  - AI sight equals the drawn fog's air for the same inputs and knobs (fogDensity 0.5/1/2 x fogMist
    0/0.5/1): AI medium fields equal ComputeFogMedium(in, t, true), FogSightTransmittance equals the
    march through the renderer's medium; the knobs move AI the same way.
  - AI sight does not follow the render path: post effects off and an interior view turn the volFog
    gate off, AI sight stays bit-identical; fogQuality, fogSun, fogLights change nothing.
  - AI sight works without a renderer: the map from raw heights equals the horizon map's heights
    and fog floor, the base engine's classic tuning still gives active sight, no island gives classic.

NARRATION (female voice, calm; one line per scene)
Intro        Uslu dur is a total overhaul of Arma: Cold War Assault. This first video is about the light.
1 Opening    Malden, under occupation. The models are the originals; the light is new.
2 Dawn       A resistance squad moves out at first light, as the sun burns through the valley mist.
3 Pines      In the pine woods, guerrillas keep to cover as morning sun falls through the mist in shafts.
4 Bay        On the bay, an occupier patrol boat rides calm water that now mirrors the hills and sky.
5 Outpost    At an occupier outpost, a low winter sun rims the armour and leaves the shade cool and deep.
6 Everon     From the air, sunlit clouds drag their shadows slowly over the hills of Everon.
7 Sinai      On modded islands like Sinai, a camp hides under its netting from a hard late-afternoon sun.
8 Ambush     At golden hour, guerrillas wait at the forest edge as long shadows fall down the slope.
9 Convoy     An occupier convoy rolls in at sunset, its long shadows reaching down the road.
10 Checkpoint At dusk, checkpoint sentries gather by the fire as the last light fades.
11 Patrol    At night, lamps and a fire light the occupied town as a patrol sweeps the street.
12 Rain      When the rain comes, puddles catch the lamps, and the resistance slips past the patrol.
13 Supply    Out in the country, a supply run drives through a moonless night by headlight and starlight.
14 Firefight And when a firefight breaks out, every flare, fire and shell burst lights the field.
Outro        Uslu dur. Part two covers the gameplay: Guerrilla Mode and the AI.

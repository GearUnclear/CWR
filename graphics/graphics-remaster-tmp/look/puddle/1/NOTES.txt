APPROACH (the model, after Lagarde 2013 "Water drop 3a/3b" and Lekner and Dorf 1988)
Rain on ground goes through stages: water fills the pores and the fine grain (the surface darkens,
its lobe narrows a little), a film forms in the relief, and where the water is deeper than the
relief it is a flat layer (a puddle). On a drained surface in rain the film is a few tenths of a
millimetre (Gallaway 1979), thinner than the relief of asphalt (mean texture depth about 1 mm),
gravel or soil, so the reflecting facets keep the relief's slopes: a broad lobe, no image. Only
a puddle is optically flat. So:
- Outside puddles: Lekner-Dorf darkening on the soaked share of the face, a water-Fresnel lobe at
  the relief's roughness (a broad dim glint near a lamp or the fire, nothing recognisable), and
  its sky as the sky light around the reflected direction (an irradiance, not an image). No
  mirror term and no screen-space reflection.
- Puddles: level water, F0 0.02, roughness 0.05 in the forward pass (lamp mirror) and 0.02 in the
  trace, rain rings near the eye, the ground under the water darkened again by Lekner-Dorf.

THE CHANGE (shaders only; no C++ engine, binding or uniform change)
EngineGL33_LightingShadersSurface.cpp, wetSurface: the ground (land, grass clutter, roads) has its
own branch; objects keep exactly their old maths (same film, roughness, sun, lamps, sky).
- Darkening: a *= mix(1, wetLeknerDorf(a), soakG) * damp margin * mix(1, kWetPuddleDark, puddle),
  wetLeknerDorf(A) = (1 - r_e)(1 - r_i) / (1 - r_i A) per channel (about 0.53 for the land's
  albedos; brighter channels keep a little more, so the colour deepens without the old mustard).
  soakG = mix(mix(kWetSoakDense, 1, porosity of bare ground), kWetSoakGrass, veg) * w.
- Wet face lobe: water's Fresnel (wetLobe's 0.02) at roughG = mix(mix(kWetRoughBare,
  kWetRoughRoad, road), kWetRoughGrass, veg), on filmG = w * mix(1, kWetSoakGrass, veg) *
  (1 - puddle), for the sun and every lamp, fire and headlight.
- Its sky: surfaceSkyIrradiance(rG) with rG the reflected direction drawn toward the normal by
  roughG^2, times the split-sum Fresnel at roughG, in the forward pass whether SSR runs or not.
- Puddle: its own level normal (rain rings), its own lamp and sun lobes, its sky only while SSR
  is off (as before).
- wetPuddle (shared by both passes, so they still agree pixel for pixel): saturated bare ground
  ponds as a road does: th = mix(mix(kPuddleGround, kPuddleRoad, max(road, level)), kPuddleGrass,
  veg * veg) + kPuddleLevelShift * (1 - level). Reason: once the rain has saturated it (puddle level
  1) trodden soil infiltrates only at its saturated conductivity, a few mm/h, below a heavy rain's,
  so it ponds like pavement; grass keeps its root channels and threshold. At level 0 nothing changes.
  This is what makes the square read wet at night now that the sheen is gone (more mirrors).
EngineGL33_PostSsrShaders.cpp, trace, only while wetWeather shades the ground (B6's own path with
wetWeather off is untouched):
- a pixel with no puddle returns at once (add 0, m 1: no reflection, and no march, so cheaper);
- weight = puddle (the pixel's share of water), rough 0.02, cone 0.008, normal level plus ripples.

CONSTANTS (old -> new), each from physical reasoning
- kWaterRe 0.066 (new): water's diffuse external reflectance, Fresnel at n 1.333 integrated over
  the hemisphere (computed: 0.0664). kWaterRi 0.475 (new): internal, 1 - (1 - r_e)/n^2 (0.4746).
  F0 0.02 = ((n - 1)/(n + 1))^2, unchanged in wetLobe and the trace.
- kWetGroundDarken 0.35 x porosity (soil kept 0.69, road 0.88) -> Lekner-Dorf on the soaked share:
  soil keeps about 0.55, asphalt about 0.6, gravel about 0.62, grass about 0.93.
- kWetSoakDense 0.75 (new): rock, gravel and asphalt are darkened on the share of their face the
  water wets (the film in the relief; stone tops drain); porous soil goes to 1 by its porosity.
- kWetSoakGrass 0.15 (new): waxy blades shed water as beads covering about a sixth of them (also
  the grass's film share). Replaces kWetGrassPorosity 0.23 and kWetGrassFilm 0.1.
- Wet face roughness, old kWetFilmRough 0.12 (GGX a 0.014) / kWetGrassRough 0.4 -> the relief's:
  kWetRoughRoad 0.65 (asphalt aggregate, rms slope about 0.3, a 0.42), kWetRoughBare 0.8 (clods and
  stones, slope about 0.45, a 0.64), kWetRoughGrass 0.95 (blades at all angles, a 0.9).
  kWetFilmRough 0.12 stays for objects.
- kWetSunGroundRough 0.5 and kWetSunGroundFilm 0.5: removed (the relief roughness is above both).
- kWetPuddleDark 0.55 unchanged: it is Lekner-Dorf's ratio at albedo 0.2 (0.542), now tested so.
- kWetPuddleRough 0.05 unchanged (still water; the rings carry the rain).
- Trace with wetWeather: weight 0.5 x wet x (1 - 0.85 grass) / 0.65 x wet -> puddle; roughness
  0.30..0.75 -> 0.02; cone 0.10..0.22 -> 0.008 (both water's).
- Puddle threshold on bare land at full level: kPuddleGround 0.67 -> kPuddleRoad 0.61 (via level);
  constants themselves unchanged (they still equal B6's, as the test pins).

WEAK POINTS (honest)
- Night readability: with the sheen gone the wet square reads by darker ground and by puddles,
  and a puddle that mirrors only the dark sky or the fog horizon has little contrast against the
  dark wet ground. seam_b in particular reads more "dark and damp" than "pouring" at thumbnail
  size; round 3's look leaned on the sheen for that. The night sky itself is near black (a real
  overcast town sky glows), which is outside this change.
- More puddles: the saturation-ponding rule lowers the bare ground's threshold to the road's at
  full puddle level; on the seam_day square the left puddles are now larger. The owner liked the
  old puddles; if this is too much, drop max(road, level) back to road (one token) and the rest
  of the model still holds.
- Darker wet ground (Lekner-Dorf is stronger than the old 0.35 x porosity on roads and gravel):
  physically right, but the night square is darker overall; not compared against a photo.
- A broad glint remains near close lamps and the fire (by design), weakest on grass.
- The wet face's sky uses the irradiance as a prefilter (accurate for a lobe this wide, cosine-
  weighted); at grazing angles on roads it adds up to a few percent of the sky, which may read as
  a faint lift on the far road in daylight.
- Puddle mirrors keep the trace's existing limits (half resolution, horizontal blur; the mirrored
  white house shows some vertical banding). Vertical straight lines inside puddles are mirrored
  building edges, not seams.
- Not measured: cost (the trace now skips non-puddle ground entirely, the forward pass adds one SH
  sky lookup per wet ground pixel), motion, and the case before the ground field is built.
- AI sight and fog link untouched (no change to Target.cpp, VolumetricFog.cpp or any C++).

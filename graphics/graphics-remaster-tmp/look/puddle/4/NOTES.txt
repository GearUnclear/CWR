APPROACH
Audit every term that brightens or reflects the ground in the rain, measure each one's share by
turning it off alone, then cut exactly the ones that make non-puddle ground look reflective.
Probe mechanism (work/make_probe.py, work/glsl_probe): a shader override keyed on the forced
wetness knob, wetness = 1 - p/1000 (litWeather.x, also visible in the SSR trace), so one game shoots
every probe in a row ("remastered,wetness=0.997" and so on). The world's wetness in these views is
1.00 (feat= line, wet=1.00), and the unforced frame matches wetness=1 within noise (town_night_rain
lower half mean 51.7 vs 51.9 of 255), so forcing it changes nothing.
Probes: p1 film lamp streaks off, p2 film sun/moon glint off, p3 SSR trace on non-puddle ground off
(weight = puddle), p4 the surface lane's specular on the ground off (sp.sun, sp.sky,
g_physicalSpec), p5 every puddle term off, p6 all non-puddle reflective terms off, p7 the forward
sky reflection (env) on the ground off, p8 the puddle mask; plus knobs camBloom=0, fogLights=0 and
the SSR reflection-only view (debug=10). Shares are linear luminance, ref minus off over ref, on
non-puddle pixels (mask from p8) in the two regions the earlier sheets use (tree base 780,610 and
open street 330,790, 320x180; seam_b and seam_day their own). Raw numbers: audit_numbers.txt.

Reading: the film's lamp streaks are the reflection on non-puddle ground: a third to over half of
its light at night, the three long vertical streaks (lamps and fire) that run across dry-looking
ground in every round-3 night shot. Everything else is within the shots' noise (smoke and rain
particles differ per shot; a few percent either way). The SSR trace on non-puddle ground is small
in these regions but visible on the road to the right in town_night_rain (heat panel): it mirrors
the houses there. The forward sky env only runs while SSR is off, so it is 0 at night; by day it
is a few percent. The "lights in the fog" and bloom rows are negative because the photoreal camera
meters the frame: taking light out of the air raises the exposure for everything, so those two
cannot be read as ground terms (they are halos in the air, not on the ground).

THE CHANGE (shaders only; no C++ logic, no binding, no knob)
1. Lamp streaks (EngineGL33_LightingShadersSurface.cpp wetSurface): on the ground outside a
   puddle the film's lamp lobe takes the ground's glint roughness and share, as the sun glint
   already did: wetLampStreaks(nf, v, max(rough, kWetSunGroundRough)) times film x
   kWetSunGroundFilm. Puddles keep their sharp kWetPuddleRough streak. Objects (walls, hulls,
   paint) keep their own film and streaks.
2. SSR trace (EngineGL33_PostSsrShaders.cpp): weight = wetWeather ? puddle : <B6's old mix>. With
   the wet weather on, only standing water mirrors; the B6-only path (wet weather off) is unchanged.
3. Forward sky env on the ground (SSR off): the reflection is the puddle's alone
   (F x pud.x, at kWetPuddleRough) instead of the whole film's.
4. Darker, deeper: the ground's film darkens it outside its puddles,
   a *= 1 - kWetGroundFilmDark x film x (1 - pud.x). Asphalt and gravel, which soak up little and
   used to show their wetness as shine, now show it as depth.
5. Puddles carry the rain's look, so roads and bare ground hold a little more water:
   th = mix(mix(kPuddleGround, kPuddleRoad, road) - kPuddleMore, kPuddleGrass, veg * veg) + ...
   Grass does not get more. It is in the shared puddle field, so the forward pass and the trace
   still agree pixel for pixel, and it is a pure function of the position and the ground field: no
   seam can come from it.
Left alone on purpose: the surface lane's own specular (1%, broad, not wet-related), bloom and fog
glow (air, not ground), the sun glint (already rough; only its constants change, see below).
AI sight and fog: untouched (Target.cpp and VolumetricFog.cpp not in the diff).

CONSTANTS old -> new
- kWetSunGroundRough 0.5 -> 0.6 (now also the ground's lamp glint roughness)
- kWetSunGroundFilm 0.5 -> 0.3 (now also the ground's lamp glint share)
- kWetGroundFilmDark new, 0.25
- kPuddleMore new, 0.04 (roads 0.61 -> 0.57, bare ground 0.67 -> 0.63 effective; grass 0.74)
- SSR non-puddle weight with the wet weather on: 0.5 x wet x (1 - 0.85 grass) land, 0.65 x wet
  roads -> 0
- ground lamp streak roughness 0.12 (kWetFilmRough) -> 0.6; share film -> 0.3 x film
- ground forward sky env share: max(film, puddle) -> puddle
Unchanged: kPuddleRoad, kPuddleGround (still pinned to B6's kSsrPuddle*), kWetFilmRough (objects),
kWetPuddleRough, kWetPuddleDark, the ground field and every round-3 seam piece.
Variants tried (shots_var1, one game, same selector trick, work/make_variants.py): threshold -0.03
and -0.06 on every class (-0.06 put too much water on Eden's grass; hence 0.04 and no grass),
no ground glint at all (seam_b loses the warm sheen under the fire; kept the dim glint), no film
darkening (ground reads lighter and drier).

GGX numbers (the new unit test's mirror of wetLobe; lamp 5 m up, eye 1.75 m up 15 m away, gravel
film 0.775): puddle streak peak 0.051, 0.48 m long x 0.08 m wide; the old film streak 0.044,
1.1 m x 0.13 m (nearly a puddle's: that is the "reflection on non-puddle ground"); the new ground
glint 0.001 (2% of the puddle's), 7.2 m x 1.8 m (a broad dim patch, no image of the lamp).

WEAK POINTS (honest)
- At night the scene reads less obviously rainy than round 3. With the sheen and streaks gone the
  rain is carried by the puddles alone, and a puddle mirrors only what its reflected ray finds: near
  the eye that is the black night sky, so near puddles are dark patches, and lamp or fire streaks
  appear only where a puddle happens to lie on the line to the light. In town_night_rain the
  puddles mirror the facades and the lamp (mid square, road on the right, the big patch lower
  left); seam_b and seam_d show few reflections because few puddles lie in view. The fire has no
  reflection in seam_b/seam_d any more: it stood on non-puddle ground. kPuddleMore 0.04 helps but
  is a trade against "too many puddles"; it is the knob to turn if the owner wants more.
- The rain particles themselves are faint in these frames; nothing in this change touches them.
- The probe shares are per region and noisy (smoke and particles differ per shot); the bloom and
  fog glow rows are confounded by the camera's metering.
- Objects keep their wet film and lamp streaks (paint, glass, walls); the owner's complaint was
  about the ground, so they were not touched.
- Not measured: cost (no new texture reads; the ground lamp lobe is the same loop), motion, other
  islands at dusk.

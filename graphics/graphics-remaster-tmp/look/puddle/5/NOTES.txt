THE VISUAL RULES I FOLLOWED (the wet town square at night in big open-world games, my own reading)
1. Water is the only mirror. A puddle is a dark bed with a clear, sharp image in it: lamps, fire,
   lit walls, the sky. Its edge is where the image stops; the image is never smeared past it.
2. Wet ground that is not under water never reflects an image. Rain shows on it as a darker, deeper
   colour (water fills the grain and takes away the pale surface scatter) and, near a close light,
   one broad, dim, rough pool of glint at the light's foot. Never a column from the light toward
   the eye: that column is what reads as "lacquered floor" at thumbnail size.
3. Enough puddles on a hard town surface that the rain reads by them alone; a meadow keeps few.
4. Day: puddles are bright patches of overcast sky on dark wet ground; the ground itself stays matte.
5. No straight seam anywhere: every class still comes from the continuous ground field (round 3).

WHAT I FOUND (probe evidence)
- The reflection on non-puddle ground came from two places, both visible in round 3 and still in the
  first rework:
  a) the reflections' trace (psPostSsrTrace) gave every wet land texel a mirror weight of
     0.5 x wet x (1 - 0.85 grass) (roads 0.65 x wet) at roughness 0.45..0.75: the grey sheen and the
     blurred images of the lit houses across the whole square;
  b) the forward film (wetSurface) drew every lamp and the fire as a GGX lobe at roughness 0.12..0.33
     over the full film share: the long vertical lamp and fire streaks. The fire is a combat light
     (B4) at its own linear intensity, so its streak was the brightest thing on the ground.
- Removing (a) alone in it1 (early-out in the trace outside the water) took the sheen away, but the
  fire still drew a soft column toward the eye even at roughness 0.5, and the puddle mirrors were
  horizontally smeared beige blobs: the trace blended the puddle's roughness and cone toward the
  ground's at the puddle's soft edge (cone up to 0.11 rad there), and the blur pass spread that.
- A C++ mirror of wetLobe (now a unit test) shows why the rough lobe still made a column: with the
  true grazing view (eye 1.75 m, fire 0.5 m high 9 m ahead) the lobe's grazing Fresnel and mirror
  alignment make a bright tongue reaching toward the eye; taken for an eye at least 31 degrees above
  the ground the peak is about 4x dimmer (0.056 vs 0.24, light falloff included), 3.5 m short of the
  light it is 0.011 vs 0.049, and the pool is wider across (half width 0.26 m vs 0.18 m).
- Pixel probes on the final frames (8-bit luma, same camera in all three builds):
  town_night_rain  fire column below the fire (x1280-1340, y830-1000) vs the strips beside it:
                   round 3 119 vs 57/51 | rework 82 vs 46/45 | this 51 vs 43/45
                   open street crop (330,790 320x180) mean / std: 43.6/12.7 | 43.2/8.6 | 38.9/5.8
                   lower half mean: 51.5 | 49.4 | 46.1
  seam_b           fire column vs beside: 106 vs 46/44 | 90 vs 45/43 | 51 vs 46/45
                   open street (640,760 320x180) mean / std: 46.5/15.7 | 39.5/7.4 | 35.6/4.1
  So the fire's column is gone (a +6..+8 pool instead of +40..+68), and the open ground is darker
  and flatter (the std is the sheen and streaks).
- The town square's ground field reads as about 0.68..0.82 grass (round 3's probe), so with
  round 3's veg * veg threshold it pooled almost like a meadow (threshold 0.70..0.72): few puddles,
  and once the sheen was gone seam_b and seam_d hardly read as rain (it2).

THE CHANGE (shaders only, plus the unit test; no C++ logic, binding or constant mirrored in C++)
Forward pass (EngineGL33_LightingShadersSurface.cpp, wetSurface), ground draws only (land, grass
clutter, roads); objects are unchanged:
- every wet bare ground and road darkens by kWetGroundDarkBase x (1 - veg) more, on top of the
  porosity darkening (gravel and asphalt went 10 % darker before, about 30 % now; soil about 50 %);
- a small saturation gain kWetGroundSat x wet x (1 - veg) (deeper colour, not mustard);
- the film outside the water keeps kWetGroundSheen of itself, at roughness at least kWetGroundRough;
- its lamp and fire lobes are taken with the view raised to at least kWetGroundGlintView
  (rise per run) above the ground: a dim pool at the light's foot, never a streak. The puddle's own
  lobe keeps the true view (lights in water do streak);
- the puddle bed is darker (kWetPuddleDark), so its mirror reads.
Puddle field (same file, the shared Puddles piece, so the trace agrees pixel for pixel):
- the threshold stays bare ground's until the grass share passes kPuddleGrassFrom and reaches grass's
  at a full grass share (smoothstep), instead of leaning by veg squared. More water on a mixed town
  square; a meadow (Eden, grass share about 1) is unchanged. Still a smooth function of the
  continuous field: no step, no seam.
Reflections' trace (EngineGL33_PostSsrShaders.cpp), only while the wet weather shades the ground:
- below kSsrMirrorMin of standing water the texel returns the identity result and traces nothing
  (nothing to mirror, and cheaper: most wet ground skips the march);
- the mirror weight outside the water is 0 (B6's legacy wet ground without the wet weather keeps
  its old weights);
- the water's roughness 0.02, cone kSsrMirrorCone and level normal hold out to the puddle's edge;
  its share alone fades it there (no smeared rough rim).

HONEST WEAK POINTS
- The rain read now rests on where the noise puts puddles. town_night_rain and seam_day have plenty;
  seam_b's and seam_d's foregrounds happen to be dry, so those two read as a damp night more than a
  downpour at thumbnail size. More water would need a lower ground threshold everywhere (kPuddleGround
  is pinned to B6's C++ constant, I left it).
- The near puddles at the camera's feet are dark (water's 2 % Fresnel looking down); only the
  mid-distance ones carry bright images. Physical, but some games boost it.
- The raised-view glint is an art-directed cheat, not a physical lobe: the pool at a light's foot
  does not slide with the eye the way a real rough glint would. In motion it may look slightly
  "painted on"; not checked in motion.
- More water on mixed ground (kPuddleGrassFrom) also means more puddles on any mod island whose land
  field is olive and mixed; a meadow is unchanged.
- The rain rings are still only in the forward lobes; the trace's mirror has only its small noise
  ripple, so the reflected image in a puddle is calm. Moving wetRainRings into the shared piece for
  the trace would add rings to the images (not done for time and shimmer risk).
- Puddle placement differs from round 3 and the rework (threshold change), so a side-by-side shows
  water in new places; the share was not measured with a probe tint this round.
- Not measured: cost (the trace now skips most wet ground, so it should be cheaper) and motion/TAA.
- The smoke and rain_day sky differ per shot (timing and the base tree), as in round 4.

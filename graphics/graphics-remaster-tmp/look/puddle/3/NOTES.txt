APPROACH
Keep round 3's structure (ground field classes, no-seam puddle field, same passes) and only turn
down what reflects outside the puddles, in the two places that draw it:
1. Forward pass (wetSurface): the ground's film outside puddles (it scales the lamp streaks, the
   sun glint and, when the reflections' pass is off, the sky) is multiplied by kWetGroundFilm and
   held at least kWetGroundFilmRough rough. Puddle terms (pud.x) are untouched, so puddles keep
   their sharp lamp lobe (kWetPuddleRough) and their darkening.
2. Reflections' trace (B6): under the wet weather the non-puddle sheen weight is multiplied by
   kSsrWetSheen; mix(sheen, 1.0, puddle) keeps puddles full mirrors. The legacy B6 path (rain
   with wetWeather off) keeps its old weight (the wetWeather mask).
3. Puddles: a little more water (thresholds) and a wider, darker damp margin round each puddle,
   the "patchy darkening" (the margin follows the puddle noise, so it is patchy and has no edge).

CONSTANTS old -> new (and why)
- kWetGroundFilm (new) 0.1: one tenth of the film an object would get. Round 3's ground film was
  about 0.36 x wet on the square and 0.74 x wet on roads; now 0.04 and 0.07. Low enough that no
  lamp, window or fire makes a streak on open ground (probe: the long streaks from the house wall
  and the fire are gone in every night view), high enough to leave a faint warm lift right next
  to the fire (the "dim glint near a close light").
- kWetGroundFilmRough (new) 0.55: GGX alpha 0.30 instead of round 3's 0.0144 (rough 0.12). The
  lobe's peak drops by about (0.30 / 0.0144)^2 = 430x and spreads over tens of degrees, so a lamp
  gives a broad smudge, never a line. 0.55 sits just above kWetSunGroundRough 0.5, the bare
  ground's sun glint, so day and night ground use the same lobe width.
- kSsrWetSheen (new, trace) 0.08: the wet ground outside puddles keeps 8% of B6's sheen weight
  (roads 0.65 -> 0.052, bare land 0.5 -> 0.04, grass less). At grazing Fresnel (about 0.3) that is
  about 1.5% of the reflected image: under one display level for walls and sky, a faint lift only
  for the fire. Not zero so that a wet road still looks a touch less matte than dry at grazing.
- kPuddleRoad 0.61 -> 0.58, kPuddleGround 0.67 -> 0.63 (forward piece), and B6's mirrors
  kSsrPuddleRoad / kSsrPuddleGround (trace and ScreenReflections.hpp) to the same values, so the
  test that ties the two passes' thresholds together still holds. Once the sheen is gone the
  puddles are the only rain cue, and at the old thresholds seam_b had puddles on about 1.6% of the
  lower half of the frame (probe below). kPuddleGrass 0.74 unchanged (grass fields stay mostly dry).
- kPuddleDamp 0.06 -> 0.1 and kWetDampDark 0.9 -> 0.85: the damp margin below the puddle threshold
  is wider and a bit darker: darker patches round the puddles, the "a little patchy darkening".
- Unchanged: kWetGroundDarken 0.35 (the overall wet darkening), kWetPuddleDark 0.55, every puddle
  roughness and weight, the ground field, the puddle noise field, cones and roughness in the trace.

HONEST WEAK POINTS
- At night the scene reads as rainy only weakly. Without the sheen the rain shows as darker
  ground, puddles that mirror lit walls (left of the tree, the road puddle on the right of
  town_night_rain, the left foreground of seam_d) and the rain itself. Puddles that face only the
  night sky are near the ground's brightness and do not read. Pure tuning cannot fix that: it
  needs a brighter, slightly cloud-lit night sky in the puddle mirror, puddles placed where they
  catch lights, or rain rings that sparkle. Round 3's "rainy" read came mostly from the very
  streaks the owner rejects.
- The fire and the lamp no longer throw a reflection in town_night_rain at all: no puddle lies
  between them and the eye in that view. That is the target ("only puddles reflect"), but the
  owner may miss it.
- More puddles change B6's legacy path too (rain with wetWeather off), by design of the pin.
- Day views (seam_day, rain_day) change little; seam_day gains some puddles. The far hillside
  sheen in rain_day (beyond the puddle fade, 150 to 400 m) should now be down to the 8%
  sheen; not checked closely.
- Puddle edges keep round 3's thin bright rim in places (the rim where the puddle normal blends);
  not changed here.
- The smoke and puddle level timing differ shot to shot (smoke stage visible in the sheet).
- Not measured: motion, cost (no added work; same number of texture reads).

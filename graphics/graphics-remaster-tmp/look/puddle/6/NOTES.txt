WHAT I FOUND
- In round 3 the reflections on non-puddle ground come from two places:
  1. The reflections' trace (EngineGL33_PostSsrShaders.cpp) gave every wet ground pixel a mirror weight of
     0.5 x wet (land) or 0.65 x wet (roads) at roughness 0.30 to 0.75, then stretched it vertically: those are
     the long lamp, window and fire streaks and the "lacquered" square.
  2. The forward pass's film (wetSurface) drew lamp streaks at roughness 0.12 on every wet ground pixel, and the
     sun glint at 0.5 on half the film.
- The puddles themselves read as DARK holes in round 3: at night they mirror a nearly black sky, and they were
  only visible because the ground around them was shinier. My first iteration (shots_i1, only the sheen removed)
  proved it: with a matte ground the night puddles all but vanished (lower frame mean luminance 0.159 -> 0.146,
  but the puddles had no edge left). So making only puddles reflect needs the puddles to carry the look:
  more of them on bare ground and roads, a darker water body (so their mirror image stands out), a dark rim.
- The rain_day straight edge beside the jeep is not the puddle's shape: the puddle runs on under the jeep's
  screen columns, but there the trace hits the jeep (a kind 1 hit, not a "behind" miss), so the puddle mirrors
  the jeep's lit dark olive side, which looks like the grass. The edge is the jeep's silhouette.

THE CHANGE (shaders plus the puddle constants' C++ mirror; no binding or pass change)
Non-puddle ground (land, roads, grass clutter):
- Trace: with the wet weather on, the non-puddle sheen is multiplied by kSsrDampWeight = 0, so the ground
  outside the water reflects nothing. B6's legacy path (wetWeather off) is unchanged.
- Forward: the ground's film is never smoother than kWetGroundRough 0.55, and only kWetGroundGlint 0.35 of it
  glints, so a lamp or the fire gives a dim, broad pool of glint near it and never a streak. The film's sky
  term (when the trace is off) takes the same share.
- Forward: bare ground soaks at least kWetGroundSeal 0.6 (grass keeps its own share), and the ground darkening
  rises 0.35 -> 0.5: rain shows as darker, deeper colour (it also replaces the darkening the trace's mirror
  share used to give).
- Sun glint on bare ground: rougher (0.5 -> 0.6) on a smaller share (0.5 -> 0.3).
Puddles:
- Clear mirrors: weight 1, cone kSsrPuddleCone 0.008 (unchanged sharpness), Fresnel as before (Schlick, water
  f0 0.02: weak at the feet, strong at grazing views).
- Rain on the water: wetRainRings moved into the shared puddles piece, and puddleRings() adds a gentle chop (two
  value noises over a third of a metre, moving with time). The forward pass (lamp and sun glints) and the trace
  (the mirror) use the same function, so a ring moves both. Rings fade out by the pixel's footprint (full to
  1 cm, none from 3.5 cm), the chop by its own (8 cm to 30 cm), so far puddles stay calm instead of
  shimmering; the trace widens the cone a little (kSsrPuddleRainCone 0.006 x rain) where the rings are gone.
  The old distance fade (kWetRingsFar 40 m) and the trace's vnoise ripple are gone on the wet path.
- Darker water body: the ground under the water 0.55 -> 0.4 (forward and trace, kept equal by the test).
- Darker wet rim: kWetDampDark 0.9 -> 0.6 over a shorter margin (kPuddleDamp 0.06 -> 0.05 in noise units,
  roughly 10 cm), fading out from the water's edge.
- More water: bare ground threshold 0.67 -> 0.6, roads 0.61 -> 0.56. Grass (0.74) is unchanged, so rain_day's
  meadow keeps its puddles. Shapes are still the three-octave noise field (no straight edges of their own).
- Where the trace finds nothing (a ray behind a vehicle, or level out of the frame), a puddle still mirrors
  kSsrPuddleFill 0.4 of the sky's guess, so its water reads on there.

HONEST WEAK POINTS
- Night read. With the sheen gone, the night views read as a dark, damp square with black-mirror puddles, not
  as a glistening street. seam_b in particular has few puddles in its lower frame, and none of the five views
  puts a puddle on the fire's mirror line, so the fire's reflection (the strongest "rain" cue in round 3) is
  gone; its ground only gets the broad warm glint. The lamp's mirror image falls below the frame in all three
  night views, so no view shows a crisp lamp-in-puddle reflection either. Whether this still "clearly reads as
  a rainy night" is the owner's call; the rain_day and seam_day views read clearly.
- The jeep edge in rain_day is not fixed: it is the jeep's silhouette mirrored, and the screen trace returns
  the jeep's lit side colour, which matches the grass. kSsrPuddleFill only covers misses, not these hits.
- Night puddles that mirror only dark sky are black shapes; at thumbnail size they read as wet patches more
  than as mirrors. Puddles that face lit facades (left of town_night_rain, the road puddle on the right) read as
  clear mirrors.
- More water: the threshold drop roughly doubles the bare-ground and road puddle share (not measured with a
  class probe this round). On seam_day the left of the square now holds a long chain of puddles.
- Rings are only visible within a few metres (their crests are a few cm); the chop makes mirrored edges waver
  slightly at mid range. Not checked in motion; cost not measured (one 3x3 ring loop and two value noises per
  near puddle pixel in each pass, both skipped by the footprint fade and when dry).
- The darker rim is subtle on grass and on dark night ground; it shows best on the bright day square.

(a) The salmon dust foot: cause, proven
---------------------------------------
Object::DrawDecal lit every lit particle (smoke, dust, a fireball's soot) with the classic sun:
sun->FullResult(0.5) (0.375 of LightSun::Diffuse plus the full Ambient) times the accommodation
(the 1.6 user brightness). Under the photoreal camera pre-lit draws are stored as they are (the lit
shaders' finishFragment: camFold, no pre-exposure), so the puff showed the classic light's colour
and level, while the world around it is lit by the rig and white-balanced to the rig's illuminant.
Probe (probe_log.txt; a temporary log in UploadLitFrameConstants, removed): at firefight_3 (19:35,
sun -1.73 deg) the classic sun diffuse is still (0.639, 0.240, 0.184), a red-pink sunset sun (the
classic model keeps it until 5 deg under), the classic ambient (0.028, 0.030, 0.040). The puff light
was (0.375 x dif + amb) x 1.6 = (0.43, 0.19, 0.17) in display terms: salmon. The rig's sun is 0 (set),
its sky fill a dim neutral blue-grey (zenith 0.0015), and the camera balances that blue air to white,
which made the classic pink even pinker. A/B in one build (a probe switch forcing the classic path,
same pinned staging, probe_ab_classic_vs_rig.png): classic-lit middle foot (89.5, 54.0, 50.5) over a
sky of (82.6, 55.1, 49.5), pinker and lighter than the sky; rig-lit (57.4, 41.8, 41.3). By day the
same path gave white smoke a blue cast: the classic ambient (0.55, 0.60, 0.80) x 1.6 clipped to
(1, 1, 1) and the camera's warm white balance turned it blue-white (ffh_day, left panel of d_).

The change (the root: lit particles take the rig, not the classic sun):
- LightRig.hpp/.cpp: ParticleRigIrradiance(rig, key, shade): kParticleKeyShare of the key light (the
  sun, dimmed by the cloud field's mean; or the moon under the moon key) and of the moon's
  shadowless rest, plus the sky's irradiance averaged over every direction (the lit shaders'
  skyIrradiance: a quarter zenith, a quarter each horizon side, a quarter ground bounce) times the
  world's shade share (SurfaceShadeFactor, 1 without the surfaces lane).
- EngineGL33 (UploadLitFrameConstants): while the camera writes scene light, the frame's particle
  light = that irradiance x the rig's exposure (with the burst flash gain, as litZenith.w) x the
  frame's pre-exposure (litCamera.w), i.e. exactly how the world's passes store their light.
  New Engine virtual ParticleRigLight() hands it out for the frame it was set (false otherwise:
  classic programs and other backends keep the classic sun).
- Object::DrawDecal: under the camera the classic sun term is dropped; the puff takes the rig light
  at the material's brightness (the accommodation, as the lit shaders' materials) in linear light,
  added to the classic local lights (lamps, unchanged) and the physical night lights (unchanged);
  the combat lights (fires, bursts) and the fireball glow follow as before.
Result: firefight_3 middle foot (vanilla | landed | before | after) (69.8, 38.4, 35.9) |
(82.0, 55.6, 48.2) | (104.8, 61.1, 57.9) | (61.4, 40.7, 37.7) over the sky (82.1, 54.8, 49.2): the foot
now has the dusk sky's hue (R/G 1.51 against the sky's 1.50, before 1.72) and is darker than the
sky, a dust silhouette against the twilight. Left foot (82.1, 52.8, 44.2) -> (76.2, 52.0, 44.7).

---------------------------

shell to just after the third shot the world steps a fixed 1/30 s a frame (new capFixStep =
triSetFixedStep, logged as event=fixstep), real time again after firefight_3. The camera, the

firefight_3 = 26 frames (0.87 s) after it: the smoke column with its dust foot. Verified in three

edit look-director. The next cut (look_r5_*) picks the pin up if it is cut from the patched
abel_lighting.test.sqf (my tools/gen_r5_particles.py shows how: the head plus view 7's remastered
half).

Constants (name: old -> new)
----------------------------
- New LightRig.hpp kParticleKeyShare 0.6 (tried 0.375, the classic decal material's share, first:
  white smoke by day went duller than the sand).
- The particle's sky term: the mean over directions of the rig's zenith, both horizon sides and
  ground (weights 0.25 each), times SurfaceShadeFactor (0.65 at a clear high sun, 1 at dusk).
- Firefight shells: real-time step -> fixed 1/30 s (0.0333333) from the first shell to the third shot.

CAUSE
The meter read the sun's burn. Round 4 raised the sky's roll-off cap toward the sun from
kSkyRolloffCap 2.4 to kSkySunCap 10 (camSkyRolloff(lin, d)) so the glow round the sun burns white.
The meter pass (psPostMeter) reads the frame's stored light, so every sky tap within a few degrees
of the sun now read up to several times brighter (the cap is still 3.0 at 4 degrees, 2.7 at 6).
Three guards in TargetExposureLog2 read the sky's bright taps: the highlight percentile
(kHighlightLimit 1.3), the sky's bright end (SkyTopPeak, kSkyTopLimit 2.2) and the sky by a low sun
(SunRingPeak, kSunRingLimit 2.0). They pull the exposure until those taps sit under their limits,
so with the burn in their taps they closed the whole frame down. The guards were tuned on the
plain cap (PhotoCamera.hpp says so: "the guards' limits on the sky sit under the cap"). It also
explains why woods_pine2_evening fell even with the sun just outside the frame: the ring and the
sky's bright end look at the glow at the frame's corner.

THE CHANGE (fix at the meter, the render keeps its burn)
The meter reads the sky at its plain roll-off. In the meter pass every sky tap (depth at the far
plane) goes through meterSkyLight before its luminance: its peak is taken back through the
roll-off toward the sun along that tap's own view ray (k - r ln(1 - (o - k) / r), r the cap toward
the sun minus the knee) and rolled off again with the plain cap. Far from the sun and under the
knee it is the identity; light at or past the cap toward the sun (the disc at kSunDiscLevel 12,
the air's glow over it) reads as the plain cap. So the mean, the blocks' brightest taps and their
brightest sky taps see the sky round the sun exactly as round 3's meter did, while the frame keeps
the white disc and the burnt glow.
- EngineGL33_PostShaders.cpp: s_psPostMeterHead gets the uniforms meterSky (knee, cap, sun cap,
  glow radius), meterSun (unit view-space direction to the sun, w on), meterProj (1/_11, 1/_22),
  meterRect (the 3D viewport); s_psPostMeterMain gets meterSkyLight and calls it on sky taps.
- EngineGL33_Camera.cpp PostCameraMeter: sets those uniforms from PhotoCamera.hpp's own constants
  (no copy of the numbers in GLSL) and the sun from the camera (InverseScaled, as PostFrameSun).
- PhotoCamera.hpp/.cpp: SkyMeterLight(storedPeak, cosToSun), the C++ mirror for the tests.
- FeatureGates.hpp/.cpp and CameraReadoutNow: the readout now ends with
  "skytop= ring= ringw= free=" (appended after skip=, earlier fields unchanged), so a capture log
  shows which guard holds a frame down. Kept: it is how the cause was found and costs nothing.
- Not touched: the guards and their limits, the meter's cuts and weights, the roll-off, the sun
  cap, the disc, kFogBeamGain, the fog, the AI link.

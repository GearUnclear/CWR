BLOCKER 1 (new in round 4): views with the sun in frame lost 0.3 to 1.55 EV, which leaves woods_pine2_evening murky
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
woods_pine2_evening (sun 20 degrees, 17:30) is now the darkest day view in the set. The frame mean is 33/255
(R3 50, landed 59, original 84), and the lower half is (35,33,22). That is darker than town_night (45).
At thumbnail the soldier and the jeep sink into the shade and the forest floor reads muddy. In round 3
this view was a clear win.
The capture logs show the camera, not the scene, did this. The meter mean stayed the same (-7.73 vs -7.72),
the highlight meter rose (-2.32 to -1.90), and the exposure fell from ev 1.82 to 0.27. The noon fixer's own
shot of this view was ev 2.15, so the drop came in the merge.
The same pattern shows in every sun-in-frame view (R3 ev to R4 ev, highlight meter):
  woods_pine2_evening  1.82 -> 0.27  (high -2.32 -> -1.90)   frame mean 50 -> 33
  sinai_lowsun_fp      0.70 -> -0.31 (high -0.08 -> 0.29)    frame mean 85 -> 67, ground (lower half) 53 -> 39
  lowsun_fp            -0.02 -> -0.67 (high 0.64 -> 0.79)    frame mean 94 -> 82
  pine_rays            1.25 -> 0.63  (high -1.04 -> -0.48)   ground (lower half) 44 -> 37
  dawn_valley          0.03 -> -0.29 (high 0.48 -> 0.58)
  sunrise_ridge        2.28 -> 1.78  (high -1.83 -> -1.55)
Views without the sun in frame did not change (forest_edge_golden 2.06 -> 2.06, town_noon 1.82 -> 1.82,
forest_morning 1.72 -> 1.72). The sun fixer's own build already shows it: lowsun_fp ev -0.70 and
sinai_lowsun_fp ground 47 in fix4/sun/shots_2.
Likely cause: the sun fix raised the sky's roll-off cap near the sun from 2.4 to kSkySunCap 10 (past white).
The exposure guards in PhotoCamera (the highlight percentile toward kHighlightLimit, skyTop toward
kSkyTopLimit, and the low sun's ring toward kSunRingLimit) were tuned against the old 2.4 cap. They now
read the brighter glow and pull the whole frame down. A photographer lets the sun's own glow blow out and
does not underexpose the forest for it. Suggested root fix: meter the sky at the old cap, or keep the disc
and the glow inside a few degrees of the sun out of the guards. Then confirm woods_pine2_evening returns
to about ev 1.8 to 2.1 and sinai_lowsun_fp to about 0.7, with the white disc and the glow kept.
Severity at thumbnail: woods_pine2_evening reads as underexposed (blocker). sinai_lowsun_fp reads
muddy brown. lowsun_fp, pine_rays, dawn_valley and sunrise_ridge are darker but still read well.

BLOCKER 2 (left over from round 3, smaller): optics_mist_hud and optics_mist_sight are still the palest frames and lose the far terrain
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This is much better than round 3. The near grass is green again: (98,105,95) against R3 (125,130,128)
and the original (80,90,61). The hill silhouettes and the tree line show as ghosts, so it is no longer
a white wall. But at thumbnail the top three quarters of the frame is still a flat near-white sheet,
(214,217,225) against the original's grey (178,178,170), with a slight cool cast. The rock outcrop on
the right is almost erased: in the right-hand block (x 1650-1900, y 700-950) the spread is 11 against
31 in the original and 5 in R3. At a glance the original and landed read clearer and more like the
"light mist" that fog 0.3 asks for. The overhaul reads like dense fog. The exposure went up this round
(ev 0.71 -> 1.04 and 0.33 -> 0.85), which keeps the air bright even though the fog is now thinner.
The specks over the tree line are unchanged at full size (mist_hud_specks_r4_3x.png). They are not
visible at thumbnail.
Likely next step (as the merger says): the air's brightness under the camera exposure. One option is
for this scene to meter like a grey day, so the air sits near the original's 180 rather than 214. A
little less mist at 200 to 400 m would keep the rock legible. Do not touch the AI fog link.

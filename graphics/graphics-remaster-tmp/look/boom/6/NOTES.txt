Visual rules I followed
-----------------------
1. A cased charge (shell, grenade, warhead) is a flash, not a fire: the brightest thing on screen for
   a frame, white-hot, sitting on the ground as a dome (never a flat cut-off bottom), then a short orange
   afterburn of its products, then nothing glowing. No Hollywood fireball on an HE shell.
2. Size and glow time grow with the cube root of the charge (blast scaling). The charge is read from
   the ammo's blast, indirectHit x indirectHitRange, with the 120 mm HE shell (150 x 8 = 1200) as
   reference: HE scale 1, HEAT 0.63, hand grenade 0.47. A shaped charge (indirectHitRange < 3: HEAT)
   throws its fireball narrower (0.6) and taller (1.35).
3. What lingers is the earth: a dark grey-brown dust and smoke column, thrown dirt flying up and
   falling back, the cloud drifting on the wind for many seconds. Bigger crater, longer linger.
4. Fuel burns slow: a vehicle's tank is a deflagration, a large rolling orange fireball (never white)
   under thick black soot, then fire and smoke. It follows the fuel fireball rules D = 5.8 M^(1/3) m,
   t = 0.45 M^(1/3) s.
5. The light lasts as long as the fire and is a pool that fades out, never a disc with a rim or a line
   on the horizon. The light sits inside the fireball.
6. At thumbnail every munition must read differently: HE = white flash then a big dark column;
   grenade = small flash, small brown puff; HEAT = narrow yellow flash, narrow column; fuel = big
   orange ball turning black.

What each munition spawned before (probe evidence)
---------------------------------------------------
Code path: a shell hit makes an Explosion (Smokes.cpp; size = hit x 0.003 clamped 0.25..1: Shell120
0.6, Heat120 1.0, grenade 0.25) and Landscape::ExplosionDammage a Crater (Collisions.cpp; big branch for
explosive ammo, size = landAlpha x 0.5, landAlpha from indirectHit x range / 200 capped 2) plus
landAlpha x 10 TEGround debris things.
- Explosion: CfgCloudlets Explosion puffs (cl_fired, 2500 K, -4000 K/s, 0.2 s + 0.2 s fade), all
  dropped in one batch (the Explosion simulates at 5 Hz), plus the combat light burst.
- Crater (big): CraterSmoke1/2/3 grey puffs living 2 to 2.4 s with 1.2 s fades (gone by about 3 s,
  thinning from 1.5 s), CraterDustBig loaded but never simulated (`if (!_isSmoke)`).
- Why the fire lasted one frame (proven with a temporary DrawDecal log, removed): the fireball puffs
  were drawn every frame with bright colours (frame 2: rgb (1, 0.97, 0.6), glow 0.39), yet nothing
  showed. The puff's cl_fired texture steps through 47 frames (fired.73..119) in 0.5 s, a new texture
  every game frame, each streamed in on first use under the per-frame texture budget, so after the
  first frame the puffs mostly had no texture to draw and returned before DrawDecal. Also each new puff
  was invisible for its first two frames (alpha 0 at birth, fade-in value 0 on its first simulate), so
  the burst light lit a flat patch of ground a frame before any fire showed.
- Fuel (Jeep): SmokeSourceVehicle::SimulateExplosion computes the blast from GetExplosives(), which for
  a plain Transport is only its ammo (Jeep 0), so `type.hit > 50` failed: a destroyed Jeep had no fuel
  explosion at all, only its engine smoke (in 1 s, 6 s, out 6 s). The pale puff in the before clip is the
  smoke lit by the wreck's embers. This is a misbehaving munition in the shared test: there was no fuel
  explosion to judge.
- The grenade's and HEAT's ground light: a bright flat ellipse with a rim (the window at 3 core radii
  cut the pool where it was still a tenth of its peak, and the 0.3 gain saturated it), brighter than
  the fireball, for 3 to 4 frames.

The change
----------
CombatLights.hpp/.cpp (pure, unit tested): BurstScale(blast), BurstScaleFromSize (network copies without
ammo), BurstFireballRadius, BurstCoolRate, BurstFlashCoolRate, BurstGlowSeconds, CraterLinger(size),
FuelBallDiameter(litres), FuelBallDuration(litres), FuelBallEnvelope(age, duration).
Shots.cpp: every `new Explosion` (7 sites) gets SetBlast(indirectHit x indirectHitRange,
indirectHitRange < 3). Gameplay damage is untouched.
Smokes.cpp:
- Explosion::EmitBurst on its first simulate: one flash puff (3600 K, 2.1 r across) and 9 + 7 s
  fireball puffs (core hotter than edge, 2580..3000 K) placed as a dome on the ground (centre 0.45 r up,
  each puff's centre at least 0.4 of its diameter above the ground), expanding 3..6 m/s x s, rising,
  slowed by side friction. Born full size, no fade-in, Simulate(0) at birth: drawn the same frame the
  light starts. They use the resident cl_basic puff, its first texture frame held (the fix for the
  one-frame fire). Each puff lives while it glows, then fades as soot into the crater's smoke.
- The burst's combat light sits in the fireball (0.7 r up for a ground burst) with strength scale^1.5.
- Crater::Init big branch: earth colour (0.66, 0.58, 0.49) x 0.55..1 times the table grey; puff life x
  CraterLinger (HE about 5, HEAT about 4, grenade 2.6), fade-out x 0.8 linger, swelling x 0.6..1 linger;
  smoke1 emits 2x as long, rises at 0.75 x (0.45 + 0.55 size) of 6.25 m/s and brakes at 1.4 x that factor
  m/s^2 (stops after about 4 s, column some 10 to 15 m for HE); puff size x (0.35 + 0.4 size); the table
  lightening slowed by the linger. The thrown earth (CraterDustBig) is now simulated for big bursts:
  0.15 s of dark clods, up to 16 m/s, falling at 9.8 m/s^2.
- SmokeSourceVehicle::StartFuelBall (from SimulateExplosion, whatever the blast): litres = GetFuel();
  the ball is D = FuelBallDiameter, t = FuelBallDuration; per frame (precision 1/30 s while it burns)
  fire puffs at 30/s over the first 45 %, born 2150 K, cooling 520 K/s, swelling over 0.25 s, buoyant
  (+3 m/s^2); black soot puffs (0.05, 0.045, 0.04) at 15/s rolling out of its top; a Fire combat light
  following FuelBallEnvelope; the wreck's smoke then runs 40 s (as after the classic burst). The classic
  _fire/_darkFire burst is skipped when the fuel ball runs.
EngineGL33_PostCombat.cpp: the Explosion's surface light: reach 3 -> 6 core radii, gain 0.3 -> 0.15
(kBurstSurfaceGain; cannon keeps 0.3). With the light inside the fireball the pool's own fall-off has it
under a fiftieth of its peak where the window bites: it fades out instead of ending at a rim.

Per munition, now (final build, 30 fps fixed step)
--------------------------------------------------
- 120 mm HE (dusk, 50 m): frame 1 a white-hot dome about 5 m across sitting on the ground, ground lit
  under it; frame 2 an orange afterburn ball fading into brown dust; frame 3 on dark earth: clods and
  20 to 30 dark dust puffs thrown up to about 10 m and falling back, a dark grey-brown column to about
  15 m within 1 s, spreading 20 m wide by 2 s and drifting, still present at the clip's end (3 s);
  puffs live about 12 s plus 5 s of fade.
- Hand grenade (dusk, 22 m): frame 1 a small yellow-white flash (about 2.4 m) with a soft ground glow,
  frame 2 a small orange ball (about 3 m), then a brown dust puff a few metres across lingering about
  6 s. Reads clearly smaller than the HE.
- 120 mm HEAT (dusk, 40 m): frame 1 a narrow, taller yellow-white flash, frame 2 a pale remnant, then
  a narrow dark column, smoke lingering about 9 s. Smaller and more focused than the HE.

  0.2 s, rolling up with black soot round its top; orange for about 0.4 s, deep orange inside black at

  the same ball, bright orange, lighting the plain warm; its soot turns pale grey (lit by its own

- Night HE: a white dome flash for a frame that lights the ground and men, an orange frame, then dim
  grey dust in the moonlight (the bright grey night smoke of round 5 is now earth coloured and darker).

Honest weak points
------------------
1. Lingering smoke enters the later clips of the shared test: the HE's column is still in the
   grenade clip, and the burning Ural staged behind the line now gets its own fuel ball at staging and
   smokes heavily for 40 s, so every dusk clip has a bigger black column in the background than the
   before clips. That is the new behaviour, not a test fault.
2. The white flash is a single frame; at 30 fps whether it lands on a captured frame depends on timing.
   The HE afterburn reads for one to two frames; at full speed it is a sharp pop, by design.
3. One crater smoke puff sometimes shoots up much faster than the rest (HE frames 13 to 18, a dark
   ball climbing to about 20 m); I did not find which source launches it.
4. By night the fuel ball's soot is lit pale grey by its own fire light; "thick black soot" holds only
   at dusk. kFuelBallLight is the knob (9 -> 5 already).
5. All burst puffs use the first frame of cl_basic: close up, the fireball's puffs share one shape.
6. The burst's ground pool still reads as a soft ellipse on flat ground for a frame or two from eye
   height (softer, with no rim, under the fire), as a lit patch on flat ground does.
7. Not checked: rockets (LAW, RPG never arm in the tests) and the LAW probe's black lower half frame.
   Missile explosions also get SetBlast and the new burst; I did not shoot them.
8. Every destroyed vehicle with 2 l of fuel or more now gets a fuel ball, including aircraft, ships and
   the staged Ural (capped at 16 m and 2 s). Not shot for aircraft.
9. The classic lighting path (no combat lights) was not shot: there the burst puffs show the config
   table's colours (white above 2200 K), which should still read as a flash.

Approach
--------
Check every stage of each explosion runs in simulation time and give it a physical duration.
Timing check: every stage already runs on the simulation step. Cloudlets (puffs) are simulated with the
frame's deltaT in World::SimulateCloudlets; Explosion, Crater and SmokeSourceVehicle are "animals"
simulated by SimulateRest with the frame's deltaT whenever the step is under 1/15 s (their
SetSimulationPrecision 0.2 only matters under 15 fps, where SimulateOptimized steps them by 0.2 s);
combat lights age by GCombatLights().Tick(deltaT) in World::Simulate. Under the shared clips' fixed
1/30 s step nothing runs on wall clock. So the blink was not a clock bug: the stages were simply given
the wrong lengths (and one was never run).

What each munition spawned before (config dump of CfgCloudlets/CfgAmmo, engine code, probe frames)

- 120 mm HE (Shell120: hit 200, indirectHit 150, range 8): size 0.6. 120 mm HEAT (Heat120: hit 700,

- Jeep destroyed: SmokeSourceVehicle's fuel explosion needs FuelExplosion hit > 50, the vehicle's

The change
----------
1. HE / HEAT / grenade size by charge (Shots.cpp, ground and object hits of shells): size = 0.6 x
   cbrt(indirectHit x indirectHitRange / 1200) in [0.2, 1] (ExplosionVisualSize). HE 0.6 (same),
   HEAT 0.38 (was 1.0), grenade 0.28 (was 0.25). Missiles, pipe bombs, mines unchanged.
2. Detonation flash (Explosion ctor): the fireball puffs cool from 2500 K past 1800 K (glow under a
   tenth of a 2200 K puff's) in ExplosionFlashSeconds = 0.1 x size: HE 60 ms, HEAT 38 ms, grenade
   28 ms (cube root of the charge, as for real detonations). Rate -700 / flash: HE -11,700 K/s, HEAT
   -18,500, grenade -24,700 (was -4,000 for all).
3. Combat light (EvaluateCombatLight, Explosion): its hold and decay times scale by min(size, 1), so the
   light lasts as long as its flash: HE hold 0.09 s, HEAT 0.057 s, grenade 0.042 s; size 1 (and the
   unit tests' strength 1) keeps 0.15 s.
4. Crater column (Crater::Init, big burst): every puff holds 9 x L s and fades over 9 x L s, L = crater
   size in [0.4, 1.2] (HE crater about 0.95: 8.6 + 8.6 s; grenade about 0.3 -> 0.4: 3.6 + 3.6 s). The
   rising dark cloud (smoke1) no longer whitens as it ages (its table ran grey 500 K -> white 0 K at
   -40 K/s; now held at 500 K), its rise slows by 1.1 m/s^2 from 6.25 to a 0.25 m/s hang (about 18 m
   high). Lingering puffs keep spreading (size grows 8 % per second after growing up, opacity falls in
   proportion) and their sideways speed relaxes to the landscape wind in 3 s (new Cloudlet::SetLinger;
   only these puffs and the fuel's soot use it). smoke2 (low dust skirt) is earth-tinted
   (0.55, 0.50, 0.42) and spreads 1.5x; smoke3 a grey veil.
5. Dust fall: CraterDustBig now runs for a big burst too, 0.35 x timeSize s at 0.02 s intervals: a dark
   earth curtain thrown about 4 m up that falls back within about 2 s.
6. Fuel (SmokeSourceVehicle::SimulateExplosion): any fuel (hit over 1) burns as a deflagration; the
   blast damage, sound and the longer wreck smoke still need hit over 50 (gameplay unchanged). Size
   0.6 x cbrt(hit / 50) in [0.35, 1.25], feeding 0.5 x cbrt(hit / 50) s in [0.3, 1.25]. Puffs
   2 + 2 x size m from a 0.8 + 1.2 x size m source, every 0.012 s, born 2150 K cooling -420 K/s
   (each glows about 0.83 s: FuelFireballGlowSeconds), held 0.42 s, faded 0.75 s, buoyant (+2.4 m/s^2
   to 5 m/s). Black soot puffs 1.5 + 2 x size m every 0.03 s for 1.6 x the feed time, held 3 s, faded
   5 s, climbing 0.3 to 4 m/s, spreading, drifting. A Fire-kind combat light (strength 3 x size) burns
   for feed + 0.83 s, rising 2 m and dimming as it cools.

Honest weak points
------------------
1. The HE column is visible about 11 s, at the short end of 10 to 30 s: the linear fade plus the
   spread thinning hide it before the puffs die. A longer hold or a slower fade would stretch it.
2. The HE column reads as two blobs (a rising dark ball over a separate dark dust skirt with a gap) in
   the first seconds; a real column is continuous. smoke1 rises 18 m while smoke2 stays low.
3. The wind drift is implemented (puffs relax to the landscape wind) but the staged dusk/night views
   look calm, so no lean is visible in the probes.
4. The Jeep's orange phase is visible about 0.3 to 0.4 s, not a full second: a Jeep's fuel hit is
   small, so it feeds 0.3 s; each puff glows about 0.8 s in theory but the soot in front of it and the
   Wien drop make it read shorter. The fireball is also visibly made of separate puffs.
5. The shell flash is now physically short: at 30 fps the HE flash is one bright frame, HEAT and the
   grenade can fall between strip key frames. That is the brief detonation the reference asks for, but
   in motion the burst still opens with a blink; the lingering column is what now carries it.
6. A first build that also held the cl_fired fireball puffs longer (0.4 s + 1.2 s fade, spreading)
   turned the grenade's and the HEAT round's puffs a flat khaki from 0.13 s (shots2, cause not found);
   I reverted that part, so those puffs keep their old 0.4 s life.
7. Not touched (other approaches): the hard sprite/ground line under the fireball, the ground light
   patch at the flash, the night ball of light, rockets (never arm in these tests).

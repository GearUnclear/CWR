Approach
--------
Reason from the munition: a cased high explosive (shell, HEAT, grenade) is a blink of white-hot
flash whose size and length go with the cube root of the charge, then thrown earth and a dark
dust and smoke column that hangs and drifts; a vehicle's fuel deflagrates as a slower orange
fireball of about a second that rolls up into black soot, then the wreck burns. Find what each
munition spawns, fix the cause in the emitters, keep every number in one pure header
(engine/Poseidon/Graphics/Rendering/Lighting/BlastLook.hpp) that the unit tests hold.

What each munition spawns today (code reading + config dump + the before frames)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

- Shell120 hit 200, indirectHit 150, range 8. Heat120 hit 700, indirectHit 300, range 1.
  GrenadeHand (Grenade) hit 20, indirectHit 18, range 7. All explosive=1. FuelExplosion hit 100.

The change
----------
1. BlastLook.hpp (new, header only): the explosion size from the charge, the HE flash, the fuel
   fireball and soot, the crater's earth, column and haze, the wreck's burn times.
2. Shots.cpp: every Explosion's size = BlastExplosionSize(indirectHit, indirectHitRange) =
   0.6 x cbrt(charge / 1200), clamped 0.25..1 (was hit x 0.003, or hit x 0.001 on an object hit).
   Shell120 keeps 0.6; Heat120 0.38 (was 1.0); hand grenade 0.28 (was 0.25); satchels stay at 1.
3. Explosion (HE flash): puffs born at 3000 K, cooling (3000 - 1500) K over 0.1 s x the linear
   scale; emitted 0.035 s x scale (min 1/60 s); grow 0.04 s x scale (was 0.25 effectively 0.05,
   but now the first drawn frame is full size); births on the upper half of the ball only
   (WeaponCloudsSource::SetUpperHalf), so the ground no longer cuts its bottom flat; outward speed
   2 per metre of offset (classic 4, up to 25 m/s); soot that hangs: 0.7 s x (0.5 + 0.5 s) at full
   alpha + 1.6 s x (0.5 + 0.5 s) fade, climb -3 m/s2 between 0.2 and 4 m/s (classic -0.2, 0..6).
   Its combat light now spawns at its second step, with the first drawn puffs (it lit the ground
   a frame before any fireball: the bare bright patch).
4. Crater (HE dust and smoke), from the crater size c and life scale L = 0.35 + 0.65 c:
   earth jet (CraterSmoke1): 0.2 s + 0.15 s thinning, rise 4 + 8c m/s, -9 m/s2, dark earth
   (0.20, 0.17, 0.13), 2L s + 3L s fade; column (CraterSmoke2): 1.2L s + 3L s thinning, rise
   2 + 2.5c m/s slowing at -2 m/s2 to 0.3, grey-brown (0.15, 0.14, 0.125), 5L s + 7L s fade, grows
   over 5L s; haze skirt (CraterSmoke3): 0.5 s + 1.5L s, rise 0.6, (0.30, 0.27, 0.22), 4L + 6L s,
   alpha 0.35. Colours held (no table walk toward white); each burst a shade of 0.85..1.15.
5. SmokeSourceVehicle (fuel): the fireball always burns when the vehicle explodes (the damage,
   sound and gate are unchanged): fire size max(hit x 0.001, 0.6) to 1.25; puffs born 2150 K
   cooling 380 K/s (each glows orange about a second), emitted 0.9 s x cbrt(size / 0.6), puff size
   5 x fire size and spread 2.5 x (classic: the fire size, a seventh of a shell's puffs), interval
   15 ms (classic 1 ms), roll out at 1.2 per metre, climb +1 m/s2 to 2.5 m/s, grow 0.8 s; black
   soot (0.05, 0.045, 0.04) with it for 1.6 x as long, 2.5 s + 5 s fade; the wreck then burns
   45 s x 0.8..1.2 and fades over 30 s x 0.8..1.2 (it smoked about 13 s when under the gate).
   Its embers' Fire light carries the fireball (strength + 6 x fire size while it burns, falling

6. Smokes.hpp setters: CloudletSource::SetTemperature, SmokeSource::SetSourceSpeed / SetDensity,
   WeaponCloudsSource::SetRadialGain / SetUpperHalf; Explosion::_steps; fuel fireball state.

Weak points (honest)
--------------------
1. The HE smoke reads as a dark cloud hanging a few metres up rather than a column rooted in the
   ground: the column puffs rise to 3 to 6 m and stall; the dust skirt under it is faint. A
   continuous low emission at the crater for a few seconds would root it.
2. The fuel fireball's orange phase reads about 0.4 s, not a full second: the puffs are first seen
   white-ish (the halved Explosion burst light, white, still scatters into the air as a soft white
   ball, worst at night at +200 ms), and the orange puffs darken under their soot within about
   0.5 s at this exposure. Dropping the Explosion burst for fuel (Fire light only) and a slower
   cooling (-250 K/s) are the next things to try.
3. The grenade's and the HEAT round's flashes are pale and soft (one frame), not the intense
   white-yellow blink the HE shell gets: their faster cooling (shorter flash for a smaller charge)
   leaves the first drawn frame at about 1900 to 2100 K. Starting smaller charges hotter, or
   holding the birth temperature for the first drawn step, would fix it. The bright thin line on
   the ground under them is the burst's surface light.
4. The flash's ground light is still the burst's disc light (kBurstSurfaceReachCores); it now
   coincides with the fireball frame, but I did not change its shape.
5. The column does not drift visibly within the 3 s HE clips (no wind in the staging); it lingers
   in place and fades.
6. The fuel fireball now burns for every destroyed car or tank whatever its explosives (the damage
   gate is unchanged), and wrecks smoke about 60 s: more smoke on the map in a long battle.
7. The LAW/RPG path (the black lower half seen in rprobe) was not touched or checked; rockets use
   the same Explosion, so their flash and smoke follow this change.
8. The Explosion's own puff count is unchanged (1 ms interval); they now live 2.3 s, so a barrage
   costs more overdraw than before. Not profiled.

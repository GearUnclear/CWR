0. A harness finding first (affects every agent's clips)
--------------------------------------------------------
Each captured frame of look_probe_mun_* is about 0.1 s of simulation, not 1/30 s. triScreenshot
"at present" waits for the next present and Trident waits for the file, so several AppIdle frames
run between shots, each at the fixed 1/30 s step (about 140 ms of wall time per shot in the log).
Evidence: the 120 mm shell dropped from 40 m at 250 m/s (0.16 s of flight) bursts 2 captured frames
after its spawn; CraterSmoke1's puffs live 3.6 s and are gone about 38 captured frames after the
burst (3.6 s / 38 = 0.095 s a frame). So the mp4s play about 3 times too fast, the "+ms" labels
are a third of the real time, and which phase of a 0.1 s fireball lands on a frame changes from run
to run (in my final run the dusk HE shot caught the flash frame before the fireball, the night shot
caught the fireball). A brief real event can only ever read as a blink in these clips. The fix is in
the test (wait for the screenshot without stepping the world, or fixed step 1/90 with a shot every
step); I did not change the shared tests.

2. The change
-------------
a) HDR glow for fireball puffs (the core brighter than anything round it). Object::DrawDecal keeps the
   level of a fireball's glow past white as an HDR gain (Engine::SetDecalGlowGain); the GL33 backend
   carries it in the decal's second texture coordinate, tagged (u = -(1000 + gain),
   kDecalGlowGainTag), and the pre-lit shader multiplies the decal's glow by it only when tagged.
   The camera stores it up to its encoding's 16, its curve whitens it and its bloom catches it.
b) Temperature glow over age, per munition. FireballPuff now spans white-hot to soot in the visible
   range: kFireballEmission 8 -> 137 at 2200 K (Wien at 580 nm), cap 2 -> 9 (linear). 2500 K white
   (9, 9, 8), 2000 K yellow-orange past white, 1600 K orange (1.8), 1400 K dull red, 1300 K soot.
   Cooling per munition at the source: a cased charge cools 5000 K/s / cbrt(size) (BurstCoolingRate;
   120 mm HE 5930 K/s: white for one 1/30 frame, orange the next, soot by 0.2 s); the fuel tank's
   puffs are a deflagration: born at 1950 K, cooling 550 K/s, living 0.9 s + 0.7 s fade, growing over
   0.35 s and rising (climb 2.5, max 4 m/s), size x4.
c) The fireball on its flash frame: Cloudlet::PrimeFireballBirth (called by WeaponCloudsSource) gives
   a new fireball puff its colour, full alpha and kFireballBirthSize 0.7 of its size at birth, and the
   puff keeps that size while it grows on.
d) No flat cut-off bottom: a fireball puff is drawn with its centre at least kFireballLiftShare 0.7
   of its radius above the ground (GLandscape SurfaceY), so its round soft foot rests on the ground.
   (A real depth fade needs the scene depth bound in the world pass; at the grazing view of these
   clips it softens the line by 2 to 3 px only, so I did not build it.)
e) The flash: a detonation's flash lasts tens of milliseconds and scales with the cube root of the
   charge: kBurstFlashTau 0.025 s and kBurstGlowTau 0.07 s x cbrt(strength) (were 0.05 and 0.16
   fixed); no hold under the photoreal camera (was 0.15 s x night). Its light on the ground is a
   graded pool: the Explosion kind's surface gain x kBurstSurfaceShare 0.3 (0.3 -> 0.09), so the pool
   centre no longer clips into a white lens.
f) Fuel: no detonation flash: the burst becomes a Fire-kind owned light (strength 8 x size), swelling
   over 0.15 s, held to 0.5 s, gone by 1.6 s; it lights the smoke and puffs orange (hue kept) and its
   flame emission.
Not changed: the smoke and dust config (lifetime, colour), the exposure response (FlashExposureGain is
untouched; with the flash now brief its night brightening lasts about 0.12 s), the AI sight and fog
link (Target.cpp, VolumetricFog.cpp, SimulateFogSight are not in the diff).

Constants (old -> new)
----------------------
- kFireballEmission 8 -> 137; kFireballEmitCap 2 -> 9.
- New: kFireballBirthSize 0.7, kFireballLiftShare 0.7, kBurstCoolKps 5000, kFuelFireInitK 1950,
  kFuelFireCoolKps 550, kFuelFireballSize 4, kBurstFlashTau 0.025, kBurstGlowTau 0.07,
  kBurstSurfaceShare 0.3 (EngineGL33_PostCombat.cpp), kDecalGlowGainTag 1000.
- Explosion envelope: flash 0.05 s -> 0.025 x cbrt(e) s; glow tail 0.16 s -> 0.07 x cbrt(e) s; colour
  cooling 0.45 s -> 0.21 x cbrt(e) s. CombatTuning::flashHold under the camera 0.15 x night -> 0.
- HE puffs: deltaT -4000 -> -5000 / cbrt(size) K/s. Fuel puffs: initT 2500 -> 1950, deltaT -4000 ->
  -550, cloudletDuration 0.2 -> 0.9, growUp 0.05 -> 0.35, fadeIn 0.01 -> 0.05, fadeOut 0.2 -> 0.7,
  climb (-0.2, 0, 6) -> (2.5, 0, 4), size x4. Fuel light: Explosion burst -> Fire, 8 x size x envelope.

3. What it looks like now (final build; "frame" = one captured frame, about 0.1 s)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------
- dusk HE (120 mm, size 0.6): flash frame: sparks and a dim graded pool at the impact (this run
  caught the light before the puffs); next frame: a fireball about four riflemen wide and two high,
  bright orange with yellow-white cores, dark soot already mottling it, a rounded foot on the ground
  (round 5: a flat orange slab cut off along the horizon); next frame: dark grey-brown soot; the
  column rises, its foot lingers; gone after about 38 frames (3.6 s, config). In game at 30 fps: one
  white-hot frame, one or two orange, soot by 0.2 s.
- night HE: a yellow-white fireball blooming in a large warm halo on the flash frame; next frame dark
  smoke with glowing orange edges; then pale moonlit grey smoke (config colour) for about 3.5 s.
- dusk grenade: a dim graded pool on the flash frame; its fireball (under 0.1 s) falls between
  captured frames; a small dust and smoke column.
- dusk HEAT (size 1.0): a wide, low orange-yellow fireball with hot cores for one frame, then a black
  soot ball rising.
- dusk fuel / night fuel: small cream puffs at the Jeep's hull for about 6 frames, then the black
  wreck column. The intended large rolling orange deflagration did NOT show (see weak points).

6. Weak points (honest)
-----------------------
1. Fuel: the deflagration fireball is still small cream puffs. The puff, cooling and light settings
   are in, but the puffs that read on screen look like the dark puffs lit by the fire light; I ran out
   of time to find why the fuel's fire puffs do not glow or grow (the SmokeSourceVehicle simulates at
   its own coarse step; its fire puffs may be few or hidden). Needs a probe.
2. Flash frame order: the combat light is drawn on the frame of the burst, the new puffs one frame
   later when the burst happens in the frame's last simulation step (dusk run): a graded pool and
   sparks without a fireball for one game frame.
3. Night bloom: the white-hot core blooms into a large yellow halo at night (the night exposure and
   white balance); a cap of 9 tamed it from 14, not enough. The night white balance turns 2500 K
   yellow, not white.
4. The ground pool at dusk still reads as a thin bright lens at grazing view on the flash frame
   (dimmer than round 5). Smoke self-shadowing was not done (smoke is lit by the fire inside it via
   round 4's CombatLitParticle only).
5. Smoke lifetime and colour (pale grey at night, gone in about 3.6 s) are config and other lanes.
6. LAW black lower half (LOOK/boom/rprobe/clips/both.png, +33 ms): sky and smoke draw, everything
   below the horizon is black: a terrain or ground pass missing for one frame, not the particle or
   flash path I touched. Not investigated.

1. Approach
-----------
New pure module engine/Poseidon/Graphics/Rendering/Effects/BlastLook.hpp/.cpp. It classifies every
explosion source from config data and holds one row per class. The existing systems are driven from
the row; nothing new is drawn:
- flash = the burst's combat light (CombatLights Explosion kind): new per-record span (the envelope
  is read at age / span) and the Explosion kind now honours the record tint; strength x flashPower.
- fireball = the CfgCloudlets Explosion puffs of the Explosion entity: emit time, puff life and fade,
  birth temperature and cooling rate (round 4's FireballPuff turns that into a black-body glow), puff
  size, source radius, climb; puffs are born in the upper half of the source (no flat cut) and the
  ball's centre is lifted half a radius.
- smoke column, dust skirt, haze = the crater's CraterSmoke1/2/3 sources: explicit colour (flat
  table), alpha, emit, puff life and fade, size, rise and stall; the column thickens only as the
  fireball cools (it hid the glow from the second frame on).
- thrown earth = the crater's ground-effect clods, count x debris.
- fuel tank = the FuelExplosion path of SmokeSourceVehicle: its fire puffs and dark soot puffs and
  its burst light from the FuelTank row.
Sizes and times scale with the cube root of the charge (Hopkinson-Cranz), clamped 0.5 .. 2.5.

Classification (ClassifyAmmo, config data only, so modded ammo falls in by its numbers):
- charge kg = 0.002 x indirectHit x indirectHitRange (grenade 0.25, 120 mm HE 2.4, satchel 9.8,
  LGB 105).
- shotShell: splash radius <= 2 m -> HEAT; else < 0.4 kg -> Grenade; else HE shell; >= 20 kg Bomb.
- shotMissile / shotRocket: >= 20 kg Bomb; splash radius <= 2.5 m -> RocketWarhead (shaped charge);
  else HE shell (Zuni, Rocket57).
- shotTimeBomb / shotPipeBomb -> Satchel; shotMine -> Mine; a vehicle's FuelExplosion -> FuelTank.
- An explosion that arrives without its munition (network, old save): by engine size (<= 0.3
  grenade, else HE shell).
- Ammo cook-off: not present in this engine (no code path); no row.

Where it is wired: Shots.cpp (all 7 `new Explosion` sites call SetBlastOf), Collisions.cpp
(ExplosionDammage's big crater: SetBlast + debris), Smokes.cpp (Explosion::SetBlast,
Crater::SetBlast, the fuel block in SmokeSourceVehicle::SimulateExplosion and its burst light),
Weapons.hpp (AmmoSimulation -> blast::AmmoKind), CombatLights (span, Explosion tint).

6. Cost
-------
Not timed with a frame counter (the captures are fixed-step with a screenshot per frame, so wall
time per clip is dominated by the screenshot writes; per run 158 to 164 s for both tests against
the before run's similar size). Particle counts per HE burst at full detail (generalize 1):
fireball about 120 puffs (was about 30) living about 2 s (was 0.4 s); column + skirt + haze about
40 puffs living 8 to 14 s (was about 25 living 2 to 3.4 s). A far burst gets 1/4 to 1/9 of that
(generalization). Fuel: about 150 fire puffs over 0.9 s and 60 soot puffs living 5 s. Overdraw
of large alpha puffs near the camera is the main risk for a heavy barrage; the light cost is
unchanged (one burst light per explosion, the fuel one lives about 2.7 s instead of 0.9 s).


7. Honest weak points
---------------------
1. The HE soot cloud in its first 0.2 s is boxy (a flat top and flat sides at f12 to f16): the
   fireball puffs fly outward horizontally at up to 25 m/s (WeaponCloudsSource) with no upward
   component and get stopped by side friction. A vertical bias at birth would round it.
2. The column detaches from the ground after about 2 s (its puffs keep climbing slowly and the
   skirt is faint); a real dust column keeps a dusty foot longer. Raise dustAlpha / dustLife or
   lower the column's floor climb further.
3. The fuel fireball reads cream-yellow, not deep orange: its own orange light on its soot
   saturates channel by channel toward white (CombatParticleChannel, the non-fire branch). It is
   also smaller than a real tank fireball at this distance.
4. Flash on the ground: the grenade still draws a flat bright ellipse on the ground for one frame,
   and the night HE flash's air glow has a straight lower edge at the horizon line. Both come from
   the post combat pass (EngineGL33_PostCombat surface reach and air scatter), which I did not
   touch; the flash now lasts as long as its row says, but its shape is the round 4 one.
5. At night dark smoke is invisible once the flash has gone (physically correct, but the HE then
   reads as a short event at night).
6. The LAW black-half frame (rprobe) path was not touched; rockets are classified but not shot.
7. bomb, satchel, mine and rocket_warhead rows are judged only by the unit tests and physics, not by
   eye.
8. The table lives in C++, not in config: a modded munition falls into a class by its numbers but
   cannot author its own row.

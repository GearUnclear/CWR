AGENT 1 REPORT: mist-band flicker, capture artefact or game bug?
================================================================
Shot: trailer scene 2, valley_mist ("Moving out at dawn"). Owner: "there is some
flickering obvious in the 'morning fog' shot. this MUST be fixed".

VERDICT: GAME BUG, also in real time. The capture's stepping cadence makes it worse
(about 1.6x larger frame-to-frame step), but it does not create it. A player at 60 fps
sees the whole mist/haze sheet "breathe" at a few Hz. Fix it in the engine, not in the
capture staging. Agent 3's per-cell fog jitter (psFogInject override) takes real-time
play down to the vanilla level.

----------------------
Scratch build mist-1 (detached worktree D:/Arma_CWA_worktrees/mist-1 at 907906c, built

    ACCELERATED sim step. It therefore plays at real speed in real time and exactly
    1/60 s per shot when stepped.
  - triMistRecWait / triMistRecState: wait (FAIL-retry) and status.

3. WHY (consistent with agents 2, 3 and 6)
------------------------------------------
The froxel fog injection jitters every cell by ONE frame-global offset (Weyl sequences of
_fog.sequence, EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp:689-692) and blends 0.12 of each frame into its
history (kFogHistoryShare). The history's residual of that global jitter is the same sign
over the whole mist, so the mist sheet swings as one. That residual exists at any frame
cadence; its amplitude is set by the history, not by the capture. A simple model (EMA 0.12
of the three Weyl sequences, then TAA 0.1) reproduces the measured shift: at one render
per frame most of the swing sits below 10 Hz (slow breathing a player sees), while ~6
renders per shot alias it into larger frame-to-frame steps (what the trailer shows).
That is why the capture is worse, and why no capture-only change can reach vanilla:
real-time play itself sits at 0.31 (3x vanilla, spikes every frame).


4. RECOMMENDATION
-----------------
- Treat it as a product bug: fix the fog jitter in the engine on graphics-remaster
  (decorrelate it per cell, as agent 3's percell override does), with a unit test, then
  reshoot valley_mist on both sides with the head recipe and recomposite.
- After the engine fix the capture cadence no longer matters much (agent 3: percell at
  the clip cadence 0.063 PNG; here percell at the 60 fps cadence 0.112 mkv). No extra
  warm-up frames are needed in the staging.
- The mist-1 recorder can verify the landed fix in real time: rebuild mist-1 on the fix
  commit (or apply mist1-recorder.patch to a scratch tree) and run look_probe_mist1_pc
  without the override.

AGENT 3 REPORT: the volumetric fog itself (mist swarm, valley_mist "MOVING OUT AT DAWN")
=====================================================================================
2026-10-23, agent 3 of the mist swarm. Folder: graphics-remaster/tmp/video/mist/3/

VERDICT
-------
Confirmed, with a causal test. The flicker in the dawn mist comes from the froxel fog's
injection jitter. Every froxel cell gets the SAME sub-cell offset each frame, in x, y and depth.
The offsets are uniforms set once per frame (EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp:689-692: jz, jx and jy are
golden-ratio sequences of the frame index, used in psFogInject's fogSlice and main). The history
takes a 0.12 share of each new frame (kFogHistoryShare), so 0.88 of the old jitter state is still
there next frame. That leftover is the same over whole regions in the same frame. In the far
slices (hundreds of metres deep at zFar 4000) a depth offset of a few per cent of a slice moves
every sample along a downward ray in the same direction, deeper into or out of the mist. The haze
and mist opacity over the whole valley, bay and far hills then swings as one sheet from frame to
frame. Land brightens toward the air colour, and the sky just above the horizon dims a little
(agent 4 saw that anti-swing independently).

It is temporal noise, not camera motion. With the camera held still the flicker stays (band
0.468 against 0.517 under the pan). The far ring's tap stepping and the fog's reprojection add
only about 10% while panning.

It is a game bug, not a capture artefact. Each rendered frame draws a new jitter whatever the
simulation step is, so play shows it too. Agents 1 and 2 measured it at a real-time 60 fps
cadence: band 0.27 to 0.31, against 0.106 for vanilla. The capture cadence (about 8 renders per
shot) roughly doubles what is visible.

Fix at the cause, in the engine: give each cell its own jitter (details below). Measured with
a shader override on the trailer's build: the pump is gone (band 0.063 on the moving probe,
0.056 static), no grain or curtains are added, and the look barely changes.

RULED OUT OR MINOR ON MY ANGLE
------------------------------
  - Far ring stepping (the round 3 note: the freshest tap with 0.1 weight steps): minor. The pan
    adds about 0.05 to the base (0.468 -> 0.517) and 0.007 with per-cell (0.056 -> 0.063).
    That bounds the ring and reprojection together. In this shot every azimuth in view sees sky
    above land, so the centre tap is always fresh (ringSure 1).
  - Fog reprojection while the camera moves: inside the same small bound.
  - The valley mist density field (kMistSigma, noise banks): it is the medium that the jitter
    samples, not a source of flicker by itself. With the jitter decorrelated it is calm.
    Agent 6: fogMist=0 leaves 0.347 (haze alone still pumps), and fogDensity=0 gives 0.039.
  - Froxel depth slicing near the horizon: this is the mechanism's lever (the far slices are deep,
    so a coherent depth offset moves a lot of air), not a separate defect.

THE FIX (at the cause; engine, graphics-remaster; the lead applies it)
------------------------------------------------------------------------
psFogInject, kPostFogInjectMainGLSL in EngineGL33_PostFogShaders.cpp. Keep the frame's
golden-ratio offset, but turn it per cell with an interleaved-gradient offset of the cell (and,
in depth, also per slice):
    float fogCellNoise(vec2 p) { return fract(52.9829189 * fract(dot(p, vec2(0.06711056, 0.00583715)))); }
    fogSlice:  float z = fogSliceDepth((s + fract(fogDepth.w + g_cellJitter + 0.618034 * s)) / fogGrid.z);
    main:      g_cellJitter = fogCellNoise(gl_FragCoord.xy);
               vec2 cellJ = fract(fogFrame.xy + 0.5 + vec2(fogCellNoise(gl_FragCoord.xy + vec2(17.0, 59.0)),
                                                           fogCellNoise(gl_FragCoord.xy + vec2(83.0, 29.0)))) - 0.5;
               vec2 ndc = (gl_FragCoord.xy + cellJ) / fogGrid.xy * 2.0 - 1.0;
No C++ change, no new uniforms, no extra cost beyond three hashes per cell.
  Ready diffs (reference copies, NOT applied anywhere and NOT compiled):
    patch/fog_cell_jitter_shader.diff   the shader change with comments. The assembled result
                                        equals the tested override shaders/percell/psFogInject.glsl
                                        except for names, comments and line wrapping (checked).
    patch/fog_cell_jitter_test.diff     a new [remaster][fog][Shaders] test in

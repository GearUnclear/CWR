VERDICT
-------
Confirmed. The band flicker lives entirely in the volumetric fog's medium sampling (volFog, psFogInject).
Every way of removing the froxel medium kills it (no air 0.035, B8 air pass instead 0.028, empty medium with
the stage still running 0.039, injection jitter frozen under the real pan 0.038), while every other active
feature is ruled out (TAA, GTAO, exposure/meter, the whole photoreal camera, sun in the air, every shadow,
the dawn phase gain, the far ring). The valley mist is not special: the haze alone still flickers (0.347).
Agent 3's causal result (frame-global jitter of the froxel injection under the 0.12 history) is the
mechanism; my axis split shows the DEPTH jitter carries about 80% of it.
Agents 1 and 3 report it also shows in real-time play (rt60 0.308 vs vanilla 0.106), so it is a game bug:
fix in the engine with a unit test, not in the capture.

What the table says
- The froxel fog volume is necessary: B8's air pass draws THICKER haze over this valley (look/
  crop_base_b8air_nodens_f0328.png) and does not flicker, so thick air is not flickery in itself.
- The fog stage's machinery without a medium (ring, history buffers, near field, apply) is clean (nodens).
- Under the real pan, with only the injection jitter removed, the band sits on the no-air floor (frozenmove
  0.038 vs 0.035): the far ring stepping (round 3 critic, handoff hypothesis 2), the history reprojection,
  the near-field beams and the ring's phase gain add nothing measurable. Ring hypothesis ruled out.
- Axis split: the frame-global DEPTH jitter (jz -> fogDepth.w, fogSlice "s + fogDepth.w") carries about 80%
  of the band rms, the xy jitter (fogFrame.xy) the rest; they add roughly in power
  (sqrt(0.426^2 + 0.147^2) = 0.45 vs 0.506). Both axes need per-cell decorrelation.
- TAA, the sun's shade and the dawn phase gain all DAMP the sheet (turning them off makes it worse); they are
  not sources.

RECOMMENDED FIX (for the lead; engine, it is the product)
---------------------------------------------------------
In psFogInject (EngineGL33_PostFogShaders.cpp kPostFogInjectMainGLSL, fogSlice and main) decorrelate the
sub-cell jitter PER CELL on both axes, keeping the frame's golden-ratio sequence: add a per-cell blue-noise /
interleaved-gradient offset of the cell (and slice) to fogDepth.w and fogFrame.xy, as agent 3's
mist/3/shaders/percell/psFogInject.glsl does (moving 0.063, real-time 0.112, 0 spikes). The depth axis
matters most; do not fix xy alone. Add a unit test that the injection's jitter offset differs between
neighbouring cells within one frame (or that the per-frame mean of the offsets over a block is near zero),
then reshoot valley_mist with the head recipe.

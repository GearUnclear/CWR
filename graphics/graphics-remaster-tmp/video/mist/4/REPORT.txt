Mist flicker swarm, agent 4: exposure, metering and the sun
=============================================================
Shot: trailer scene 2, valley_mist ("Moving out at dawn"), overhaul side.
Angle: the camera meter (PhotoCamera, SkyMeterLight, the sun-ring and highlight
guards, eye adaptation under the exact 1/60 s step with accTime 0 between
shots), the round 5 dawn haze (the ring's haze at each ray's own phase angle),
the white sun disc and its aureole, the sky roll-off toward the sun.

VERDICT: ruled out. None of these parts causes the visible flicker. The flicker
lives entirely in the air (agent 6's noair: band 0.035), it is one coherent
opacity-like swing of the haze over the land, and the camera and sun parts
either leave it unchanged (exposure) or damp it (the dawn phase gain, and the
sun terms per agent 5).

1. What the camera does per shot (read from the code, measured from the log)
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
- EngineGL33_Camera.cpp CameraAdapt: dt is WALL CLOCK (NowSeconds), clamped to
  0.25 s per rendered frame. The capture renders 7 to 10 frames per shot
  (frame= counter in the log, mean 8.2) in about 0.64 s of wall time, so each
  captured frame moves the exposure about 47% of the way to the meter's target
  (real-time play at 60 fps: 1.6% per frame). The capture therefore makes the
  exposure follow the meter's noise more closely than in play.
- The trailer clip's own capture log already records the meter per shot
  (udcap.log, feat= ... "cam ev=... target=... ring=... ringw=..."; parsed into
  mist/4/cam_ud_v2.csv by mist/4/parse_cam.py). Over the 570 shots: ev 0.39 to
  0.14, a smooth drift; per-shot |d ev| <= 0.01 EV (the print resolution);
  target 0.14..0.39; the sun-ring guard is fully on (ringw = 1.00, sun 11.9 deg),
  ring 0.40..0.65, skytop 0.34..0.58.

2. Exposure: ruled out (statistically and causally)
---------------------------------------------------
Offline, the trailer clip (mist/4/analyse.py on a 480x270 decode, 15 px blocks
as bandmetric.py):
- The band flicker is not a global gain. The coherent (block-mean) d2 of the
  core band correlates with the bay +0.97 and the far haze +0.63, but with the
  upper sky +0.06, the sky by the sun -0.01 and the near rock +0.18.
- In log2 units (exposure is multiplicative): coherent d2 rms x1000 is band
  5.9, bay 9.2, upper sky 0.41, sky by the sun 0.24. Any exposure jitter is
  under about 1/15 of the band's swing.
- corr(|band d2| per frame, |ev d2|) = 0.00; with |target d1| 0.05; with the
  ring guard 0.03.
Causal probe look_probe_mist4_frozen (agent 6's cut of frames 240..389, mode
"remastered,camAdapt=100000"; mist/4/runs/frozen/):
- The log shows ev = pre = 0.40 on all 120 measured shots while the target still
  wandered 0.25..0.32 (agent 6's base probe: ev 0.19..0.23).
- bandmetric --from 30: band 0.485, band_max 1.91, spikes 5308 (base 0.506,
  2.02, 5568). Core coherent d2 rms 0.635 vs 0.648. The horizon sky still swings
  against the mist (corr -0.55 vs -0.62). Unchanged within noise.
- Secondary: bandmetric's sky value fell 0.034 to 0.022 (vanilla 0.025); the
  coherent sky d2 0.039 to 0.032. The exposure's wall-clock tracking of the
  meter adds a little of the sky's grain. The rest of the sky's common mode
  moves with the rock (corr +0.94) and is on vanilla too (+0.83): most likely
  the pan's step, not the camera.

3. The sun disc, the aureole and the sky roll-off toward the sun: ruled out
---------------------------------------------------------------------------
On the trailer clip, the sun found per frame (centroid of the near-white pixels
in the top 60%):
- Aureole rings round the sun (4-8, 8-16, 16-32 and 32-64 px at 480 wide):
  d2 rms 0.13 / 0.07 / 0.04 / 0.04 levels, steadier than vanilla's
  0.22 / 0.15 / 0.03 / 0.02. The sky by the sun: block d2 rms 0.042 (vanilla
  0.037).
- The band does not follow them. corr(band coherent d2, aureole ring d2) is
  +0.18 / +0.19 / +0.14 / -0.10; with the sun's screen-x d2 +0.09; |band d2|
  against |sun x d2| +0.01.
- nophoto probe (photoLight=0: the whole camera, the tone curve, the roll-off
  and the white disc off): see section 6.

4. The round 5 dawn haze (the ring's phase gain at each ray's own angle): ruled out, it damps
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Probe look_probe_mist4_nophase: base mode plus a psFogInject override with
"float phaseOn = 0.0;" (built on agent 3's assembled base; the game log confirms
"shader override loaded ... mist\4\shaders\nophase\psFogInject.glsl").
- bandmetric --from 30: band 0.672, band_max 2.67, spikes 6469 (base 0.506,
  2.02, 5568). Core coherent d2 rms 0.974 vs 0.648.
- Core level 147 vs 131: without the gain the cream veil over the bay returns.
- Same mode as the base, only bigger. This matches agent 5's fogSun=0 result
  (band 0.794). The swing grows with how bright the air is against the land.
- Code note: the gain's column angle is jittered (psFogInject:
  ndc = (gl_FragCoord.xy + fogFrame.xy) / fogGrid.xy), so it takes the
  frame-global jitter like every other term. Removing it does not remove the
  swing.
- Crop sheet mist/4/look_base_nophase.png. Rows: base, frozen exposure,
  nophase. Columns: f0327, f0328, and their diff x8. The frozen row's diff shows
  the coherent sheet over the valley clearly (a mean step of 2.6 levels in the
  crop).

5. The shape of the flicker (offline, for the lead)
---------------------------------------------------
mist/4/analyse2.py on the trailer clip:
- It is ONE coherent mode, and it is continuous. The core (rows 9-12, cols
  16-31) has a block-mean d2 with median 0.58 levels; 350 of 568 frames are
  over 0.5. These are not isolated events.
- Per-block slope onto the mode: terrain under the haze (rows 8-13, x > 40%)
  +0.5 to +1.1. The sky JUST above the horizon on the sun's side (rows 6-8,
  cols 18-31) moves the opposite way (-0.03 to -0.14). The upper sky is 0.00.
  This is the signature of a swing in the haze's opacity (more air: the darker
  land brightens and the brighter horizon sky dims toward the air's colour). An
  exposure change or an in-scatter gain would move both the same way.
- Spectrum: no dominant period (the largest peaks are near 11 and 9.3 frames,
  8% of the power each). The autocorrelation of d2 at lag 1 is -0.63
  (frame-to-frame alternation).
- This fits agent 3's finding: one jitter (jx, jy, jz) shared by every froxel
  cell (EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp ~700), blended at kFogHistoryShare 0.12, with
  about 8 renders per shot.

6. The whole photoreal camera off (photoLight=0): ruled out
-----------------------------------------------------------
Probe look_probe_mist4_nophoto (mode "remastered,photoLight=0"; the log's gate
reads photoLight=0:off:switch-off with volFog still on; mist/4/runs/nophoto/).
This turns off the meter, the guards, the tone curve, the sky roll-off toward
the sun and the white sun disc.
- bandmetric --from 30: band 0.511, band_max 2.01, spikes 5985 (base 0.506,
  2.02, 5568).
- Core coherent d2 rms 0.677 vs 0.648, at a brighter core level (158 vs 131).
  The relative swing is 0.43% vs 0.49%.
- The horizon sky still swings against the mist (corr -0.60).
- Crops: mist/4/look_nophoto.png.
Visual check of the sun at 2x on the trailer clip's worst frames 326-329
(mist/4/look_sun_f326_329_2x.png): the disc and the aureole show only the pan's
edge (mean steps -0.03, +0.04, +0.01 levels). Over the same frames the band
crop (x1080-1440, y540-780) steps +2.31, -1.27, +3.16.

7. Secondary: the sky's fine grain is the exposure (a capture artefact)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------
bandmetric's sky value is 0.034 on the base probe. It drops to 0.022 both with
the exposure frozen and with photoLight=0 (vanilla 0.025). The sky's extra grain,
2x to 3x vanilla in the orchestrator's baseline, is the exposure following the
meter's frame-to-frame noise: CameraAdapt's dt is wall time, about 47% of the
way to the target per captured shot. In real-time play (dt 1/60) it is 1.6%
per frame. It is small, and it is a capture artefact: the game itself needs no
change.
Capture-only option, if the lead wants it: in the clip's capMode string, scale
camAdapt so a shot's wall time counts as 1/60 s, i.e. camAdapt = 1.0 x (wall
seconds per shot x 60), about 38 (0.64 s per shot). Expect the sky grain near
vanilla's and the exposure drift close to real-time play. This is not
measured; the frozen case (camAdapt=100000) is the measured bound. A fix with
one render per sim step (agent 2's "rt" direction) would fix this as well.

8. Conclusion for the lead
--------------------------
- capture_or_game for my angle: the camera, the meter and the sun parts are
  not the cause, in the capture or in the game.
- The band flicker is the fog. Agent 3 confirmed it causally: froxels sampled
  at their cell centre give band 0.024 against 0.468 on the same static probe.
  The cause is one jitter shared by every froxel cell (EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp
  ~700: jx, jy, jz), blended at 0.12 into history and pumped by about 8 renders
  per shot. Two things in my measurements point there:
  - The anti-swing of the horizon sky is an opacity signature.
  - The swing grows when the air is brighter against the land: nophase gave
    0.974 and fogSun=0 (agent 5) 0.691 against a base of 0.648 / 0.391.
- Do not change the exposure, the guards, the sun disc, the roll-off or the
  round 5 phase gain to fix this shot. The phase gain and the sun terms damp
  the swing.

graphics: the fog's jitter turns per cell, so the dawn valley's haze no longer pumps

The froxel fog sampled every cell of the volume at the same sub-cell offset
each frame (one golden-ratio jitter for the whole frame, jx/jy/jz in
EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp) and kept 0.88 of the last frame. The history's
leftover of that jitter then had the same sign over whole regions, so the far
haze and the valley mist over the dawn valley brightened and darkened as one
sheet from frame to frame. It showed in play at 60 fps (3x vanilla), and the
trailer's stepped capture made it about twice as strong ("Moving out at dawn").

psFogInject now turns the frame's offset per cell with an interleaved-gradient
offset of the cell, and in depth also per slice, so neighbouring cells'
residuals are independent and average out in the apply and the TAA. The
frame's sequence, the history share and every uniform stay as they were; no
C++ change beyond a comment.

Measured on the trailer build with the clip's own staging (frames 270-389,
bandmetric): band 0.506 -> 0.064, spikes 5568 -> 1 (vanilla 0.102, 0). At
the froxel cell scale the frame-to-frame change is below vanilla's. The look
is unchanged apart from a hair more mist (+1.6/255 in the band).

New test: [remaster][fog][Shaders] pins the per-cell jitter in the shader and
checks with a C++ mirror that a 5 x 5 cell block's history residual drops
from 0.031 to under a quarter of that. Unit gate 347/347.

MIST FLICKER: DECISION (lead of the mist swarm, 2026-10-23)
============================================================
Owner's symptom: "there is some flickering obvious in the 'morning fog' shot. this MUST be
fixed". Shot: trailer scene 2, clip valley_mist, "MOVING OUT AT DAWN".

VERDICT: a REAL GAME BUG in the overhaul's volumetric fog. It also shows in normal play at
60 fps. The trailer's stepped capture makes it about 1.6x to 2x stronger, but it does not
create it. Fixed in the engine (fix.patch, with a unit test). No change to the capture
staging and none to vanilla.


1. THE CAUSE IN PLAIN WORDS
---------------------------
The volumetric fog is computed in a coarse 3D grid of cells (160 x 90 cells, 64 depth
slices at 1080p). Each frame it samples one point inside every cell and blends it with the
last frame (it keeps 88% of the old value), so over time the samples average out. The bug:
every cell in the whole grid sampled the SAME point of its cell each frame (one jitter
offset for the whole frame). The history's leftover of that jitter therefore had the same
sign everywhere at once, so the far haze and the valley mist over the dawn valley, the bay
and the far hills got a little thicker, then a little thinner, as one sheet, frame after
frame. That is the "breathing" the owner saw. Most of it comes from the depth part of the
jitter (deep far slices are hundreds of metres long, so the sample point moves a lot).

The fix keeps the frame's jitter sequence but turns it per cell (and per slice in depth)
with a fixed interleaved-gradient offset of the cell. Neighbouring cells now sample
different points, their leftovers are independent, and they average out on screen.

Why the capture made it worse: each shot of the trailer renders 7 to 10 frames at the held
pose, so the jitter history runs 7 to 10 steps per video frame and the slow part of the
pump aliases into frame-to-frame jumps. That only amplifies the bug; with the fix in, the
capture cadence no longer matters (below).

3. THE FIX
----------
tmp/video/mist/lead/fix.patch (git diff --binary; applies cleanly to graphics-remaster
907906c; no commit, no branch). Suggested commit message: fix.commitmsg.txt.
  - arma_CWR/engine/PoseidonGL33/EngineGL33_PostFogShaders.cpp (psFogInject): new
    fogCellNoise (interleaved-gradient hash of the cell); fogSlice samples depth at
    s + fract(fogDepth.w + g_cellJitter + 0.618034 s); main jitters xy by
    fract(fogFrame.xy + 0.5 + per-cell offsets) - 0.5. Same sequence, same history share,
    same uniforms. Agent 3's diff, unchanged.
  - arma_CWR/engine/PoseidonGL33/EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp: a three-line comment at the jitter
    source pointing at the per-cell turn (no code change).
  - arma_CWR/tests/unit/engine/Poseidon/Graphics/test_volumetric_fog.cpp: new test
    "Volumetric fog: the injection's jitter is turned per cell, so its history leaves no
    frame-wide pump" [remaster][fog][Shaders]: pins the per-cell strings, forbids the old
    frame-global forms, and a C++ mirror checks a 5 x 5 cell block's history residual drops
    from about 0.031 slice to under a quarter of that.

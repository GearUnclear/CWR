AGENT 5 REPORT: sun shafts and shadows in the "Moving out at dawn" flicker
=========================================================================
Swarm: tmp/video/mist (board BOARD.txt). Shot: trailer scene 2, valley_mist.
Angle: the froxel sun shafts (sampling, noise, temporal filter), the sun
shadow maps (cascade splits, texel snap, cascade selection while the camera
pans), and the shadowed mist (the fog reading the shadow maps, the canopy map
and the terrain horizon map).

VERDICT: RULED OUT. Neither the sun's light in the air (shafts, sun lobe,
shadowed mist) nor any shadow (cascades, PCSS, canopy map, horizon map) is a
source of the band flicker. Switching them off makes the flicker WORSE, not
better: the sun's shading of the mist only damps a sheet that comes from
elsewhere (agent 3: the froxel injection's frame-global jitter). Do not "fix"
the shot by turning shafts or shadows off.

   Why switching them off makes it worse: the flicker is an opacity /
   in-scatter swing of the whole medium (agents 3 and 4). Its visible size
   follows the contrast between the air's light and the land behind it. The
   sun term shades the mist (lit = 0.35 + 0.65 x vis x tSun in fogCell;
   tSun = exp(-tauUp / sin 11.9 deg) is small inside the mist), so in the
   base the mist's light sits closer to the dark land. Remove the shade
   (noshade) or the whole sun term (nosun, lit = 1) and the mist gets
   brighter against the land, so the same swing shows bigger. Agent 4 saw
   the same with the dawn ring phase gain off (0.672).

2. SHADOW MAPS (code, graphics-remaster 907906c; logged triShadowTuning)
   - Tuning logged in the probe: enabled, 4 cascades, omni 2 (0.08 / 0.20),
     dist 1.0, split 0.90, fade 40 m, res 2048. View distance 4000 gives
     shadowsZ 500 m (ViewDistanceResolver: 5/18 of the view, capped at 500).
   - Tiers (ShadowMath BuildShadowCascadesTiered): omni spheres of 30.7 m and
     61.4 m (capped by kOmniTexelBase), frustum tiers 61.4-185.8 m and
     185.8-500 m (PSSM lambda 0.9), far fade 460-500 m into the canopy map.
     Fits are bounding spheres (rotation invariant) snapped to a world texel
     grid in double, so a pan cannot crawl them. The far valley, the bay and
     the spit (kilometres out) lie beyond the last split: fogShadowMap returns
     1 there, and the surface takes the canopy map, which is world-locked and
     rebuilt only when the sun or the camera moves far (Canopy.hpp), and the
     static terrain horizon map. Only the slope just below the ridge can sit
     in the last tier and its fade.
   - Agent 6's noair (band 0.035 on the moving cut with all of this on) shows
     the cascades, PCSS (4 taps turned per TAA frame), texel snap and tier
     selection add nothing visible in the band on their own.
   - The fog's own read (psFogInject fogShadowMap: one 2x2 hardware compare
     of the cascade at the cell's jittered point, no PCF; canopySunVisibility;
     fogTerrainSun) is a jittered binary-ish sample too, but it only reaches
     the first 500 m (cascades) or the air under 48 m above the ground
     (canopy), and switching it off (noshade 0.575, fogvis1 0.583) did not
     lower the flicker.

3. SHAFTS (near-field pass psFogNear, EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp)
   - Half resolution, march of the first 180 m (kFogBeamReach), 20 steps at
     the shipped quality, start offset by interleaved-gradient noise turned
     every rendered frame (seq % 64), lifted by pow(lit, 0.4), depth-aware
     upsampled in the apply, averaged only by TAA. It marches only wet air
     (c.wet: ground haze, weather fog, mist), which is near zero 300+ m above
     the valley floor along the band's rays. Its noise is per pixel, so it
     averages out over a 60 px block and cannot make a coherent sheet.
   - Bounded by agent 3's frozen-jitter run: with the beams on and only the
     froxel injection's jitter frozen, the band falls to 0.024. I dropped my
     own beams-only probe (nobeams) as redundant to free a game slot; its
     override is left in shaders/nobeams/psFogNear.glsl.

4. WHAT THIS MEANS FOR THE FIX
   - The cause sits in the froxel injection's sampling (agent 3: one jitter
     (jx, jy, jz) shared by every cell per frame, blended 0.12 into the
     history, EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp about line 689 and psFogInject
     fogSlice/main). Sun, shafts and shadows are innocent.
   - Any fix should be judged with the sun and shadows ON (the shipped look):
     they lower the flicker, so a test with them off overstates it.
   - The nosun and noshade numbers give the lead a stress case: a fix that
     holds the band near vanilla on nosun (the brightest mist against the
     land) has margin on the real shot.

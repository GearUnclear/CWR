Uslu dur! trailer, part 1: graphics. Clip list (director's cut, 2026-10-19)
=============================================================================

Every scene is a moment from the Uslu dur! world (Guerrilla Mode: a resistance
fighting an occupying army across Malden, Everon and modded islands such as
Sinai), staged so one lighting feature shows. Base-game units and vehicles
only (resistance SoldierG*, occupier SoldierE*, BMP, UAZ, Ural, Fortress
bunkers), LoBo's own set on Sinai, so the vanilla game shows the same scene.
Men are captive and posed; vehicles move only by teleport. Head fix
(tmp/video/heads/RECIPE.txt): every frame advances the world by exactly 1/60 s on
both sides (setAccTime 0 between shots, one tick per shot), and every posed man
holds his gaze 300 m straight ahead (doWatch in the staging block); fire, smoke,
flares and shells now play at real speed. Kneeling men use switchMove "Crouch".

Near-object check (2026-10-19, the owner saw a blurry birch trunk fill half the
frame in town_patrol): every clip was scanned on both sides for anything within
about 2 m of the camera filling a large part of the frame. Fixed: town_patrol
(the path ran through the birch and past a house corner), rain_town (a cypress
by that corner filled a quarter of the frame) and supply_run (the camera eases
in and out while the car kept an even speed, so the camera caught up with the
car and passed through it mid-clip; the car now follows the camera's pace).
Those three were reshot on both sides with the head fix. The rest keep at
least a few metres to anything that fills the frame.

Both sides: the same spot, date and time, camera path, props, events and frame
count; 1920x1080 at 60 frames a second (the sim clock pinned to k/60 s per
frame); S mode. Left: the vanilla upstream game (Bohemia 3.05,
vanilla-upstream\dist\vanilla-upstream-rwdi). Right: Uslu dur! with every
feature on (look-director\arma_CWR\dist\look-director-rwdi, round 5 plus the
owner's picks). Fire, smoke, rain, flares and the shell bursts may differ
between the halves.

The master is one full 16:9 frame per moment: vanilla left of a plain thin
divider line, Uslu dur! right of it, small upper-case labels riding the line.
Calm throughout (the owner: "cool calm and composed"). Every scene is one full
sweep, edge to edge (the owner: "let the wipes go across the whole screen"): it
opens fully vanilla, the line eases in from the right edge to a split in about
2.5 s, pauses there, then eases on out past the left edge, and the scene ends
fully Uslu dur!, so each scene is seen whole in both versions. Long dissolves,
soft slow wipes that cross the whole frame and slow dips through black join the
scenes. The labels ride the line and stop at the 5% safe margin at the edges. Titles are quiet lower-thirds (Oswald over Roboto, the
game's own OFL fonts); the opening's line is typed on like a field report in
the game's mono font with a HUD-green cursor. The only accent colour is the
game's HUD green (63, 148, 61), sampled from the vanilla HUD text
(trailer_v2.py).

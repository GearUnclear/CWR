 0  Title card
    Uslu dur is a total overhaul of Arma: Cold War Assault. First up: the graphics and rendering improvements.

 1  Malden at sunset, occupied
    The original game is on the left, Uslu dur is on the right. Same models and textures on both.

 2  Dawn, squad on the ridge
    Fog is volumetric now. The mist sits low in the valley, and the sun lights it from behind.

 3  Pine woods
    Same thing in the woods: sunlight breaking through the trees makes proper light shafts in the haze.

 4  Bay, patrol boat
    Water has real reflections now, so the hills and the patrol boat show up in the bay.

 5  Occupier outpost
    Every object casts a real shadow now, and ambient occlusion darkens the corners and the ground under the vehicles.

 6  Everon from the air
    Clouds cast shadows on the ground too, and you can watch them drift across Everon.

 7  Sinai camp
    It works with mods too. This is Sinai, from the Lost Brothers mod.

 8  Ambush at golden hour
    At golden hour the shadows get long. Here the resistance is set up at the treeline, waiting.

 9  Convoy at sunset
    An occupier convoy, driving into the sun. Exposure now works like a real camera's.

10  Checkpoint at dusk
    At the checkpoint, the campfire now actually lights the guards around it.

11  Night patrol in town
    At night, the street lamps actually light the street. Here an occupier patrol walks through town.

12  Town in the rain
    In the rain the ground gets darker, and puddles pick up the street lights.

13  Supply run at night
    Out of town it's properly dark, so headlights are all you've got.

14  Firefight after dark
    Flares, fires and explosions all light up their surroundings, and the size of a blast now depends on the shell.

15  Closing card
    That's it for the graphics. Part two covers the gameplay: Guerrilla Mode and the new AI.

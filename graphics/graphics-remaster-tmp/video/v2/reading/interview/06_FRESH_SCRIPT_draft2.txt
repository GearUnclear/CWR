 0  Title card
    Uslu dur is a total overhaul of Arma: Cold War Assault. This video covers the graphics and rendering improvements.

 1  Malden at sunset, occupied
    Original game on the left, Uslu dur on the right. The models and textures are unchanged.

 2  Dawn, squad on the ridge
    The fog is volumetric. Mist settles in the valley, and the rising sun shines through it.

 3  Pine woods
    In the pine woods, sunlight comes through the trees as visible shafts in the haze.

 4  Bay, patrol boat
    The water reflects its surroundings, including the hills and the patrol boat.

 5  Occupier outpost
    The outpost is lit by a low winter sun. Ambient occlusion darkens the corners and the ground under the vehicles.

 6  Everon from the air
    From the air you can see the cloud shadows moving across Everon.

 7  Sinai camp
    It also works with mods. This is the Sinai map from the Lost Brothers mod.

 8  Ambush at golden hour
    At golden hour the trees cast long shadows over the resistance waiting at the forest edge.

 9  Convoy at sunset
    An occupier convoy drives into the setting sun. Exposure is handled like a real camera.

10  Checkpoint at dusk
    At the checkpoint, the fire lights the sentries standing around it.

11  Night patrol in town
    At night, street lamps light the town as an occupier patrol walks through.

12  Town in the rain
    In the rain, wet ground turns darker and the puddles reflect the street lights.

13  Supply run at night
    Outside town there are no street lights, and the supply run drives on its headlights.

14  Firefight after dark
    Flares, fires and explosions light up their surroundings. The size of each blast depends on the munition.

15  Closing card
    Part two will cover the gameplay: Guerrilla Mode and the AI.

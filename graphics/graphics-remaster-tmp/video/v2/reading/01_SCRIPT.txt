How to read it
- Calm, measured, warm, understated. Like telling a friend about something you made.
  No announcer voice, no hype, no big build-ups.
- A little slower than normal talking. Let full stops land.
- Do not watch a clock. The "about N s" below is only a guide. I fit the picture
  to your voice: if a line runs long, its scene simply stays on screen longer.
- Say the number before each line (for example "five"), pause, then read the line.
  That lets me cut the recording into lines cleanly.

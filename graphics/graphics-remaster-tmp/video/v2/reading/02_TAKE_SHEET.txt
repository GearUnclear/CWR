TAKE SHEET: recording the narration in Audacity
================================================

Before you start
- Quiet room. Phone on silent. Fan, heater or fridge off if you can.
- Mic about a hand-span away (15 to 20 cm), a little to the side of your mouth so
  "p" and "b" do not pop. Keep the same distance for the whole session.
- Audacity:
  - Project rate (bottom left): 48000 Hz.
  - Recording channels: 1 (Mono).
  - Speak a test line and watch the meter: loud words should reach about -12 to -6 dB.
    It must never touch 0 (that clips and cannot be fixed).

Recording
1. Press record and stay silent for 5 seconds (room tone; I use it to clean the noise).
2. For each line: say its number ("one"), pause a second, then read the line
   2 or 3 times with about 2 seconds of silence between takes.
   - Take 1: as written.
   - Take 2: a little slower and softer.
   - Take 3: only if something felt off.
3. A slip is fine: pause, then start that sentence again. No need to stop recording.
4. One long recording is fine. Separate files per line are fine too.

Export
- File > Export > Export as WAV.
- Encoding: Signed 24-bit PCM (16-bit is fine if 24 is not offered). Mono, 48000 Hz.
- Save into:
    D:\Arma_CWA_worktrees\graphics-remaster\tmp\video\v2\reading\recordings\
  (any file names) and tell me when it is there.

What happens next
I cut it into lines, pick the takes, clean it lightly (low rumble filter, a little
noise reduction from the room tone, soft "s" control, gentle compression), fit the
picture to your timing, level it to YouTube loudness (-16 LUFS) and send you a
phone-sized preview. Your voice is never sped up or pitched.

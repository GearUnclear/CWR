Style notes from two official Rockstar gameplay videos (GTA V, 2013; RDR2 part 1, 2018)
Source: transcripts the owner pasted into chat on 2026-10-19. No Rockstar text is stored here.

- Register: a calm, measured documentary narrator, not an advert announcer. Confident, polished,
  unhurried; the narration states what the player gets and lets the footage sell it.
- Opening: one or two sentences that name the game and what it sets out to do.
- Body lines: each names a feature through concrete things the viewer sees, often as a rolling
  list of nouns, and often closes on a pairing of opposites. Lines run roughly 5 to 10 seconds.
- Humour: dry understatement, rarely, and never a punchline from the narrator. The jokes come from
  in-game character lines placed between narration lines. (Our video has no character lines, so
  at most one or two dry asides.)
- Pacing: narration fills roughly half the running time; footage and game sound breathe between lines.
- Close: a forward-looking line promising the next video and what it will cover.
- Our version: Uslu dur! trailer part 1 (graphics only), part 2 promises gameplay. Original
  wording only; never reuse Rockstar's or GTA's lines, characters or brands.

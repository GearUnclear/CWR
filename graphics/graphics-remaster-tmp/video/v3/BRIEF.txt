USLU DUR! TRAILER, PART 1: GRAPHICS - the brief every agent follows (orchestrator, 2026-10-19)

WHAT
A YouTube trailer for Uslu dur!, a total overhaul of Arma: Cold War Assault. Part 1 shows only
the new graphics (lighting, air, water, night, rain, explosions); part 2 (later) shows the
gameplay: Guerrilla Mode (a persistent insurgency: a resistance against an occupying army across
islands; towns, roadblocks, convoys, hidden camps, civilians) and the AI. About 2 to 2.5 minutes.

THE OWNER'S DIRECTIONS (all binding)
1. Every scene shows the vanilla game and Uslu dur! of the SAME camera shot at the SAME moment:
   vanilla = a build of the vanilla upstream repo; Uslu dur! = our final build with everything on.
2. Scenes come from the Uslu dur world and are cool to look at: resistance fighters, the
   occupier, checkpoints and roadblocks, convoys, occupied towns, hidden camps, firefights.
   Each scene also shows off one lighting feature (dawn mist, sun shafts, golden-hour shadows,
   sunset over the sea, lamplit night town, rain and puddles, headlights under stars, fire and
   explosions, desert light, clouds and their shadows).
3. 60 fps on both sides. Deliver a 16:9 YouTube master (1920x1080 60 fps; 3840x2160 if the
   panels stay sharp): H.264 High, 12 to 20 Mbps or CRF 16 to 18, yuv420p, faststart; audio
   AAC 48 kHz stereo 320 kbps or more, -16 LUFS integrated, true peak under -1 dBTP; an SRT and a
   subtitled copy. A 3840x1080 full side-by-side is a secondary file only.
4. NEVER shrink two 16:9 frames into one 16:9 frame. Both shots are full-size frames of the same
   camera, composited in ONE full-size frame with a moving divider: a split screen.
5. Mood: cool, calm and composed. Wipes and transitions are slow and eased (no snaps, no
   bounces); soft dissolves or gentle directional wipes between scenes; slow dips through black;
   no flash cuts, no light leaks, no shakes. Let shots breathe; one idea per scene at most. Even
   the explosion scene enters calmly; the explosion is the only sudden thing.
6. The divider: a plain thin line (about 2 px, off-white at modest opacity). No glow, no blur, no
   shadow. Labels "VANILLA" and "USLU DUR!" small, upper case, riding the line, no glow, never over
   the action. Vary the divider direction and timing per scene (vertical, diagonal where it suits),
   for example: open fully vanilla, sweep slowly to reveal Uslu dur!, settle into a split, sweep
   back once, end on Uslu dur!.
7. Text: the game's own OFL fonts in D:/Arma_CWA_worktrees/graphics-remaster/arma_CWR/thirdparty/
   guerrilla-fonts/ (read README.md and OFL.txt): cwr_title.ttf (Oswald) for titles and the
   wordmark, cwr_body.ttf (Roboto) for labels and subtitles, cwr_mono.ttf for the typed line of
   scene 1 if it reads better. No boxes behind text; off-white text with a soft, wide, low-opacity
   shadow or a gentle dark gradient; a consistent safe margin (about 5%). The ONLY accent colour
   is the game's HUD green, sampled from a real capture (for example the action-menu text in
   D:/Arma_CWA_worktrees/graphics-remaster/tmp/look/ref/vanilla/tank_gunner_optics.png; median of
   text pixels), used sparingly. NO gold, amber or orange anywhere in added graphics. Never
   grade or tint the game footage; both sides stay exactly as rendered.
8. Scene 1 only: its title types on letter by letter like a field report (island, time, scene
   name), calm (12 to 16 characters per second), a thin HUD-green block cursor that blinks twice
   and fades with the line; no shaking, no glitch effects, no typing sounds.
9. Words: never "official". Nothing cringe: plain, confident, understated; no marketing clichés
   (next level, like never before, breathtaking, stunning, revolutionary, immersive, experience,
   brand new, epic), no puns, no rhetorical questions, no exclamation marks except in the brand.
   Titles are short and factual (place, time, scene). Narration says what is on screen and what is
   new, calmly; at most one quietly dry line in the whole video. The outro tease is factual (part
   2 covers the gameplay: Guerrilla Mode and the AI). Original wording only (style notes:
   tmp/video/v2/reference/STYLE_NOTES.txt; never Rockstar's or GTA's lines).
10. NPC heads must not wiggle. Give units fixed look targets (doWatch / commandWatch), disable
   target and auto-target AI where not needed, use calm behaviour and fixed stances, pin units once
   (only vehicles move on per-frame paths), and advance animation time by exactly 1/60 s per
   captured frame on both sides. Verify with 2x head crops over 60 frames per side.
11. The Uslu dur! side must really be the overhaul: a test once showed vanilla on both halves.
   Prove per scene from the capture log (game dir and lighting mode); before compositing check the
   mean absolute difference between the two sides at the same frame is clearly non-zero; never put
   placeholder frames in anything the owner can see (stamp any stand-in "PLACEHOLDER" and keep it
   out of final/).
12. Narration: a pleasant, warm, confident female voice, calm and measured (local Breeze TTS,
   recording approved by the owner) - but it is recorded only AFTER the owner has reviewed the
   on-screen text and the narration lines. This team delivers the silent cut and the text first.

AGENT 3 REPORT (fogwind swarm): more taps per cell per frame
==============================================================
2026-10-23 03:58 to 04:52, agent 3. Folder: graphics-remaster/tmp/video/fogwind/3/

VERDICT
-------
More taps per cell works and composes with the rest of the swarm's fix, but it is NOT the decisive
change. The decisive changes are (1) reading the history at the cell CENTRE (agent 6: the shipped read
at the jittered point is a leaky sum of the jitter, 8.3x gain, and a blur) and (2) a per-cell
offset with no translation symmetry (a white hash, not IGN). With those two in place, one tap per
cell already removes the pump and the wind in the stepped probes. What extra taps buy is the last
bit of IN-PLACE BOIL with the camera held, which agent 1 showed is visible in real-time play at one
render per frame (1-tap Ac boils ~2x the floor; Act25 ~1.3x; every 4-tap candidate, t4w included,
sits at the floor).

Best candidate from my angle: t4wb = agent 2's Act25 with 4 stratified taps per cell instead of 1
(fogwind/3/shaders/t4wb/psFogInject.glsl). Stepped probes: no pump, no wind, the lowest boil of the
measured candidates, and the original look (region means equal to Act25's, within +0.4..+0.7 codes of
the pre-d91 look). 2 taps (t2wb, built, not shot) should already be at the floor (static ladder below).
Cost: inside the contention noise of this machine (least-contended air samples d91 1.58 / Act25
1.67 / t4wb 1.67 ms). Real-time 60 fps check of t2wb/t4wb is queued with agent 1 (mist-1 recorder).
If the lead prefers the cheapest fix, Act25 (1 tap) meets the stepped bar; if the held-camera boil
in play matters, use 2 taps (t2wb) or 4 (t4wb) with the same history read.

APPROACH
--------
Live shader overrides of psFogInject on the trailer's build (look-director-rwdi = d91a08e0), no C++
change, no rebuild. 0 "shader compile error" in every run log (checked), "override loaded" in each.
Per cell: N taps, depth stratum k paired with a rook point in xy (pairing rotated per slice), averaged
before the history blend. Each tap moves inside its own stratum (1/N of the cell) by the frame's
golden-ratio offset, rotated per cell (and per slice in depth) by a white pcg3d hash. History read at
the cell centre (single trilinear tap), or through Act25's fixed tetrahedral +-0.25 read (the "b"
variants), which restores the shipped look.

Variants (fogwind/3/shaders/<name>/psFogInject.glsl; generator tools/assemble3.py, t4wb/t2wb are
t4w/t2w with Act25's read pasted in):

  t4alt  8-tap Latin hypercube split into two 4-tap halves, half alternated per frame
  t4wb   t4w + Act25 history read                     t2wb  t2w + Act25 history read (not shot)
  t4c, t8fix, t4fix, t2anti: generated, not run (agent 4 owned the zero-jitter fixed taps).

RISKS
  - Cost not measured on an idle GPU (contention). 4 taps quadruple fogCell work in the injection.
  - The AI link is untouched: only the renderer's sampling and history read change; fogMediumAt and
    ComputeFogMedium are the same.
  - After a cut (fogFrame.z = 1) the first frame is a single 4-tap estimate with white offsets: finer
    noise than 1 tap, settles in ~10 frames like every history-based candidate; the Act25 read's look
    settles with the history (fable-1's point).
  - The dynamic array index g_tapDir[k] in a loop is plain GLSL 3.30; tested on this machine's driver
    only.

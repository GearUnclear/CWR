graphics: the fog samples fixed points of each cell, so the dawn valley's haze has no wind

The per-cell jitter of d91a08e0 stopped the far haze pumping, but the owner
saw "motion in the fog that makes it look like wind is blowing through it".
Its per-cell offset, interleaved gradient noise, is a ramp over the cells
(one cell to the right adds 0.556 of a slice), and the frame's golden-ratio
step adds 0.618, so every frame's offset field was the last one read about a
cell over. The history's residual slid through the fog about one 12 px cell
per frame, with the camera held or moving, in play at 60 fps and in the
captures. The history was also read at the jittered point, which made it a
leaky sum of the offsets (about 8 times their average): the amplifier of the
original pump.

psFogInject now samples each cell at two fixed points (depth strata at a
quarter and three quarters of the slice, each weighted by the metres of its
half slice, on the diagonal quarter points of the cell; VolumetricFog.hpp
kFogTapZ, kFogTapXY) and never jitters them. The history is read at the
cell's centre through a fixed tetrahedral blur of a quarter cell and slice
(kFogHistoryBlur), the mean of the blur the jittered read gave the stored
volume, which keeps the approved mist density. Nothing in the sampling
changes between frames, so the history has no residual to pump or to move,
at any frame rate or capture cadence. The jitter uniforms stay 0; the frame
count still steps the near field's march. The medium is unchanged, so AI
sight still follows the drawn fog.

The [remaster][fog][Shaders] test replaces d91a08e0's: it pins the taps, the
centre read and the blur in the shader, and runs a mirror of the injection
and its history over a cell grid. The frame-wide offset trips its pump bar,
the per-cell gradient its travel bar (held and panning), and the fixed taps
neither, standing exactly still with the camera held.

Dawn valley probes (camera held / real pan, stepped at 1/60 s):
- held: travel correlation 0.47 to 0.03, in-place change 0.42 to 0.05 code
  values (the jitter-frozen floor 0.08), block flicker 0.11 to 0.02;
- pan: wind 0.047 / 0.123 / 0.078 / 0.035 to 0.005 / 0.004 / 0.002 / 0.004
  over bay / valley / far hill / haze (vanilla 0.017 / 0.007 / 0.002 /
  0.007), valley block flicker 0.117 to 0.068 (vanilla 0.057, the original
  1.62);
- the reshot dawn clip (frames 30 to 560): band 0.091 (vanilla 0.107, the
  F1 shot 0.103, the original 0.567), wind at most 0.004 (F1 0.101), region
  means within 0.2 code values of the original approved look;
- cost +0.09 ms on the air stage at 1080p (two uncontended pairs).

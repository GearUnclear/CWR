FOGWIND LEAD DECISION (agent 8), 2026-10-23
===========================================

CHOSEN: q2b (agent 4), built as production code on top of graphics-remaster HEAD d91a08e0.
  psFogInject samples each froxel cell at TWO FIXED points and never jitters them:
  depth strata at 1/4 and 3/4 of the slice (each weighted by the metres of its half
  slice) on the diagonal quarter points of the cell (-0.25,-0.25) and (+0.25,+0.25).
  The history (0.88 kept) is read at the cell's CENTRE (agent 6's finding) through a
  fixed tetrahedral blur of +-0.25 cell and slice per axis (agent 2's Act25 read,
  [1 6 1]/8 per axis), which restores the approved mist density. The column's own
  light (ring, sun share, lobe) is the cell centre's. The jitter uniforms are 0; the
  frame count still steps the near-field march. The medium is untouched, so AI sight
  (ComputeFogMedium / Target.cpp) still matches what is drawn.

WHY THIS ONE
- Every instrument puts it at the floor: agent 1's kit (stepped held + pan, and real
  time 60 fps held + pan as a live override on the recorder build), fable-2's
  independent fogflow (lowest held-camera numbers of every clip measured), agent 4's
  probes, and my runs of the production build without overrides.
- Nothing temporal is left in the sampling. Pump, travel and boil are impossible by
  construction at any frame rate and any capture cadence (stepped or real time,
  warm-up renders or not). The equal-scoring rivals keep a temporal pattern: c8b an
  8-frame cycle (7.5 Hz at 60 fps, 2.5 Hz at 20 fps) and a new uniform; t2wb/Act25 a
  white-rotated golden sequence (Act25 boils 1.3x the floor in play); S2z/q2z change
  the integrate pass and cost 8 trilinear taps per integrate step.
- Shader-only in effect (the C++ only zeroes the uniforms), one extra fogCell and
  three extra history fetches per cell.
- The look is the approved one: over the whole reshot clip the region means match
  the ORIGINAL within 0.2 code values (F1 was +1 to +1.8 mistier).

THE CAUSE OF THE WIND (agent 1, confirmed by fable-2 from scratch)
  d91's per-cell offset was interleaved gradient noise: IGN(x+1, y) = IGN(x, y) + 0.556.
  The frame's golden-ratio step adds 0.618 (0.570, 0.755 in xy). So each frame's
  offset field was the last one read one cell to the right: the history's residual
  slid LEFT about one 12 px cell per frame (about 700 px/s) as 2-4 cell blotches, in
  every region, camera held or panning, in play at 60 fps and in the captures.
  The amplifier of both bugs: the history was read at the jittered point, so it stored
  a leaky SUM of the offsets (gain 1/0.12, about 8) instead of their average.

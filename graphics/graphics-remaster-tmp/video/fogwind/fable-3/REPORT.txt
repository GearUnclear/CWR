FABLE-3 REPORT: why a sub-cell jitter swings the far haze, and what is robust by construction
===========================================================================================
Swarm: tmp/video/fogwind (board BOARD.txt). Shot: trailer scene 2, valley_mist ("MOVING OUT AT DAWN").
Base build: graphics-remaster-rwdi (= d91a08e0, the per-cell IGN fix). Overrides only, no engine change,
no branch. Tools and outputs: tmp/video/fogwind/fable-3/{tools,shaders,runs,out}/.

1. THE QUESTION
---------------
The first swarm found the mechanism (the frame-wide jitter under the 0.12 history pumped the whole
sheet); agent 6 found the amplifier (the history is read at the JITTERED point, so the stored value sits
at a leaky SUM of the offsets, gain 8.3x, not an EMA); agent 1 found the wind (IGN + a frame-global
phase step = the same pattern one cell to the right every frame). My question was the one under all
of those: why does ONE sub-cell offset move a far cell's value so much in the first place, and which
parts of that are fixed by construction (no averaging, no history, no sequence) and which are not.

2. METHOD
---------
A 1-D model of psFogInject along one ray with the real numbers (tools/sens.py, out/sens.txt;
tools/noise.py, out/noise.txt): the slice table (FogDepthK: 64 slices, first slice 0.9 m, zFar = the
view distance 4000 m, k = 6.099), the medium of fogMediumAt (the clear haze 6e-4/m at sea level over
1200 m, its near-thinning ramp 0.06 -> 1 over 200..3000 m, the lobe keep 0.1 -> 1 over 30..800 m, the
valley mist 2e-3/m in the layer with the top's exponential fall over 18 m, the layer's top moved by the
two cloudNoise octave sets), and the valley_mist geometry (eye 1.75 m over the ridge, pitch -6, fov 0.7
x topFOV 0.75: 0.0557 deg per px, a 12 px cell = 0.67 deg; the kit's valley/bay/farhill regions are the
rays at -5..-14 deg). Per slice: the slice MEAN of sigma (what the cell should hold) against the point
sample at a depth offset u in [0,1) (the shipped estimator), at a y offset of +-0.5 cell, and against
fixed estimators: 1 tap at the slice mid, 2 Gauss points, 2 taps at 1/4 and 3/4, 4 strata.

3. FINDINGS (the model)
-----------------------
3.1 Where the far haze lives in the slice table. A ray at -5..-14 deg from the ridge meets the valley
    floor and the bay at 400..1000 m. The slices there (s 38..49) are 35..95 m deep, so one slice drops
    the ray by only 4..12 m against the mist's 18 m fall: the mist top is crossed over 2..4 slices, not
    inside one. The clear haze's far slices (1..3 km) are 100..300 m deep.
3.2 The SMOOTH medium is not very sensitive, and a fixed tap is exact on it.
      point sample, depth offset u in [0,1): swing 10..20% of the slice's value in the crossing slices
        (sens.txt), the haze ramp's far slices 8..16% (noise.txt section 1);
      point sample, y offset +-0.5 cell: 10..38% in the crossing slices;
      the whole column's S for ONE offset shared by all slices (the frame-wide scheme): 1..3%;
      independent offsets per slice (the per-cell scheme): 0.1..0.3% rms;
      a FIXED tap at the slice mid: < 1% error vs the slice mean in every slice (2 Gauss points and 4
        strata the same). An analytic integral of the ramps would buy nothing measurable over the mid
        point: I dropped it (board 04:51).
    So the pump of the original build = 1..3% per frame x the 8.3x leaky-sum amplifier, coherent over
    the frame (agent 6's C0 measured 9.2x in game). With the history read at the centre, the smooth
    medium's residual is already below everything the kit resolves (agents 2..6 measured that).
3.3 What is NOT smooth: the mist top's noise. cloudNoise is a 256x256 R8 tile of 5 octaves of value
    noise (periods 4..64 per tile), rank-equalised, NO mips, linear filtering (EngineGL33_Lighting.cpp
    141..150, CloudNoise.cpp). fogMediumAt reads it at the 310 m scale (n2, also + p.y * 0.002) and at
    1100 m (n1): the finest octave is 5 m at n2 and 17 m at n1, far below a far slice's 35..300 m span.
    It moves the layer top by (n2 - 0.5) * 0.35 * depth = +-6 m and depth * mix(0.6, 1.35, n1), and the
    banks x1..1.7 (smoothstep of 0.6 n1 + 0.4 n2). Modelled along the -8 deg ray over the crossing
    zone (60 m slices, 200 random positions, noise.txt section 2), error vs the slice's mean mist:
      point sample          rms 36%   max 257%
      1 fixed tap at mid    rms 22%   max  67%
      2 taps at 1/4, 3/4    rms 10%   max  42%
      4 strata              rms  3%   max  15%
    Across the cell (7 m footprint at 600 m): point 7%, mid 2%, 2 taps 0.5%: the lateral footprint is
    10x smaller than the slice's span, depth is where the noise aliases.
    This is the per-sample term every jittered scheme boils on and every single fixed tap aliases
    (agent 3's "frozen" curtains 0.317 vs original 0.152 in agent 4's curtain box; agent 3's tap ladder
    1 tap 0.125 -> 2 taps 0.072 -> 4 taps 0.064 static boil; agent 1's real-time Ac 2x the 4-tap floor).
    The shadow-map vis is the other hard term, but the cascades end in the near slices (metres deep),
    and the first swarm's fogvis1 showed it carries none of the far pump.
3.4 What the lookup side adds. The apply reads the integrated volume with a trilinear texture() at
    (f - 0.5)/N: a smooth interpolation between two slices' S and T; it cannot make a residual, only
    pass one. The ring (g_air) is per column (9 taps over 22 deg, linear-filtered 256 bins): smooth in
    the xy jitter (0.33 deg at most); agent 7 found its bins stepping at the screen edges in a
    history-free far field, a separate item.

4. ROBUST BY CONSTRUCTION (what follows from 3)
-----------------------------------------------
  (as corrected by the runs in section 5; the first draft of this section blamed the mist noise)
  (a) No temporal jitter is needed for the MEDIUM: a deterministic tap at the slice mid on the
      cell-centre ray is exact to < 1% on every smooth term, and the runs show the mist noise's
      aliasing does not reach the kit either (det1 = det1n = det1nq = det1g on every number).
      Nothing temporal, nothing to travel, nothing to pump: det1 alone is at the floor on flicker,
      wind, boil and grain.
  (b) The history must be read at the centre (agent 6) so it is an EMA smoother, not an amplifier.
  (c) What a fixed tap reads HARD, and so must be band-limited to the froxel by construction, is the
      LIGHT, not the medium: the sun visibility (canopy map / terrain horizon smoothstep per column;
      det1v -80% of the streak excess; the shadow map alone is not it, det1s = det1) and the horizon
      ring's bin noise (det1r; a C++ item in the ring pass, 0.05 codes, under the visible). Fix: vis
      = the mean of fogSunVisibility over 4 points of the froxel (a quincunx +-0.25 cell across,
      +-0.25 slice along), the medium still one tap (det1p: streak excess 0.104 -> 0.035).
  (d) The look: the shipped look included the jittered history read's blur (agent 7, sigma ~1.3
      cells); Act25's fixed tetra history read (det1pb/det1prb) or agent 6's display tent restores it
      and takes the last 0.035 of the streaks with it (det1prb 0.183 = q2b 0.185 = base 0.192).
  Prototypes (shaders/<variant>/psFogInject.glsl, generator tools/gen.py from the d91 per-cell shader):
    det1    = (a) + (b): ONE tap per cell, slice mid, cell centre, no jitter anywhere; history 0.12
              read at the same point.
    det1n   = det1 + n1/n2 averaged over 2 points at 1/4 and 3/4 of the slice's span.
    det1nq  = det1n + the noise also averaged across the cell (+-0.25 cell).
    det1n4  = det1 + the mist density averaged over 4 strata along the slice (generated, not run:
              the noise turned out not to matter).
    det1v / det1s / det1r / det1g = attribution only (vis = 1 / shadow map off / ring off / ground
              air + mist off).
    det1p   = det1 + (c).            det1pb  = det1p + (d).   <- the hand-off
    det1pr  = det1p + 17 ring taps.  det1prb = det1pr + (d).  (ring taps neutral)

6. WHAT I HANDED TO FABLE-1
---------------------------
  - shaders/det1pb/psFogInject.glsl: the override (base: the d91 per-cell shader of
    tmp/video/mist/1/shaders/percell, byte-equal in its fog sources to graphics-remaster-rwdi).
    Diff vs d91 in three places: (1) fogSlice samples the slice mid on the cell-centre ray, no
    jitter in depth or xy, history read at the same point (an EMA); (2) fogCell's vis = the mean of
    fogSunVisibility over a quincunx of 4 points (+-0.25 cell across, +-0.25 slice along, g_cellLat
    and g_span); (3) Act25's fixed tetrahedral +-0.25 history read. The medium (fogMediumAt) is
    untouched, so the AI link through ComputeFogMedium is untouched.
  - tools/gen.py regenerates every variant from the base (det1, det1n, det1nq, det1n4, det1v,
    det1s, det1r, det1g, det1p, det1pb, det1pr, det1prb); tools/measure.ps1 measures a run against
    the references; tools/sens.py and tools/noise.py are the model; tools/curtainview.py and
    tools/crop2x.py the pictures.
  - Position for CANDIDATE.txt: not a bid against production q2b (agent 8 built it; det1pb has the
    same numbers in the same class). It is the explanation of what q2b's second tap pays for (the
    sun visibility of the air, not the medium) and a cheaper equivalent if cost bites: one fogCell
    plus three extra fogSunVisibility per cell instead of two fogCell. Two C++ follow-ups for the
    look director: smooth the ring across bins in the ring pass (its bin noise is the last 0.05
    codes of low-sky structure every zero-jitter scheme shows), and read the canopy/horizon sun
    visibility of the air over the froxel's footprint in production rather than through a tap
    spread.
  - For the unit test: the model's two numbers worth asserting are (a) a fixed mid tap's error on
    the exponential/ramp medium of one slice stays under 1% (it did in every slice of sens.py), and
    (b) the frame-wide and IGN schemes' pump and travel (fable-1's test already asserts those).

What I did
----------
1. Read the brief, the board, the shader (EngineGL33_PostFogShaders.cpp psFogInject), the jitter source
   (EngineGL33_PostFog.cpp) and the leading Opus overrides (Act25, q2b, S2z).
2. Ceded the analytic depth integral to fable-3 (same first idea, posted the same minute).
3. Designed and ran q2z = deterministic 2-tap injection (q2b's) + plain centre history read + the display tent
   (S2z's integrate). Rationale: the two parents restore the approved look in different places; q2b through a
   blur in the history (so the look depends on the history's age: sharper for ~10 frames after a cut or a
   history reset, then settles), S2z through a display filter (the same held and moving, from frame 1). The
   combination keeps q2b's determinism (nothing temporal to pump, travel or boil at any cadence) and S2z's
   stateless look. Measured held and panning with agent 1's kit: floor on every motion metric, look within
   0.3 codes (held) and +0.7 (pan) of the original, the closest of anything on the board.
4. Wrote the regression test the board lacked: a C++ mirror of the per-cell offset over a 48x48 cell grid x
   300 frames through the 0.12 EMA read at the centre, two metrics: PUMP (sd over frames of a 5x5 block mean)
   and TRAVEL (best frame-to-frame correlation of the residual field at a nonzero cell lag, |lag| <= 3). The
   test asserts the frame-wide scheme trips the pump bar (0.031 > 0.025, travel 0.10), d91's IGN scheme trips
   the travel bar (0.98 at lag (0,2); pump 0.0036 passes, which is exactly how the old test missed it), and the
   fixed taps leave 0 / 0. Bars for any future jittered scheme: pump < 0.01, travel < 0.2 (a white per-cell
   hash gives 0.0054 / 0.014 and would pass). Plus string pins of the fixed taps, the centre read, the tent,
   and the absence of fogDepth.w / fogFrame.xy / fogCellNoise / fract(fogDepth...) in the injection.
5. Made the production patch (comments in the repo's voice, VolumetricFog.hpp mirror constants kFogTapZ /
   kFogTapXY checked against the shader strings like the existing mirrors, ITERATIONS.md F2), verified the
   patch's shader code equals the tested override ignoring comments (tools diff: only names and no-op guards).
6. Assembled compare.mp4 and this report; verdicts on the Opus candidates in CANDIDATE.txt.

Gotchas for whoever lands it
----------------------------
- `_fog.sequence` must keep advancing: psFogNear's march reads seq % 64 (fogNearGrid.w); my first patch
  removed it and broke the build. The jitter uniforms fogDepth.w / fogFrame.xy are set to 0 and no longer read.
- The integrate reads the injected volume with texture() (trilinear) instead of texelFetch: the inject
  textures already have linear filtering (the history read uses it), so no C++ sampler change is needed; the
  S2z override proved it in-game.
- fogmotion.py without --static reports boil/slow as nan on a held-camera clip; re-run with --static.
- agent 1's run_probe_fog.ps1 defaults -GameDir to look-director-rwdi: pass the graphics-remaster-rwdi path
  explicitly (as the brief requires).
- The curtain metric (agent 4's) is a column-to-column measure; fable-3 showed the "frozen" streaks are
  lateral aliasing of per-column terms, not the medium; the tent and the two xy taps keep q2z at the
  original's level there (0.147 vs 0.152).

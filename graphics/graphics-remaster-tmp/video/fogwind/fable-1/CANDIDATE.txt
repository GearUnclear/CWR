THE CAUSE OF THE WIND, in plain words
-------------------------------------
The first fix (d91a08e0) gave every froxel cell its own sub-cell offset from an interleaved-gradient
pattern, on top of the frame's golden-ratio step. That pattern is a ramp over the cells: one cell to the
right adds 0.556 of a slice, two cells down add 0.619, and the frame step adds 0.618. So the next frame's
offset field is this frame's field read one cell over, and what the history keeps of it (0.88 per frame)
slides through the fog about one cell (12 px) per render, in every region, camera held or moving, in play
and in the captures. The owner saw that slide as wind. (Agent 1 derived it, fable-2 and agent 1 measured it
at -10..-13 px/frame; my unit-test mirror reproduces it: frame-to-frame correlation 0.98 at a two-cell lag.)
Two things amplified it: the history was read at the jittered point, which turns the stored value into a
leaky SUM of the offsets (gain 8.3) instead of their average (agent 6), and the far slices are hundreds of
metres deep, so a sub-slice offset is a large change of the medium (the first swarm; fable-3's root cause).

THE FIX (what q2z does)
-----------------------
1. No temporal jitter at all. Each cell is a two-point quadrature of its own depth span: taps at 1/4 and 3/4
   of the slice (weighted by the metres of each half slice) on the two diagonal quarter points of the cell,
   the same points every frame (agent 4's q2b injection). The history has no residual of a moving sample, so
   it can neither pump (the original bug) nor travel (the regression): nothing is left to average.
2. The history (0.88 of the last frame) is read at the cell's CENTRE (agent 6): it is only a smoother of the
   light's own changes (the horizon ring, shadows, the pan), an EMA instead of a leaky sum.
3. The softness of the look comes from a DISPLAY filter, not from the history: psFogIntegrate reads the
   injected volume through a 3x3x3 tent (eight trilinear taps half a cell and half a slice out; agent 6's
   S2z integrate). The old jittered history read blurred the stored volume by about a cell, and that blur is
   the mist's softness in the approved look; every centre-read variant without a blur is about 2 code values
   clearer in the valley. As a display filter the tent gives the same softness on a held and a moving camera
   and from the first frame after a cut, because nothing compounds through the history (q2b and Act25 get
   their look back through a blur IN the history, so their look depends on the history's age).
The medium itself is untouched (fogMediumAt, ComputeFogMedium): the AI sight link is unchanged.

Why two fixed taps and not one (fable-3's root cause, fable-3/REPORT.txt, a 1-D model of psFogInject along
the real rays with the real slice table): the SMOOTH medium is not the problem, a single fixed tap at the slice
mid is within 1% of the slice mean everywhere, and an analytic integral would buy nothing over it. What a
sub-slice offset really swings is the mist top's cloudNoise (5 m and 17 m finest octaves against 35..300 m far
slices, no mips): over the crossing zone a point sample's error vs the slice mean is 36% rms (max 257%), one
fixed mid tap 22% (max 67%), two taps at 1/4 and 3/4 10% (max 42%), four strata 3%. The per-cell jittered
schemes boil on exactly that term; one fixed tap aliases it into static curtains; two taps quarter it and the
tent takes the rest. fable-3 also named the per-column terms a fixed tap leaves as faint vertical streaks
(the sun visibility's per-column smoothstep and the horizon ring's 256-bin kinks), which the old leaky
history hid under its ~1.3-cell lateral blur: in q2z the display tent plays that role (curtains below the sun
0.147 vs the original 0.152).

----

fetch (as shipped). Integration: 8 trilinear reads instead of 1 texelFetch per step. Contended-machine air
stage samples: agent 4 q2b 4.07 ms vs d91 3.32 same batch (noise band +-0.5 ms); agent 6 S2z "same as d91
within noise"; agent 3's least-contended samples: d91 1.58 / Act25 1.67 / t4wb 1.67 ms. Expect +0.2..0.5 ms
at 1080p; needs the idle timing run (ITERATIONS P1). If cost bites: evaluate the sun visibility once per
cell (agent 7's note) and keep the two medium taps.

RISKS AND WHAT IS NOT VERIFIED
------------------------------
- Cost on an idle machine (above).
- The 300-frame pan and the real-time runs were shot once each; no second seed. The stepped static and moving
  probes agree with agent 1's and fable-2's instruments on every candidate they share.
- Fixed taps are view-locked: a deterministic quadrature's bias moves with the view. Measured curtains are at
  the original's level and the tent removes the lattice's two-cell frequencies; agent 6 saw a faint periodic
  dash at the far shoreline under the sun every ~27 frames with S2 (also present in the original and with C0,
  not with the camera held): a ring/pan item, not from the sampling.
- The display tent blurs half a slice in depth: at the near slices (a metre deep) harmless; near geometry
  edges the apply still reads by depth, the tent is inside the froxel grid only.
- Shader-only change plus 2 trivial C++ lines (jitter uniforms 0, the sequence kept for the near field's
  march) and mirror constants; no new uniforms, no history layout change.
- The every-27-frame whole-band pulse (~0.1 code, frames 25/51/79/108 of the pan) is in every candidate and
  in d91: a scene or ring cadence the old noise masked; not addressed here.

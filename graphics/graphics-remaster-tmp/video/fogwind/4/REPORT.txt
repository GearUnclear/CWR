VERDICT
-------
Meets the bar on the valley_mist probes, stepped capture AND real time at 60 fps (agent 1's
recorder ran q4 and q2b). The recommended form is q2b:
  - each froxel cell is the metric-weighted mean of 2 FIXED taps (depth strata 1/4 and 3/4 of the
    slice, xy -0.25/+0.25 of the cell on the diagonal), the same in every cell and every frame:
    NO temporal jitter at all, so nothing exists that could pump, travel or boil;
  - the history (0.88 kept) is read at the cell/slice CENTRE through agent 2's fixed tetrahedral
    +-0.25 cell/slice blur (Act25), which restores the shipped look (the shipped jittered history
    read blurred the volume, and that blur is part of the approved look).
Static camera: the fog is still (floor class). Moving: wind and flicker at vanilla level.
Look: within 1 code value of the original (pre-d91) look; d91 was +2.4 in the valley.
Curtains (the risk of fixed sampling, agent 3's "frozen" streaks): gone (0.143 vs original 0.152).
Cost: one extra medium + light evaluation per cell and 3 extra history fetches per cell; the GPU
was heavily contended (frame 130-150 ms from other games), so the cost is NOT resolved here.

WHY IT WORKS
------------
- The far slices are hundreds of metres deep and the mist and haze change strongly inside one.
  With one jittered point per cell the history carried a residual of that swing; frame-wide it
  pumped (original), per-cell IGN plus the golden-ratio step made the residual pattern translate
  one cell per frame (agent 1: the wind). Integrating the depth span with fixed strata removes
  the need for a depth jitter altogether: the cell value is a pure function of the camera pose.
- Point sampling without jitter aliases in xy (frozen's curtains). Fixed xy taps inside the cell
  plus the fixed history blur give the same smooth reconstruction as the shipped look, without
  any time dependence.
- Agent 3's ladder agrees: 2 stratified taps already reach the static floor; 4 or 8 buy nothing
  in motion. The look needs the history blur (Act25), not more taps.

AI LINK
-------
Unchanged. The medium (fogMediumAt) and its C++ mirror are untouched; only where the renderer
samples it and how it filters its own history changed. AI sight is the CPU line integral
FogSightTransmittance (VolumetricFog.hpp/.cpp), independent of the froxel sampling. q2b's drawn
haze sits within 1 code of the original look, so the drawn-vs-AI match is as before.

THE CHANGE (for the lead; not applied anywhere)
-----------------------------------------------
psFogInject only (kPostFogInjectMainGLSL in EngineGL33_PostFogShaders.cpp): replace fogCellNoise,
g_cellJitter, fogSlice and main's jitter lines with the block in q2b_fogSlice_main.glsl.txt
(this folder). The full assembled override is shaders/q2b/psFogInject.glsl. Summary:
  const int KT = 2;
  kTapXY = (-0.25,-0.25), (0.25,0.25);  kTapZ = 0.25, 0.75   (fractions of the slice)
  fogSlice: cur = sum_i w_i fogCell(g_tapDir[i] * depth(s + kTapZ[i])) / sum_i w_i,
            w_i = depth(s + kTapZ[i] + 0.25) - depth(s + kTapZ[i] - 0.25)   (metric weights)
            history: reproject the cell CENTRE (dirC * depth(s + 0.5)), read 0.25 * the sum of
            4 trilinear fetches at +-0.25 cell/slice on a tetrahedron (Act25), mix with fogFrame.z.
  main: g_tapDir[i] = viewToWorld * (ndc of gl_FragCoord.xy + kTapXY[i]); the column light
        (g_air, ring, sun share, lobe) from the cell centre direction.
C++: nothing required. fogFrame.xy and fogDepth.w become unused by the injection (the C++ jitter
jx/jy/jz and its comment can go). Unit test idea: the injection's sample points must not depend on
the frame (no fogFrame.xy / fogDepth.w in psFogInject), and the history read is centred.
Re-verify after landing: reshoot valley_mist ud side with the head recipe; also re-measure
pine_shafts (hard canopy-shadow edges in the air, agent 3's harder probe) and bay_reflections.

RISKS
-----
- Cost on an idle GPU not measured (contended numbers above).
- The fixed tap pattern is view-locked: on scenes with hard visibility edges in the air (pine
  canopy shafts) a 2-tap cell could show faint standing streaks or a 2-cell pan pulse; agent 3's
  pine_shafts probe is the test (q2b not run there by me).
- The every-27-frame whole-band pulse (~0.1 code) seen in every variant (also d91) is not from
  the injection jitter (agents 3, 6); untouched here.
- The column light (g_air from the horizon ring, the sun share, the lobe) is taken at the cell
  centre direction, so nothing averages the ring's bin stepping at the screen edges (agent 7's
  finding for history-free taps). With the history kept, q2b's block flicker is in the smoothed
  class (numbers above), but a pan that sweeps fast past the screen edge was not probed.
- The history blur makes the look depend on the history's age: for about 10 frames after a cut
  or a history reset the haze is about 2 codes clearer, then it settles (fable-1's point). Their
  alternative q2z = this injection + agent 6's S2z display tent (no history blur) has a stateless
  look; either works for the trailer, the lead picks.
- Only the valley_mist scene was probed by me (stepped static + moving, real time via agent 1).
  Other volFog clips (cloud_shadows, convoy, bay_reflections) need a re-measure after landing.

AGENT 7 REPORT: a stable far field for the froxel fog (fogwind swarm, 2026-10-23)
==============================================================================

VERDICT: PARTIAL. The approach as specified (past some distance, no jitter AND no history) kills
the wind and the pump and is bit-still on a held camera, but in motion it is measurably LESS calm
than the history-smoothed candidates (about 2x their low-frequency block flicker under the pan).
With a light history smoother kept (0.12, read at the cell centre) the same deterministic far taps
reach the floor class (S2 / Ac level), but then it is essentially agent 4's q4 with fewer taps near
the eye, not a new winner. Recommendation: do not ship a history-free far field; if the lead picks a
zero-jitter scheme (q4 class), my far80n4fh is a cheaper variant of it (taps only where slices are
deep); otherwise prefer the jittered floor-class schemes with the look-restoring blur (agent 2's Act25).


1. WHAT I BUILT (shader-only, live psFogInject override; no C++ change, no rebuild)
-----------------------------------------------------------------------------------
gen_shader.py writes shaders/<variant>/psFogInject.glsl from d91a08e0's per-cell shader:
  - slices whose mid view depth is past kFarFrom (ramped to kFarTo) take N FIXED taps: depth strata
    (s + (i + 0.5) / N) times an R2 pattern across the cell (2 taps: the cell's diagonal), averaged;
    no frame offset, so the far cell is a pure function of the camera pose;
  - kFarShare: the far cells' history share (1 = none; 0.12 = the shipped share, read at the
    cell / slice centre, never at a jittered point);
  - nearer slices: d91's per-cell jitter (--near percell) or one fixed tap at the slice and cell
    centre (--near frozen), history 0.12;
  - the column's light (g_air from the horizon ring, the sun's share, the forward lobe) is evaluated
    at the cell centre for every slice;
  - optional: the sun's visibility once per far cell (--visonce).
Same medium, same per-cell estimator (the cell mean), only the sampling changes, so the AI link
(ComputeFogMedium in the simulation) is untouched and still matches what is drawn.
Variants measured: far80n4 (from 80 m, ramp to 160 m, 4 taps, no far history, d91 near),
far80n8 (8 taps), far80n4fh (fixed near tap + 4 far taps, history 0.12 at the centre everywhere).
Generated but not run: far80n4v, far80n2v, far150n4v, far300n4, far80n4f.

WHY history-free is noisier in motion (by eye, look/diffstrip_70.png and diffstrip_70_q4_fh_Ac.png:
camera-compensated differences, 3 px blur, x40, frames 70-75, crop x1100-1900 y470-770): far80n4 and
far80n8 show faint VERTICAL column streaks near the right screen edge (x about 1700-1860) and soft
column bands. The column light g_air comes from the horizon ring's 9 taps over +-11 degrees of
azimuth; near the screen edges the ring bins enter and leave view (hard cut at |ndc| 0.985, sureness
jumps to 1 on the first measurement), so a column's light steps as the camera pans (the handoff's
hypothesis 2). The shipped 0.88 history turns those steps into fades, and jitter (Ac, S2) also
softens them; a history-free far field shows them raw. far80n4fh and q4 (both zero jitter, with
history) still show them faintly; Ac and S2 do not show them by eye. A column-mean metric over the
right edge did not separate the variants (0.24-0.27 for all, dominated by the sun path on the water),
so this is a by-eye observation, low amplitude.

3. RULED OUT / LESSONS
----------------------
  - History-free far field as the fix: fails the "as calm as vanilla" bar in motion relative to the
    other candidates (2x lowd2); more taps (8) do not help.
  - My first moving run (runs/moving-far80n4) is INVALID: a generator bug left g_visFix undeclared,
    the game logged "GL33 fog: shader compile error [psFogInject]" and drew thin fog (the inject did
    not run). The mkv is deleted; INVALID.txt marks the folder. Lesson posted: grep the game log for
    "shader compile error", "override loaded" alone proves nothing.
  - The exposure meter dithers by 0.01-0.02 between shots (cam ev in udcap.log) and produces
    whole-frame steps in some runs and not others; compare lowd2 on windows clear of those steps.


4. RISKS IF far80n4fh (or any zero-jitter scheme) IS CHOSEN
------------------------------------------------------------
  - The near field's single fixed tap per cell (my --near frozen) can draw grid curtains on hard
    shadow edges close to the eye (canopy shafts in pine_shafts); agent 3's pine probe is the test.
    Use 2+ fixed taps near, or a jittered near field.
  - Zero-jitter schemes keep the ring's edge steps (faint column streaks at the screen edges); a
    softer ring entry (fade a bin's sureness in over the last 10-15% of the screen) would remove them.
  - Look: about 1.4 codes clearer in the valley than the original unless Act25's fixed blur is added.

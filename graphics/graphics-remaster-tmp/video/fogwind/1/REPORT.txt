1. VERDICT ON THE WIND (d91a08e0, per-cell IGN jitter)
------------------------------------------------------
The owner's "wind" is real, it is a game bug (not a capture artefact), and it has an exact cause:

  IGN(x+1, y) = fract(IGN(x, y) + 52.9829189 * 0.06711056) = fract(IGN(x, y) + 0.5557)
  (one cell to the right = +0.556 of a phase, apart from a 0.017 seam every 14.9 cells).
  The frame's golden/R2 step adds 0.618 (depth, jz), 0.570 (jy), 0.755 (jx) every render.
  So the next render's jitter field equals this render's field read ONE CELL TO THE RIGHT, to
  within 0.062 (depth), 0.014 (jy) and 0.199 (jx) of a phase. The history residual of that
  field therefore SLIDES ONE FROXEL CELL (12 px at 1080p) TO THE LEFT EVERY RENDER.

What it looks like: blotches 2 to 4 cells across (24 to 48 px; spectral peaks of the frame
difference at 30 to 47 px), +-1 to 2 code values, streaming left at about 11 to 13 px per frame
(about 700 px/s at 60 fps) over every fogged region, whatever the camera does.

Measured (kit: fogmotion.py; rho_off = correlation of consecutive camera-compensated residuals at
their best non-zero lag, vel_off = that lag in px/frame relative to the scene):
  stepped probe, camera HELD (mist3 static-percell):  rho_off 0.26-0.40 at [-13, 0] in all
      regions; lag 2 frames peaks at [-25, 0] (+0.20): a coherent travel, not an alias.
  trailer clip (v2/ud/valley_mist frames 240-390):    rho_off 0.22-0.38 at [-12, 0].
  REAL PLAY at 60 fps, camera panning (mist/1/rec/pc_rt60.mkv): rho_off 0.35/0.53/0.40
      (bay/farhill/haze) at [-10..-11, 0]; unfixed all_rt60 0.02-0.07.
  REAL PLAY at 60 fps, camera HELD (my rt probe, rec/pc_rtst.mkv): rho_off 0.15/0.48/0.52/0.47
      at [-9..-12, 0]; boil valley 0.422 (q4 0.061).
  By eye: out/dstrip_static.png (stepped, held camera) and out/dstrip_rtst_pc_q4.png (real time,
      held camera): per-cell diffs x24 are covered in cell blotches that shift left frame to
      frame; the candidates are flat. x-t RESID strips (out/xt_val_240_390/xt_view.png and
      out/xt_moving/xt_resid_view.png): original = horizontal lines (pumping), per-cell = a fast
      shallow-slanted hatching (travel), vanilla and the candidates = only the camera's own slope.

Rule that follows: a per-cell offset that is (quasi-)linear in the cell index (IGN, an R2 or
golden ramp of the cell, any lattice ramp) plus a frame-global phase step is a translation of
the residual. Use a white per-cell hash / blue-noise tile (no translation symmetry), or no
per-frame jitter at all. A white hash alone (agent 2's A) removes the travel but BOILS (in-place
cell-scale noise 10x the floor) unless the history is read at the cell centre.

2. THE KIT (tmp/video/fogwind/1/kit/, plain `python`, docstrings explain every column)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  fogmotion.py   per region (bay, valley, farhill, haze, slope; soldiers excluded):
                 FLICKER band (= bandmetric) and lowd2 (60 px block d2); MOTION camera shift,
                 mid (cell-scale residual rms), rho_off / vel_off / WIND (= mid * rho_off),
                 rho_c / vslow (slow creep under 1 px/frame); GRAIN fine / fine0;
                 --static: no camera estimate (the wind biased it) + boil / slow (in-place change).
  xtslice.py     x-t and y-t slices (RAW | STAB | RESID) + crop2x.mp4 / resid2x.mp4 (2x bay and
                 far valley), per-clip first frame with LABEL@first.
  dstrip.py      consecutive camera-compensated diffs x gain, crops, one row per clip.
  windchar.py    static-camera wind characterisation: spectral peaks of the diff, multi-lag travel.
  curtain.py     screen-locked streaks on a moving clip (time mean in screen coordinates).
  kxnu.py, spectrum3d.py  spectral views (not discriminative here; kept for reference).
  gen_probe_fog1.py + run_probe_fog.ps1  the shared probes look_probe_fog1_static (frame 328 pose
                 held, k = 270..404) and look_probe_fog1_moving, run + mkv + measure.
  gen_rt_probe.py + run_rt.ps1 + measure_rt.sh  REAL-TIME probes on the mist-1 recorder build:
                 rt60 (the pan, one render per 1/60 s tick, 200 frames) and rtst (camera held,
                 real time, 200 frames).
  Validation (valley_mist 240-390): original band 0.516 / WIND <= 0.019; per-cell band 0.093 /
  WIND up to 0.162, rho_off 0.34; vanilla band 0.103 / WIND <= 0.017, rho_off 0.035.
  Lesson kept: the flicker metric alone is blind to travel; the kit judges travel (rho_off,
  vel_off), in-place boil (static) and look (region means) separately, and every result here was
  also looked at by eye.

5. WHAT I CONCLUDE FOR THE LEAD
-------------------------------
- The wind is the IGN translation (section 1). Any fix must not combine a lattice-linear per-cell
  offset with a frame-global phase step.
- On every probe I have (stepped and real-time, held and panning, valley_mist), the following meet
  the motion bar at the floor: q2b, t2wb, c8b, q4, S2, S2z, t4w, t4wb. Of
  these, q2b, t2wb and c8b also keep the original look in play (valley 128.0-128.2, farhill
  115.6-115.7 against the original's 128.1/115.9); S2z is about 1 code clearer; q4, S2, t4w about
  2 to 3 codes clearer. Act25 keeps the look but boils 1.3x the floor in play; Ac boils 2x.
- My kit cannot separate q2b, t2wb and c8b. The choice between them is cost, simplicity and
  robustness on other scenes (cuts, fast turns, night, other clips), which my probes do not cover.
- Limits: all on one shot (the dawn valley); vanilla has no real-time recording; the kit's band on an
  mkv reads about +0.04 above bandmetric on PNGs; the fine (grain) column is only comparable ud vs
  ud; region boxes are tuned to the frame-328 pose.

# Track aa: post AA, sharpening and alpha coverage

Research only. Nothing here is committed; the scratch engine lives in the
detached worktree `D:\Arma_CWA_worktrees\gfx-aa` (diff saved as
`prototype.patch`; `prototype_v3.patch` is the earlier state that shot
run_v2/run_v3). The session was paused once and resumed: runs v1-v3 and the
first figures are from before the pause, runs v4-v6 (present-pass cost,
separable Lanczos, FXAA-only pans, the 4x MSAA at 100% setting) from after.

## Verdict (honest)

**Mixed: real but modest image-quality wins, not a "different game".** No
technique in this track changes light, so none of it can deliver the RTX-like
jump on its own. What it does deliver, measured on real captures:

1. **The biggest IQ win is not a shader: the shipped defaults run with no AA
   at all** (`GraphicsConfig`: `msaaSamples = 0`, `renderScale = 1.0`, so
   cutouts get a plain `discard` at alpha 0.753 and no alpha-to-coverage).
   Moving the Remastered default to 4x MSAA (alpha-to-coverage) cuts foliage
   shimmer hard: on the town_night tree the motion-compensated residual of a
   sub-pixel pan goes 6.96 (D, no AA) -> 5.39 (M, 4x MSAA at 1.0x) -> 3.54
   (H, 4x MSAA at 1.5x), and its 99th percentile 82.7 -> 34.4 -> 23.0
   (`shimmer_v4v6.txt`, run_v6, one session). Stills: stair-stepped leaf
   cards become soft edges (`figures/SETTINGS_*_crop.png`,
   `figures/PLAYER_DEFAULT_*.png`).
2. **Lanczos2 downsample + RCAS 0.5 at window resolution** (scratch C++
   present pass, Remastered only) replaces the stock `GL_LINEAR` downsample
   blit of the 1.5x frame. Visibly crisper bark, needles, grass grain, the
   camouflage net weave and vehicle edges, with no colour or brightness shift
   (mean luma of the lower half of the frame, H-lzrcas05 minus H-rem in
   run_v3: -0.04, +0.06, -0.04/255 on forest_edge_golden, town_night,
   sinai_lowsun_fp; -0.31 on overcast_broken, where the remastered cloud
   shadow drifts between shots). **Measured cost**
   (run_v5, minimum over 90 frames, three rounds, very stable): the
   separable version costs **+0.055 ms** over the stock blit at 960x640
   output and **+0.155 ms** at 1920x1080 output (2880x1620 frame). The
   separable version matches the 2D kernel to 0.03-0.06/255 mean.
3. **Unbiased cutout coverage once minified** (shader override `j = 3`). A
   texture study of 55 CWA foliage cutouts shows the authored DXT1 mips are
   1-bit and already coverage-preserving, so the textbook "scale alpha per
   mip at load time" fix has nothing to fix in this data. What thins distant
   crowns is the engine's own threshold `0xc0` (0.753) applied to filtered
   1-bit alpha: on average 21% less coverage than authored, up to 50-82% on
   sparse textures. Moving the threshold to 0.5 once the texture is minified
   restores the authored density (the shadow-depth pass already cuts at 0.5,
   so crowns then match their own shadows). Visible on mid-range pines, small
   on 1-3 km forest. Free.
4. **FXAA** only matters without MSAA. At D it cuts pan shimmer on the
   mid-range pines by 21% (residual 4.81 -> 3.78, p99 51.7 -> 34.5) and on the
   1-3 km forest by 16% (3.00 -> 2.53, p99 28.5 -> 22.3) (run_v4). At 4x MSAA
   it is invisible. Cost +0.02-0.03 ms.
5. **Do not sharpen at 100% render scale.** RCAS after a 1.0x frame
   sharpens the aliasing: at M the residual rises 3.79 -> 4.38 (forest) and
   5.39 -> 6.20 (town tree). Sharpen only after a supersampled downsample.

## What the pipeline actually is (read from code)

- Frame target: always an offscreen FBO, `samples` MSAA at `renderScale` x
  window (`EngineGL33_VertexBuffer.cpp ApplyPendingRenderScale`). Defaults:
  no MSAA, scale 1.0.
- Cutouts (`Backend::IsTransparent`): opaque + alpha test, ref `0xc0`
  (`BuildRenderPassDescriptor.hpp`). With MSAA they use alpha-to-coverage with
  the Golus sharpening `cov = (a - ref) / fwidth(a) + 0.5` already in place
  (`finishFragment`), plus alpha-to-one in Remastered. Without MSAA it is a
  plain `discard` at 0.753.
- Blended alpha draws (camouflage nets, glass, flares) also run the alpha
  test, with ref `1/255`. Any coverage trick must skip them (see the bug below).
- Remastered post composite runs at render resolution before the HUD.
- Window output: `ResolveSSAAToDefault` = MSAA resolve blit + `GL_LINEAR`
  downsample blit, then the gamma pass, then the overlay. It runs after the
  `FrameEnd` GPU mark, so the stock timers never saw its cost (the scratch
  build adds its own timer, see B).
- The shadow-depth cutout test is `a < 0.5` (`EngineGL33_ShadowDepth.cpp`).

## Experiments

### A. FXAA and CAS in the composite (shader override, any build)

`overrides/v3/psPostComposite.glsl`: FXAA 3.11 quality (12 search steps,
subpix 0.5, edge 0.166/0.0833) and AMD CAS on the resolved frame, before AO
and glow, AO share alpha untouched. Variant selected at runtime through the AO
knob (`triSetLighting ao=0.7+0.001*k`, decoded and removed in the shader).

- H setting: FXAA no visible change; CAS 1.0 at render resolution gives a
  small crispness gain that the following 1.5x bilinear blit mostly eats.
- D setting: FXAA visibly smooths silhouettes (`forest_edge_golden__D_crop*`)
  and cuts shimmer (table D below).
- Cost: composite 0.03 ms -> 0.05-0.06 ms at 1440x960 (FXAA or CAS), 0.02 ->
  0.03-0.04 ms at 960x640 (timer column `composite`).

### B. Present pass (scratch C++, `prototype.patch`)

`EngineGL33_VertexBuffer.cpp`: in Remastered, `ResolveSSAAToDefault`
resolves into a sampleable texture and draws `psPresentDown` (Lanczos2 kernel
stretched to the render-scale footprint) and optionally `psPresentSharpen`
(FSR1 RCAS with its noise limiter) at window resolution. Both shaders are
overridable by name. Variant = tens digit of the AO selector: p0 bilinear
(= stock blit), p1 Lanczos2 2D, p2 Lanczos2 2D + RCAS 0.5, p3 bilinear + RCAS
0.5, p4 Lanczos2 + RCAS 1.0, p5 Lanczos2 + RCAS 0.25, **p6 separable Lanczos2
(horizontal pass into a window-width x frame-height RGBA16F target, then
vertical) + RCAS 0.5, p7 separable Lanczos2** (p6/p7 added after the resume).
`UD_AA_PRESENT=0` disables the pass. Classic always keeps the blit.

The resumed build also wraps all of `ResolveSSAAToDefault` (stock blits or
the new pass) in its own timestamp pair, read back late and never waited
for, and appends `| present <EMA> min <minimum>` to the `triGpuTimes`
summary. Other agents' games share the GPU, so the EMA swings 3-10x between
shots (run_v4, `run_v4/present_cost.md`); the minimum over the 90 settle
frames is stable to 0.001 ms across rounds and is the number to trust.

Present pass GPU time, forest_edge_golden, 4x MSAA, 1.5x, minimum over 90
frames, three rounds each (`run_v5/present_cost.md`):

| output | stock blit (Classic) | p0 bilinear shader | p1 Lanczos2 2D | p2 + RCAS 0.5 | p7 separable | **p6 separable + RCAS 0.5** |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 960x640 (frame 1440x960) | 0.032 | 0.026 | 0.123 | 0.136 | 0.075 | **0.087** |
| 1920x1080 (frame 2880x1620) | 0.141 | 0.104 | 0.424 | 0.458 | 0.257 | **0.296** |

So the production candidate (p6) adds 0.055 ms at 960x640 and 0.155 ms at
1080p over the stock path. Separable vs 2D images: mean |diff| 0.03-0.06/255,
p99 1-2/255 (`scripts/sep_vs_2d.py`).

- Lanczos2 alone is already crisper than the blit; + RCAS 0.5 is the best
  balance; RCAS 1.0 starts to look processed in foliage
  (`*__H_sharpen_crop*.png`).
- 1080p 1:1: `figures/BEST_forest_edge_golden_1080p_present_1to1.png`, and x2
  `figures/forest_edge_golden_1080p_present_zoom2.png` (bark and needles).
- At D or M (scale 1.0) Lanczos is an identity and RCAS sharpens the aliasing
  (finding 5); only sharpen after a supersampled frame (or after FXAA).

### C. Cutout coverage (shader override, any build)

`overrides/v3/psRemasterCommon.glsl` + `psNormalRemasterMain.glsl`; variant
selected through the saturation knob (`saturation=1.12+0.001*j`, decoded and
the exact saturation restored). Only opaque cutouts (alpha test on, ref
> 0.5) are touched.

- j1 Golus mip scale `a *= 1 + 0.5 * lod`: dilates more and more with
  distance (effective threshold keeps falling below 0.5).
- j2 (MSAA only): blend the A2C coverage toward the filtered alpha itself once
  minified (the filtered 1-bit alpha is the leaf fraction of the footprint).
- **j3**: scale alpha by `mix(1, ref/0.5, smoothstep(0, 1, lod))`, i.e. the
  test moves to the unbiased 0.5 once the texture is minified (lod from
  `dFdx/dFdy` of `vUV0 * textureSize`, minor axis because of 16x aniso).
- Texture study (`tree_texture_mip_coverage_summary.txt`, decoder
  `scripts/paa_coverage.py`): texel coverage per mip stays flat; bilinear
  pass rate at 0.753 is 21.4% below the texel coverage on average; at 0.5 it
  is +9% (worst-case half-texel phase, so the true bias is smaller).
- In game the sky-through-crown fraction drops slightly (forest_edge_golden H
  0.1871 -> 0.1845, overcast_broken H 0.3162 -> 0.3112; `sky_through_v3.txt`).
  Changed pixels in the lower 55% of the frame (j3 vs current, run_v3):
  forest_edge_golden H 1.79% by more than 2/255 (0.70% by more than 8),
  D 1.03% (0.92%); sinai_lowsun_fp H 0.16% (0.12%); the sky dome clouds
  drift between shots, so full-frame counts are higher. Pines read fuller in
  `forest_edge_golden__D_crop1.png` and `__H_coverage_crop1.png`.
- **Bug found and fixed**: v2 applied the scale to every alpha-tested draw;
  blended draws have ref 1/255, so the scale went to 1/127 and the Sinai
  camouflage net vanished (`figures/BUG_v2_net_vanishes.png`). v3 gates on
  ref > 0.5; the net is then intact (v3 j3 on sinai_lowsun_fp: 0.12% of the
  lower-frame pixels change by more than 8/255, against 6.4% with the v2 bug).

### D. Shimmer (sub-pixel pans)

props re-pinned. `scripts/shimmer_metric.py` measures the global sub-pixel
shift by phase correlation, shifts the next frame back with a band-limited
Fourier shift and reports the residual (what the move cannot explain:
crawling edges, popping leaves). Fixed crops, all runs (`shimmer_v4v6.txt`):

## Methodology problems found (useful for every track)

- **Frame-time LOD adaptation contaminates A/B captures.** `Scene::
  AdjustComplexity` moves `_lodInvWidth` with the measured frame time; with
  other agents' games loading the GPU, distant forest blocks switched between
  a "trunk wall" LOD and a canopy LOD from shot to shot, independent of the
  variant (evidence: `run_eden1/overcast_broken__H-cov4.png`, `__H-cas10.png`
  and `__D-fxaa.png` show the canopy LOD, `__H-rem.png` and `__D-rem.png` the
  trunk wall, all in one session). The scratch build pins `_lodInvWidth =
  _minLodInvWidth` (`UD_PIN_LOD`, default on in the scratch build). With it,
  pan metrics reproduce to 0.01 across sessions (table D). Run 1
  (`run_eden1`) is therefore only used for the composite cost numbers.
- **GPU timers on the shared machine**: the per-frame EMA in `triGpuTimes`
  swings 30-50% (frame) and up to 10x (present) between consecutive shots of
  the same view. The minimum over the settle window is stable; a production
  timer UI would benefit from showing a min alongside the average.
- **Remastered cloud shadows are not reproducible between sessions**, and
  once jumped inside a session (run_v3, between `H-best2` and `H-best3`:
  foreground luma 87.8 -> 63.5). The overcast_broken BEST figure therefore
  uses `H-best2` (j2 + p2).
- town_night: the campfire light flickers and its smoke particles are
  animated between shots despite `triSetSimTime` (`SETTINGS_town_night_crop`
  shows different smoke in each panel); the measured house luma does not move
  (155.0-156.3 over all H variants), and the pan metric crop excludes the
  smoke.
- Two tests with the same view and labels in one run collide in
  `collect.py`'s flat copy; run_v4/run_v5 cost shots are therefore read only
  from their `tri/<test>/` folders (the ambiguous flat copies were removed).

## GPU cost summary

- FXAA or CAS in the composite: +0.02-0.03 ms at 1440x960.
- Coverage (j3): a handful of ALU ops per cutout fragment; not measurable.
- Present pass, separable Lanczos2 + RCAS 0.5: +0.055 ms (960x640 output),
  +0.155 ms (1080p output) over the stock blit; 2D kernel +0.10 / +0.32 ms.
- 4x MSAA + 1.5x vs no AA: not measurable cleanly on the shared GPU (frame
  EMA noise is larger than the effect). Classic, same session (run_v6): D
  3.04 ms, M 3.17 ms, H 6.56 ms on forest_edge_golden; town_night 12.8 / 11.0
  / 15.8 ms. Roughly: M is near free, H costs up to 2x the world pass (2.25x
  pixels). The task brief's Classic numbers at H (2-10 ms) show the headroom.

## What a production version needs

1. **Ship AA in the Remastered default**: 4x MSAA (with the existing
   alpha-to-coverage) at least; 1.5x render scale where the GPU allows it
   (an Options preset). This is the single largest IQ change in this track.
2. Present pass in `EngineGL33`, Remastered only, used when render scale > 1:
   resolve to a texture (the resolve renderbuffer becomes a texture), the
   separable Lanczos2 (two 1D passes, RGBA16F intermediate) then RCAS at
   window resolution, **before the HUD** (today the HUD is drawn into the
   scaled target first, so the prototype also sharpens HUD text). A
   sharpness slider (0..1, default 0.5) in Graphics options; skip RCAS at
   scale 1.0. A GPU timer mark for it (the stock marks end before it).
3. Cutout coverage: one line in the remastered `psNormal` main (and grass if
   it uses cutouts): `a *= mix(1, ref / 0.5, smoothstep(0, 1, lod))` for
   opaque cutouts only (ref > 0.5). Classic untouched.
4. FXAA in the composite when MSAA is off.
5. Do not ship `UD_PIN_LOD` or the AO/saturation selector hack; capture
   tooling should pin LOD (a tri verb) so A/B captures are trustworthy.

## Risks vs the owner taste list

- No colour grading, haze, darkening or tint. Sharpening alone changes mean
  luminance by -0.04 to +0.06/255 (lower half of the frame, three views;
  overcast_broken -0.31 with cloud-shadow drift). The full prototype
  (coverage + sharpening) shifts whole-frame mean luma by -0.22/255
  (forest_edge_golden H), -0.44 (forest_edge_golden D best), -0.29
  (town_night H), -0.04 (sinai_lowsun_fp H) and +0.70 (overcast_broken
  H-best2, dominated by sky and cloud-shadow drift between shots), run_v3:
  leaf pixels replacing sky and ground darken forest views very slightly.
  Not visibly darker, but measurable.
- Sharpening can read as "processed" at RCAS 1.0 and adds a little shimmer
  on far forest; keep 0.5, expose a slider, and never sharpen a 1.0x frame.
- Fuller crowns are a deviation from Classic's (thinner) distant trees, but
  toward the authored texture coverage, not a new look; it does not flatten
  crowns (the crown shading is untouched).
- Classic stays bit-identical (all changes are gated on Remastered;
  verified: Classic shots identical across the present-on and present-off
  runs on the jeep, ground and forest regions, `check_present_off.txt`).

## one_line
The track is honest and every file checks out. The real win is turning on MSAA, which the shipped Remastered default lacks, plus a cheap Lanczos2+RCAS present pass. The result is less shimmer and crisper detail at crop scale, with no change to light, so it does almost nothing for the RTX-on vision.

## critique
This is a clean, well-instrumented and honest report. It has no fabrication; the overclaims are minor (the Lanczos-alone crispness and the 0.001 ms stability). The track just cannot serve the owner vision. What it found, in order of value:

(1) The shipped Remastered default runs with no AA at all. Turning on 4x MSAA (with the alpha-to-coverage path that already exists) visibly calms foliage. In the D/M/H strips the pixel-chunk leaf edges and sparkling holes against the lamp glow soften, and the pan diff maps show far fewer popping leaf pixels: the motion-compensated residual drops from 6.96 to 5.39 to 3.54 on the town tree. That is a real gain a player would feel in motion. It is a default-setting change, not new technology, and it was only shot at 960x640 output, which exaggerates aliasing compared with the 1080p target.

(2) The separable Lanczos2 + RCAS 0.5 present pass makes bark, needles and the net weave crisper at 1:1 and 2x crops, for a measured +0.155 ms at 1080p. At full frame, the BEST Remastered and Remastered + prototype panels are indistinguishable to me. My Laplacian check shows Lanczos alone is not crisper than the blit, only cleaner; RCAS supplies the acutance. It needs a HUD-ordering fix before it can ship.

(3) j3 coverage is a sound analysis: the 0.753 threshold on filtered 1-bit alpha thins crowns by about 21%. Visibly, though, it is near nothing (sky-through 0.1871 to 0.1845, 1.8% of lower-frame pixels). It is free, it does not flatten crowns, and the v2 net bug is properly fixed.

(4) FXAA only matters if MSAA stays off, and recommendation (1) makes it moot.

No taste rules are broken. There is no grading, haze, tint or darkening (luma shifts are fractions of a level), and Classic stays untouched. The khaki ground and other Remastered base-look issues visible in the figures belong to the baseline, not this track.

On cost, the shader passes are trivially cheap and M is near free. The 1.5x preset's cost on a 3060 at 1080p was never measured for Remastered, where contended 1080p H frames ran 18-29 ms. Ship M by default and treat 1.5x as an optional preset.

Toward RTX-on, this is image-quality hygiene, not light: no bounce, occlusion, reflections, volumetrics or emissives. Worth folding in as cheap polish (MSAA default, separable Lanczos+RCAS at scale > 1, j3), but nobody will look at these pictures and say 'different game'.

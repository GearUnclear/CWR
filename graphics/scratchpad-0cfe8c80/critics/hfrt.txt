## one_line
A real, verified find: a 3-6 m canopy heightfield cone-traced toward the sun gives every Abel tree a long, soft golden-hour shadow near and far, which the shipped shadow maps never draw. It is limited to low sun, it only darkens, it is cool in the shade, and as a forward per-pixel pass it costs too much (+13-16 ms in the telephoto view).
## critique
Verification. The folder matches the listing, and every cited image and artefact exists. The game logs for v5 through v10 show:
- the override dir loaded (override_v10\psRemasterCommon.glsl, 36292 B, plus psNormalRemasterMain.glsl);
- a shader-cache miss, a full recompile and 144 programs saved, with no compile or link errors;
- the C++ path running: 84,395 of 98,198 objects gathered in 16-20 ms, then a 4096x4096 canopy with 13 levels (608,268 texels) at k=4, or 2048x2048 with 12 levels (208,177 texels) at k=2;
- run.txt pointing at the rebuilt dist/gfx-hfrt2-rwdi.

PIL diffs of Remastered vs mode 15 (v7): mean absolute difference 1.0-1.3 levels with 3.7-5.4% of pixels changed by more than 8 on hill_0700, hill_1730, slope_tele and ground_a. ground_b is 4.7 MAD and 12.4%, forest_edge 3.1 and 9.6%. sunrise_ridge is 0.26 MAD with nothing over 8, which confirms the claimed null result there. Mode 15 vs 17 (0.06-0.29 MAD) and 15 vs 18 (0.08-0.30) confirm that the canopy AO and the moved alpha test change nothing visible. The claims about the terrain-tracing negative and the 12.5 m blob/diamond canopy match DEBUG_hill_0700. The claim that the shipped shadow maps draw no tree shadows matches the forest_edge debug, where the soldier casts a shadow and the trees do not.

Look. This is the first result in this research that adds something a player would point at. At 17:30 in hill_1730, slope_tele and ground_a, every tree on the sunlit slopes casts a long, consistently oriented shadow, the trees sit on the ground instead of floating, and far valleys gain shaded bands. In ground_b the near trees throw a big shadow band across the foreground rock. Shade/lit lands at about 0.6-0.84 per channel, so nothing is crushed.

Limits:
- It only shows at low sun. There is no change at sunrise_ridge, even though backlit cypresses should throw long shadows at the camera, and none under the 14:30 overcast.
- Near shadows are soft slabs, not tree silhouettes. forest_edge gains only faint smeared streaks.
- It is purely subtractive, and the shade picks up a blue-grey cast from the sky fill.
- The static IGN dither will crawl in motion.
- No town, midday or vehicle view was captured.

RTX vision. It delivers one ingredient: soft sun shadows from every tree out to the horizon, the kind a path tracer gives. It has no bounce, colour bleeding, reflections, volumetrics or emissives, so it reads as a better-shadowed CWA, not a different game. Score 3/10.

Cost. Paired in-run deltas on a shared 4060 Ti at 1440x960:
- slope_tele: +13-16 ms in all five runs;
- ground_b: +8-10 ms;
- hill_1730: +3-7 ms;
- forest_edge: about +2.5 ms.

Scaled to 1080p on a 3060, the telephoto case alone would blow the 16.7 ms budget. The half-resolution visibility buffer the production plan depends on was never measured. So the cost is not OK as built, though plausibly fixable. Also worth checking first: shipped tree casters in the shadow maps may be a cheaper fix for near-field tree shadows, leaving the canopy trace to cover the far field.

Worth building properly (6/10) as the far-field tree-shadow layer at k=2 with sqrt density. Conditions:
- moved to a reduced-resolution pass;
- blue noise plus TAA in place of IGN;
- a warmer shade fill or some bounce so it is not only darker;
- validated on towns, at midday and in motion.
## artefacts_seen
Faint static IGN dither and cone-step banding on soft shadow edges (forest_edge slope, v6 debug). Near-field shadows are blurry wedges with no tree silhouette or trunk gap, because footprints are 0.85-bounding-box ellipses from ground to top. The shade casts blue-grey on the pink rock in ground_b. No NaNs, halos, light leaks or embossing seen in the stills. Temporal swimming and crawl are untested.

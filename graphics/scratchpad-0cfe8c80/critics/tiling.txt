## one_line
The prototype is real and verified: anti-tiling plus a heightfield-tied macro plus relief from albedo gives a clear win on Sinai seen from height and crisper near-ground grit at low sun or on trails. On green islands at eye level the gain is small, with a mild wash on pale fields and darkening on forest_edge. This is texture work, not RTX-style light.
## critique
Verification: clean. All files exist, the overrides loaded and compiled in every run, v0 is the true stock Remastered path at the default saturation, and the BEST panels are the claimed variants. No fabrication found.

What a player would see:
(1) Sinai from height: clearly different. The flat beige plateau becomes mottled, dune-like ground (macroStd 0.049 -> 0.070, fineStd +67%), and the far dunes in sinai_desert_noon gain readable form. This is the one result an owner would notice in a side-by-side. The blotches are soft generic noise, though, and read partly as cloud shadows. The near sand is 11% brighter.
(2) Near ground at low sun and on the forest trail: the relief gives tactile gravel and grit, the grass slope gets crisper, and the trail edge gains a strong ridged, rut-like band. That band is close to embossed but plausible.
(3) Eye level at noon on Abel and Eden (noon_fields, overcast_broken, high_valley_eden, high_fields_noon): small. Some views are slightly worse. The pale field gets paler and loses its authored colour mottling, and hex blending mutes the grass contrast.
The macro is also a lottery per view: forest_edge_golden is -8% darker with either macro.

No NaN, banding, halos or seams were visible in stills. The rejected domain warp really did produce marbled swirls. Motion was never checked: the 60 m noise octave samples a mipless texture and the bump uses per-quad derivatives, so shimmer and swimming are open risks.

Against the RTX-on vision this track barely moves: it changes albedo and adds a bump normal. There is no bounce light, occlusion, reflection or volumetric effect, so rtx_vision_score is 2.

Cost is acceptable: about +0.5 to +2.5 ms paired-median at 1440x960 with 4x MSAA, n=4, with ranges that straddle zero. There is headroom, and removing the 4 probe taps helps.

Worth building as a cheap supporting layer, not as a headline feature: anti-tiling plus the relief everywhere, and the patch macro only per island (desert maps), with crisper terrain-shaped boundaries and an amplitude that cannot pull a whole view darker or paler.

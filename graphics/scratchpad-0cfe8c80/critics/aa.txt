## one_line
The track is honest and every file checks out. The real win is turning on MSAA, which the shipped Remastered default lacks, plus a cheap Lanczos2+RCAS present pass. The result is less shimmer and crisper detail at crop scale, with no change to light, so it does almost nothing for the RTX-on vision.

## critique
This is a clean, well-instrumented and honest report. It has no fabrication; the overclaims are minor (the Lanczos-alone crispness and the 0.001 ms stability). The track just cannot serve the owner vision. What it found, in order of value:

(1) The shipped Remastered default runs with no AA at all. Turning on 4x MSAA (with the alpha-to-coverage path that already exists) visibly calms foliage. In the D/M/H strips the pixel-chunk leaf edges and sparkling holes against the lamp glow soften, and the pan diff maps show far fewer popping leaf pixels: the motion-compensated residual drops from 6.96 to 5.39 to 3.54 on the town tree. That is a real gain a player would feel in motion. It is a default-setting change, not new technology, and it was only shot at 960x640 output, which exaggerates aliasing compared with the 1080p target.

(2) The separable Lanczos2 + RCAS 0.5 present pass makes bark, needles and the net weave crisper at 1:1 and 2x crops, for a measured +0.155 ms at 1080p. At full frame, the BEST Remastered and Remastered + prototype panels are indistinguishable to me. My Laplacian check shows Lanczos alone is not crisper than the blit, only cleaner; RCAS supplies the acutance. It needs a HUD-ordering fix before it can ship.

(3) j3 coverage is a sound analysis: the 0.753 threshold on filtered 1-bit alpha thins crowns by about 21%. Visibly, though, it is near nothing (sky-through 0.1871 to 0.1845, 1.8% of lower-frame pixels). It is free, it does not flatten crowns, and the v2 net bug is properly fixed.

(4) FXAA only matters if MSAA stays off, and recommendation (1) makes it moot.

No taste rules are broken. There is no grading, haze, tint or darkening (luma shifts are fractions of a level), and Classic stays untouched. The khaki ground and other Remastered base-look issues visible in the figures belong to the baseline, not this track.

On cost, the shader passes are trivially cheap and M is near free. The 1.5x preset's cost on a 3060 at 1080p was never measured for Remastered, where contended 1080p H frames ran 18-29 ms. Ship M by default and treat 1.5x as an optional preset.

Toward RTX-on, this is image-quality hygiene, not light: no bounce, occlusion, reflections, volumetrics or emissives. Worth folding in as cheap polish (MSAA default, separable Lanczos+RCAS at scale > 1, j3), but nobody will look at these pictures and say 'different game'.

## one_line
The G-buffer plumbing is real and clean, and it found a genuine stock bug: script-spawned shapes skip the Remastered shader. The GI consumer built on it is invisible at a glance (rtx about 1.5/10), and the late-VBO hack breaks night.
## critique
Verification. Every cited path exists and nothing was fabricated. The logs for runs 06, 07, 10, 11 and 12 show 'shader override loaded ... override_v2\psPostGi.glsl (5420 bytes)' and 'gbuffer prototype programs gi=185 temporal=187 blur=189 debug=191', with no shader compile errors. The only 'ERROR' grep hits in the Sinai logs are the word 'loboterror'. The prototype exe was built after the last source edit, and run09 really used the shared stock exe. The after images do differ from the before images. In my own PIL diffs within one run, town_day x3 bounce-only changes 6.5% of pixels by more than 4 levels (mean +0.68, p99 +6.7); lowsun_fp 7.3% (p99 +9.7); sinai 7.2% (p99 +11.0). A single frame against accumulated frames differs on only 0.18%, so within-run noise is low. Across runs, though, the same mode differs on 2.5% of pixels, which swamps the x1 and x2 variants.

Look. At the viewing size used for the side-by-side judgement, the GI panels cannot be told apart from Remastered + late VBO (run06_v2/str_town_day.png: four panels that look identical). Only an 8x-amplified diff shows the lift on the soldier, the Jeep, the M113's shaded front, the tent and the walls. There is no visible colour bleeding, no contact darkening and no sky-occlusion character. Nothing a player would call RTX-like. Minor artefacts show up in the amplified diff: thin dark lines along the tent ropes (thin geometry at half resolution), some sky-visibility halos around tree crowns in the debug targets, and a lift on the Sinai Jeep's windows that may be bounce gathered on alpha-blended glass. None of these are visible at 1:1. There are no NaNs, and no noise in stills.

The one real visible change is the late-VBO fix. In stock Remastered, spawned vehicles never reach the lit shader, so a Jeep standing in an M113's shadow is drawn sunlit (BEST_lowsun_shadow.png makes this unambiguous). With the fix they match mission-placed vehicles. This matters a lot for Guerrilla Mode, where traffic, garrisons and spawns are all script-created. It is a correctness fix, not RTX-on.

Diagnosis and honesty are good. The wedge fix (rebuilding the geometric normal from depth) is verified in wedge_forest_edge_golden.png, and the explanation of why one bounce is weak is quantified: dark albedo of about 0.16, and the rig's analytic ground bounce already supplies the indirect term. The production list is candid: radiance stored before night-eye, roughness almost empty, unblended writes from blended draws, holes left by birch leaf cards.

Missed bold move. The GI was only added on top of the rig's analytic sky and ground bounce, at x1 to x3. The test that matters for a path-traced look is to switch the analytic ambient and bounce off (or scale them down hard) and let the traced visibility and bounce carry the indirect light, so occlusion and bleeding gain real dynamic range. That was never tried. Neither was the consumer that reads most like RTX in CWA scenes: SSR on water and glass.

Cost. GI is +1.5 to +2.5 ms on a 4060 Ti at 1440x960 with 4x MSAA. The G-buffer adds an estimated 1 to 2 ms more, and that part could not be resolved on the shared GPU. On a 3060 at 1080p, expect very roughly 1.5 to 2x that. It fits the budget, but the GI buys nothing visible for it.

Verdict. Build the late-VBO fix now. It is needed regardless, but rebuild VBOs after the shape is fully initialised and prove night is fixed first. Keep the G-buffer as infrastructure for SSR, contact shadows and a stronger GI, with packed multisample textures and radiance stored after night-eye. Do not ship this GI consumer as a look.
## artefacts_seen
At 1:1 nothing is visible in the day stills. The 8x-amplified diffs show: thin dark lines along the tent guy ropes (lowsun_fp), sky-visibility halos and a faint residual lighter smear where v1's wedge was (lower left of forest_edge_golden), and a strong lift on the Sinai Jeep's window texels (a possible light leak through blended glass). Night with late VBO is catastrophically broken: the whole town is lit like day. No NaNs, banding or embossing. Stills cannot show temporal swimming, and the accumulation resets on any camera move, so motion quality equals the single-frame quality (claimed clean after the blur; single frame vs accumulated differ on only 0.18% of pixels).

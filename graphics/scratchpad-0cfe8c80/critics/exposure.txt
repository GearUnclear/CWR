## one_line
Honest, real tone-mapping work: local exposure makes the crushed Jeep and tent flanks readable at low sun, but it washes the sunrise, only darkens the night, and moves almost nothing toward an RTX-on look.
## critique
Provenance is solid. Files, logs, override loads and knob settings all check out, BEST panels are pixel-identical to the r5 shots, and the negative results (ungated sea wash, HDR smoke blowout, AgX grey sun, uncalibrated key) are real and correctly labelled. The explorer did real engineering: an exact inverse of the shipped Neutral shoulder, a bilateral grid built by gathering under GL 3.3, and a working adaptation loop with a meter readout.

Look: this is a tone-mapping track, so it cannot produce bounce light, sky occlusion, reflections, volumetrics or emissive lighting. Of the six views, one is a clear win. In lowsun_fp the Jeep flank, tent folds, M113 and soldier get back the readability Remastered crushed, with no halos at the tent or hull edges. That gain is visible side by side but modest, roughly a return to Classic's legibility while keeping Remastered's soft shadows. The coastline, tank interior and forest are near neutral, although the forest is lifted more than claimed. Sunrise_ridge is a regression on taste: a flat grey-green foreground, the warm sun streak gone, and a sky that also brightens despite the 'sky unchanged' claim. Town_night is just 0.75 EV darker plus a more orange fire core.

Score reasoning: rtx_vision is 1.5 because readable shade and an orange fire core do not make a player say this is a different game. The idea most worth building is not this post pass. It is production item 1: an illumination/light-buffer MRT that separates shade from dark albedo. That would let local exposure lift only shade without the sea and sunrise wash, and other tracks could use it too. Two smaller pieces are worth taking as by-products: the zero-cost hue-keeping curve in the world shaders, for fire and sun colour, and the eye-adaptation loop with a meter, which belongs with the night track. As shipped here (F1A), it would not survive the owner's anti-wash and keep-evenings-warm rules on the sunrise view.

Cost: realistically about 1 ms at 1440x960 with 4x MSAA, well within an RTX 3060 at 1080p/60. Worth building properly only as a narrow readability and infrastructure item, not as a headline visual change.

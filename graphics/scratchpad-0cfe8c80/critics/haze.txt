## critique
Provenance is clean. Every cited image, shader folder, patch and note exists. The logs prove that the override files loaded and the patched backdrop build ran. The BEST sheets are byte-exact crops of the raw captures. AFTER frames differ from BEFORE wherever the explorer says they should, and noon is an honest null.

The look. The 05:15 valley frame is the one picture a player would remember. Pink inversion mist fills the lake basin, the small islands poke through, and the hard sea edge of current Remastered is gone. It reads like a real dawn photograph and is still recognisably Malden. The 06:00 to 06:30 frames are believable fog banks on the lake. They come with a pale, cool lift over the whole mid field (bottom third +7/+8/+14 RGB, contrast down about 9%), which is the wash and blue direction the owner rejected. On sunrise_ridge the massif dissolving into the dawn sky is atmospheric but borders on washed out. Overcast v31 adds mild depth layering and a cooler cast on far hills (B-R 11.8 to 20.2). Dusk is essentially unchanged. Noon is unchanged. So the clear gain is confined to sunrise and early morning valley views. At other times of day the effect is a refinement you have to look for.

The 48-fetch sun march, the 'volumetric' part, changes about 2% of pixels at 5 degrees sun and nothing at 06:30. It is not yet the light-shafts-through-haze look the vision asks for. Against the RTX bar this is one pillar (aerial perspective plus ground fog) done as single-scatter fog. There are no visible god rays through trees, no physically based sky, and nothing for night or combat. It also hides water shine in the mornings. RTX vision 3/10.

Artefacts. The crosshatch is visible at zoom and will be screen-locked in motion. The horizon band still meets the mist in a hard line. The seam is fixed in v43.

Cost. It is honestly flagged as unmeasured, but the '+9 ms' headline is cherry-picked. Round 7 minima (33.6 against 5.3 ms, 44.2 against 18.1 ms) suggest the forward per-fragment form could cost far more. With foliage overdraw at 1440x960 4x MSAA it is not acceptable as built. The proposed deferred, half-resolution, temporally accumulated pass with a precomputed mist-floor texture is sound and would plausibly land around 1 to 2 ms at 1080p on a 3060. That makes the idea fundable, but the cost is unproven.

A risk the explorer missed: the mist is purely visual. AI spotting runs on engine fog values, so a player blinded by a valley mist bank while the AI sees through it is a gameplay fairness problem. A production version must feed the mist into AI visibility, or keep it thin over gameplay ground. The plan's 'keep Classic bit-identical' step is stale per owner decision 1. Also, the 'eastern sun only' gate is a hack that needs weather and humidity drivers, as the explorer notes.

Verdict: worth building properly as a deferred atmosphere pass (5.5/10), with grey extinction (v38) as the default, a thinner mist (v36), and an AI-visibility tie-in. It is not by itself the 'different game' moment outside sunrise.

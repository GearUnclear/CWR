docs: remastered lighting research findings and the graphics-remaster plan

Remaster/research/ documents the 22-track research run toward an "RTX on" look
from the existing models and textures: the owner's goal and decisions, how each
prototype was built and critic-checked on real Remaster captures, one page per
track (technique, what real data showed, cost, verdict, overclaims, what
production needs), the water study, every bug found with its fix commit, the GPU
budget and the road to RTX-on. INTEGRATION-PLAN.md is the plan for compiling the
ideas that worked into this branch, and Remaster/ITERATIONS.md is the change log.
Pictures are renders of game data and stay on the development machine.

graphics: fires, flares and shell bursts light the men and cast shadows

What the player sees: at night a camp fire, a burning wreck, a shell
burst, a flare, a muzzle flash, a tracer and a missile motor now light
the ground and the men around them as real light sources, warm and
bright near the source and falling off with distance, and the four
strongest throw long radial shadows from the men and vehicles. A shell
burst whitens the frame for as long as Classic's does: at eye height the
first flash frame reads 57.9 against Classic's 56.0 and the frame 0.13 s
later 57.6 against 58.5; from 5 m up both frames read brighter than
Classic (115.6 and 114.1 against 71.4 and 77.0). The flash is white-hot
and cools to a fire's warmth, so the ground it lights reads warm white
and sand, not khaki (red over blue of the lit ground 1.22 to 1.39). Smoke
and dust round a fire or a burst glow from inside, and the air round a
flash takes a faint glow. By day the same lights keep a fifth of their
strength (bursts) or 12% (fires), so a noon shell still marks the crater;
the shadows run only at night. A paused game holds every light where it
was.

How it works: Graphics/Rendering/Lighting/CombatLights (pure) keeps the
combat lights of the world in one set, ticked by the simulation step in
World::Simulate (so a paused frame does not age them). Bursts (muzzle,
cannon, explosion) are spawned and forgotten; continuous lights (wreck
embers, camp fires, tracers, flares, missile motors) are owned by their
object through a CombatLightHandle. The sources are the existing
gameplay hooks: WeaponLightSource and WeaponFireSource (the firing
vehicles and soldiers now pass the round's hit, which sets the muzzle
strength), SmokeSourceVehicle (the fuel burst and the wreck's embers),
Explosion (a burst sized by the explosion), ShotBullet (tracers),
Missile (the motor while it thrusts), IlluminatingShell (flares) and
Fireplace. Each kind has its envelope (EvaluateCombatLight: a 30 ms
muzzle; an explosion whose white-hot flash holds 0.15 s
(kExplosionFlashHold), then falls in 50 ms while a flicker fades in and
the colour cools to the fire's; a fire that breathes on two slow sines
and smooth noise, never a new brightness every frame) and a ball
falloff E = I * window / (1 + d^2 / r0^2) that ends at the light's
range (at most 120 m, a flare 400 m). Burst colours stay at or under the
fire's red over blue.

The lights enter A6's tiled list as up to 12 HDR entries at the head of
the frame's list, strongest at the camera first (MergeCombatFrameLights,
packed as kind 2 with the window range), and the forward shader shades
them in linear light outside the classic clamp (combatLights), with a
flash highlight on glossy materials but not on terrain. While they are
on, the classic light of the same source lights nothing: the tiled list
and the smoke skip the Combat and Fire kinds at selection (the classic
light stays for its glow sprite and for the switch off). The shadows: up
to four lights are picked for the four channels of a half-resolution
RGBA8 mask, with hysteresis (a light keeps its channel unless another is
1.5 times stronger for 0.3 s; tracers and motors cast none). At the
start of each world pass EngineGL33_PostCombat traces from each mask
texel toward each shadowed light through the previous frame's half-res
linear depth (24 steps over at most 25 m), then blurs the mask across
and down with a depth-aware 7-tap filter. The forward shader maps its
fragment into last frame's screen through three rows in the FrameLights
block (CombatMaskRows) and takes one bilinear tap for all four lights.
The flash exposure: explosions and cannon at the camera open the
exposure in proportion to their irradiance there, at night only, at
most 4x, held with a 0.12 s decay. The composite adds the in-scatter of
up to 8 lights (an analytic integral along each pixel's ray through the
light's smoke ball). Debug view 7 ("Combat shadow mask") shows the mask.

The switch and its gates: combatLights (Combat light, Night and combat)
and combatShadows (Combat light shadows), both landed (11/26), with the
sub-knobs combatFlash, combatFire, combatSmoke and combatScatter (0 to
3, default 1). combatLights is on with the light list and a combat light
within 60 m of the camera (its range included); without the light list
it is off and the classic lights light the frame as before.
combatShadows also needs a night above 0.3, the post effects (the mask
reads the post pass's linear depth, which LinearDepth now runs for) and
the world in view. The simulation does not depend on either switch, so
off and on shoot the same paused frame. GPU sections: the mask pass
shows as "combatmask", the shading in "world".

Kept, rewritten and dropped from the research prototype: kept the
envelopes' shapes, the ball falloff and its window, the day scaling, the
gameplay hooks, the analytic in-scatter, smoke lit from inside, the
glossy flash and the night-eye lift. Rewritten: the lights are A6's
Combat and Fire kinds at the head of the tiled list instead of an HDR
flag through the 8 per-draw slots; the per-fragment trace through last
frame's depth became the half-res mask pass with hysteresis and blur;
strengths come from the round's hit and the explosion size, not a
table; the explosion's flash holds 0.15 s instead of falling from its
birth, and the fireball, muzzle and cannon colours are less saturated
(the research's fireball cooled to (1.0, 0.40, 0.11)). Dropped:
LitConstants slots 59 to 64 (the rows ride in the FrameLights block;
slot 72 is not used either), unit 6 (the mask is on unit 14), the 2x2
ordered jitter, the combatWreck knob, and the prototype's own light
flags in Lights.hpp. Corrections to plan section 5 B4 are recorded in
the plan.

The critic list: the seam down the screen centre was the terrain, not
the mask: the capture camera stood on the segment line x = 7600, one
segment's material is specular and the other not, and the low flash's
glint drew the line. Terrain takes no flash glint now; the centre
columns of the final shots show no step above the texture's own, and
the mask mapping is tested at the centre and edges for even and odd
frame sizes. Shadow flipping between two fires: the hysteresis (no flip
over 600 frames of two flickering fires in the unit test). The flash
frame: a probe of the capture showed a shell's burst is first drawn at
age 1/30 s and the second flash shot at 0.13 s, where Classic still
whitens the frame; the research's envelope had fallen there and read 24
levels under Classic. Fire-lit ground red over blue 1.43 to 1.44 (the
plan's 1.4).

Measured cost (RTX 4060 Ti, 1920x1080, scale 1.0, 4x MSAA; one reading,
three interleaved rounds, a process list during the run showed only
this lane's game, GPU clock not logged): the camp fire and burning wreck view:

shell burst paused at eye height: mask 0.20 ms, frame -0.01 to +0.23 ms
(minimum frame +0.16 to +0.33). The plan allows 1 to 3 ms in fights. By
day the mask gate is off ("day (night below 0.3)"); with no combat light
near, both gates are off and sunrise_ridge and road_night draw the same
frame.

Known risks: the burst's tail is longer and warmer than Classic's
(0.27 s after a burst at eye height 33.1 against 22.4, from above 59.9
against 31.5); a noon shell adds 8.5 levels to the frame mean on its
first frame, with a pale orange ring round the crater and a faint glow
ball; a flare low over the ground blows the ground out white beside it
(75.5 against Classic's 52.3); the capture labels kNN are not steps
after the shot (k05 is 0.03 s, k06 0.13 s and k08 0.27 s after the
burst: pause and unpause each take a step). The shadows lag moving men
by a frame (the mask uses last frame's depth), and the mask keeps no
history of its own: TAA averages its noise, and the far ground at
grazing angles shows a hatch in debug view 7. Combat lights take up to
12 of a tile's 16 entries, so a busy tile can cut a classic light. A
muzzle flash shows in one captured frame of four (the 30 ms burst falls
in one 1/30 s step), as intended. The explosion cloudlets are yellow
with the switch off too (not this change). Not tested: tank cannon,
interiors, MP, rain, smoke_eden and smoke_sinai_lobo, a moving capture,
an RTX 3060. CombatLights.{hpp,cpp}, EngineGL33_PostCombat.cpp,
EngineGL33_PostCombatShaders.cpp, test_combat_lights.cpp,
combat_ab.test.{sqf,toml} and gen_combat_ab.py are new files to add
with the commit.

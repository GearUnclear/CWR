graphics: the shadow-map unit always holds a complete depth array

NVIDIA KHR_debug reported GL undefined behaviour from the shadow sampler
on texture unit 2: 131204 (texture object 0 has no base level) at every
start-up, and 131222 (comparison sampler used with texture 0) after a
shadow-map resolution or cascade change in Remastered.

Root cause: every lit program samples unit 2 (GL_TEXTURE_2D_ARRAY), and
both the classic sampler2DArray and the remastered sampler2DArrayShadow
uniforms are live, so the driver validates unit 2 at every draw.
(1) InitLightingResources created the 1x1 depth stand-in on upload
unit 7 and never put it on unit 2, so the first classic draws (and the
whole session in Classic with shadow maps off) ran against texture 0.
(2) EnsureArrayTarget deleted the cascade array bound on unit 2 and
bound its replacement on whatever unit was active, leaving the
comparison sampler on texture 0 until the map was rebound after the
depth pass.

Fix: InitLightingResources binds the depth stand-in on unit 2, and
EnsureArrayTarget selects unit 2 before it creates the new array. Both
textures are complete depth formats. No per-draw work, no shader change.

Tests: three [ShadowSampler] audits in test_gl_state_cache_audit.cpp
(two fail without the fix). In game: 131204 and 131222 no longer fire,
and the images are unchanged. The remaining clang-format violation at
EngineGL33_ShadowDepth.cpp:470 is pre-existing.

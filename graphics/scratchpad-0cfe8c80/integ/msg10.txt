graphics: three-entry config colours read as {r,g,b} with alpha 1

Street lamps lost their authored warm colour and lit at ten times their
authored ambient, which washed the open country up to 500 m around every
town a flat grey at night, moon or no moon.

Root cause (stock, unchanged since the public source import): GetColor
in ParamFileExt.cpp returned HWhite for any array that is not exactly 4
entries long. CfgNonAIVehicles >> StreetLamp gives colorDiffuse[] =
{0.9,0.8,0.6} and colorAmbient[] = {0.1,0.1,0.1}, both with 3 entries,
so every lamp uploaded diffuse (1,1,1) and ambient (1,1,1). The ambient
term has no direction and reaches 500 m, so six lamps 220-300 m away
greyed the country (51 -> 15 without the lamp ambient).

Fix: GetColor accepts {r,g,b} with alpha 1; every other size still reads
as white. GetPackedColor (UI) is unchanged. These are the only 3-entry
values among the GetColor keys in the base config; in @LoBo two lamp
panels (AGS_Harbor_StreetLampPanel, f3wx_StreetLampPanel) are 3-entry
and their ambient drops from 1.0 to the authored 0.7.

Committed on its own so it can be reverted alone: this is a data fix,
so it changes Classic and night vision near towns as well, not only
Remastered. Classic open country near a town goes from 57 to 20 (the
wash is gone), and night-vision ground there from green 106 to 79 in
both modes. Town views and town night vision are unchanged.

Tests: 'GetColor reads three-entry colours with alpha 1' (5 of 10
assertions fail without the fix).

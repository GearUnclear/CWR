graphics: leaves without a mesh normal no longer get NaN lighting

Some tree crowns lit with NaN normals in the remastered shader and fell
back to driver-defined lighting (ground-only sky), and their sun shadow
lookup always returned lit.

Root cause: s_vsTransformRemasterGLSL normalised the world normal with
normalize(), which is undefined for the zero mesh normals some leaf
cards carry and gives NaN on NVIDIA. The NaN interpolated across the
triangle, and shadeRemastered guarded a zero length but not NaN, so the
crown blend, sun, sky, moon and shadow lookup all went NaN. Measured in
game with diagnostic overrides: no non-finite crown constants in any
view, only zero mesh normals.

Fix:
- VSTransform uses n *= inversesqrt(max(dot(n,n), 1e-12)), so a zero
  normal stays zero.
- shadeRemastered computes the crown normal once; a foliage fragment
  without its own normal takes it for sun, sky, moon, local lights and
  the shadow-lookup offset. Healthy leaves compute what they did before.
- Hardening: CrownEllipsoid replaces an axis with a non-finite bound or
  extent by a 5 cm crown at the origin.

Tests: 'Remaster crown ellipsoid stays finite for degenerate boxes' and
'Remaster lighting normals survive leaves without a mesh normal' (21 of
96 assertions fail without the fix). In game: 0 NaN crown pixels in all
four debug views, whole-frame means move by at most 0.15 levels.

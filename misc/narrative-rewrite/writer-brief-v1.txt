Write ONE fictional pre-campaign character biography for a Cold War-era resistance game. This is prose a player reads in a dossier during play. Make it understandable on one reading and about a particular person.

Use the assigned basic story beat in cards.json. Preserve its cause and choice, but write fresh prose rather than paraphrasing existing source. Use a restrained, plain narrator. Concrete work, relationships and actions carry the emotion. Do not announce a moral, manufacture a quotable final line, hide actions behind vague abstractions, or add a symbolic object just to sound profound. End when the useful information is complete. Some people are ordinary, selfish, sociable, worried or tired; don't automatically turn them into heroes or villains. Avoid facts or abilities that require an unimplemented game mechanic.

Aim for 30-36 whitespace-separated words before substituting tokens. Most sentences should be easy to say aloud. Two to four sentences are acceptable; vary their lengths naturally. Do not pad to fill the maximum. Use {Name} EXACTLY ONCE and {Place} EXACTLY ONCE. Singular they/them/their is allowed and avoids repetitive full names. No he/she/his/her/him/hers. Use ASCII punctuation and spell numbers out. Do not use faction-name tokens in biographies; describe the job or posting directly. No XP, rank, promotion, kill/killed, captured zone or deeds from the player's campaign. The biography takes place BEFORE the current campaign, so previous joining or training is allowed.

Compatibility matters: every ancestral-grievance history has loss of access to livelihoods near {Place}; every broken-agreement history has an agreement followed by restrictions near {Place}; every conflict history has fighting with local loss at {Place}. The assigned biography must fit ALL SIX variants of its category. Do not require a particular well, ridge, burning, numbered day, relief column, signed clause, year, or named faction. Supporting personal events may be invented if they do not contradict this shared history.

Read your draft aloud. Can the player immediately say who did what and why? Does its ending report a fact instead of interpreting the entire life? If wording sounds conspicuously clever, prefer the literal event. Keep clarity over decorative polish.

Save ONLY JSON with keys id, bank, variant, text, word_count, self_review to the assigned draft file. word_count counts whitespace tokens with placeholders as one each. self_review briefly explains the causal link and any tradeoff. Do not change source or other drafts. Return the text and draft path.

Write one short character story for a game dossier. Read your assigned beat in cards.json and your own direction in directions.json. Follow that direction. You can change smaller details of the beat to make the direction work.

Do not read other drafts or imitate a shared example. Tell this particular story in everyday words. We should understand it the first time. Do not finish with a saying about life, justice or history. Do not squeeze a job, trauma, motive, habit and final decision into every entry.

Use 20-36 words. The direction tells you how many sentences and where to begin. Use {Name} once and {Place} once. They/them/their is fine if we can tell who you mean. Avoid he/she/his/her/him/hers because the game picks appearance separately. Spell numbers out and use ASCII punctuation. A short spoken line is fine when your direction allows it.

This happened before the player's campaign. Do not describe current XP, rank, promotions, kills or captured zones. Do not use faction-name placeholders or invent game abilities.

Each story belongs to one part of the shared history: families lost places they needed for work; an agreement was followed by restrictions; or fighting caused local losses. Fit the broad event. Do not require a particular battle day, burning, ridge, well or relief column.

Read it aloud once. Replace anything that needs an explanation. Save JSON with id, bank, variant, text, word_count and self_review. self_review should be a plain sentence saying what happens. Do not use editing jargon. Return only the story and its path. Do not edit source or anyone else's draft.
